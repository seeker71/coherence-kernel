# Sema Sessions

This room lives inside the project folder. If the project is not on your
computer yet, Step 1 of [`YOUR-OWN-COMPUTER.md`](YOUR-OWN-COMPUTER.md) walks you
through copying it down; to see the folder, open Finder, choose Go → Home from
the menu bar, and open `coherence-kernel`.

Inside the folder is **Sema Sessions.app**. Open it and the Sessions room opens
in the browser. No Terminal window, installer, setup screen, mode, account, or
configuration is part of the path.

On the first opening, Sessions receives and verifies its local listening voice
inside the room. The page stays present while that happens, then becomes the
recording room by itself. Later gatherings no longer need that first arrival.

One honest thing before you plan a gathering around this room: a real
gathering of several people has not yet crossed it — the end of this page says
exactly what has and hasn't been witnessed. If yours is the first, that is
welcome, and worth knowing before you invite the neighbors.

## A gathering

Name the gathering and let everyone’s recording agreement arrive. Choose
**Begin recording**, or choose **Bring recording** for sound that already
exists. When the sound ends, the session opens with its recording and the words
that were heard.

The listening and transcription happen on this Mac. No recording or transcript
is sent to an account or a remote transcription service.

## Returning to the words

The **Sessions** page holds the living sessions. Open any session to:

- change its name;
- listen to the recording;
- correct, remove, or reshape any part of the transcript;
- add a speaker name when that person has seen and agreed to it;
- save the new shape of the words.

Each save also tends a workshop-manual draft and a book-source ledger. Both
retain their source link to the session. A draft remains a draft; it does not
create publication agreement.

## Archive, restore, and delete

**Archive session** moves a session out of the everyday Sessions page without
removing any of it. The **Archive** is always beside Sessions in the top of the
room.

An archived session can be opened, heard, and read. **Restore session** returns
it to Sessions. Permanent deletion lives only inside an archived session and
requires the nearby confirmation box. It removes that session’s recording,
transcript, names, and drafts from this Mac.

## Where the gatherings rest

The private session library lives in the hidden `.sema-sessions` folder beside
the app. Git ignores it, so recordings and transcripts do not enter the public
body when the code is shared.

After the listening voice has arrived once, recording, transcription, reading,
editing, archiving, restoring, and deleting all stay on this Mac. Unknown
speaker is kept as a visible whole state; Sessions does not turn a voice guess
into someone’s identity.
