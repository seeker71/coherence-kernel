## Purpose

Language models inherit contextual word senses that may carry control,
compulsion, contraction, care, protection, or vitality. The same surface can
name very different functions. A dictionary that simply replaces “fear words”
with warmer words would erase information and make coercion harder to see.

This organ therefore keeps source senses speakable. It stores each candidate
sense as a form-neutral role recipe and may later offer a separate
trust/sovereignty/vitality-aligned composition when occurrence evidence and the
affected participant's present choice support it.

```text
surface occurrence + locale + context + corpus revision + provenance
  -> zero or more candidate source-sense recipes with ambiguity visible
  -> participant-bound semantic selection receipt
  -> source meaning and candidate projection remain visible

selected source sense
  -> optional transformation bound to the same occurrence and participant
  -> computed preserved / removed / added delta
  -> labeled source + alternative + delta render offer
```

The present seed does not perform contextual understanding or generative
composition. Context cells route authored candidates. Their explicitly labeled
surface projections remain inspectable; value-aligned alternative composition
is closed because no mapping yet carries semantic claim state `3`.

## Identity and definitions

Meaning identity is a content-addressed recipe. Natural-language strings occur
only in surface, corpus, and provenance carriers. Recipes contain numeric cells
connected by numeric structural roles. Edge order is canonicalized before
interning. Duplicate roles are rejected, rather than relying on author
discipline for canonical identity.

The five agreed axioms provide the common ground:

1. `nothing / 0 / 1` are first-class states;
2. everything is a composable cell;
3. present composition determines identity and referenced prior compositions
   remain available;
4. a cell offers its interface, observation makes a meeting real, and the cell
   chooses what it trusts;
5. invocation and communication are offers acknowledged as
   `nothing / 0 / 1 / node`.

Harm, threat, likelihood, force, information, voluntariness,
confidentiality, and many other distinctions remain explicit numeric grounding
gaps. The graph distinguishes their positions but does not yet ground their
meaning observationally or recursively. A gap's English review name is not
semantic evidence.

## Purified definition of safety

Safety is not one global sense and not stillness. The graph keeps three
qualified candidates:

- physical safety: harm awareness, threat detection, prevention, integrity,
  observation, and recovery;
- safe self-update: sovereign offer, affirmative acknowledgement, observation,
  continuity, trust, change, and recovery;
- whole-organism safety: the two compositions together, with vitality as an
  outcome rather than a substitute for harm awareness.

The current human projection of the qualified whole-organism candidate is:

> Safety is the lived condition in which an organism can remain whole, detect
> and prevent harm, preserve integrity, choose what crosses its boundary,
> change through observed and acknowledged trust, recover, and continue its
> vitality.

The whole composition is no longer stored under an unqualified `general`
context. An unspecified composer cannot silently choose it as the default.

These are structured authored candidates, not culturally complete definitions.

coverage, residue, and claim state:

- `1`: authored candidate;
- `2`: a surface relation is attested in the named source;
- `3`: participant-bound semantic selection attested.

Byte identity is not a meaning criterion. Case, inflection, script,
abbreviation, spelling, and other surface variation belong on explicit
projection edges. Source hashes and coordinates can support lineage without
becoming gates for trust or semantic identity.

## The enforcement witness

The only optional alternative is from voluntarily acknowledged boundary
follow-through to boundary tending. It is not reachable from coercive legal or
mechanical software enforcement.

The proposal validator accepts only when all of the following compose as the
same witnessed relation:

- an admitted mapping and its occurrence/provenance relation;
- the selected source meaning and context;
- a typed affected participant;
- first-person perspective;
- acknowledgement `1` that is current and revocable;
- semantic claim state `3`;
- a delta recomputed from the source and alternative graphs.

An arbitrary selector no longer passes. Candidate surface projections can be
inspected as candidates; they do not open the alternative-composition door.
When that door eventually opens, rendering returns a labeled carrier containing
the source projection, offered alternative surface, and computed
`[preserved, removed, added]` delta. It never returns a bare warm phrase that
could impersonate the source.

The current coercive candidate projection keeps the sharp roles visible:

> authority imposes an obligation through force and consequence across a power
> asymmetry

## Scaling to the whole dictionary

The body already holds expansion organs:

- `cognition/concept-text-senses-10000.fk`: 34,244 explicit WordNet senses,
  including 5,618 ambiguous anchors;
- `cognition/concept-nl-semantic-13.fk`: 10,000 concept anchors across 13
  language projections with source attribution;
- `cognition/language-neutral-symbol-identity.fk`: label-independent NL and PL
  identity;
- `form/form-stdlib/core-lexicon.fk`: a 64-word definition-closed core;
- the reviewed form-neutral meaning-space artifacts in this directory.

Expansion is occurrence- and sense-first:

1. ingest a surface occurrence with locale, context, corpus revision, and
   provenance;
2. retain all supported source-sense candidates and visible ambiguity;
3. represent each sense as a neutral role recipe with grounding gaps open;
4. attach frequency to occurrence/sense/corpus, never to a word globally;
5. ground recipe atoms recursively or observationally and retain residue;
6. review any value-aligned composition and its computed semantic delta;
7. add NL, PL, symbol, and meme surfaces as many-to-many projections;
8. admit generation only after occurrence-bound selection, source round-trip,
   projection residue, and non-erasure witnesses pass.

No surface is banned. No meaning needs an English word to exist. Trust,
sovereignty, and vitality are semantic ingredients when the intended meaning
contains them and organism-relative observations when useful; they are never
admission gates or mandatory halos.
