# Inheritance — bringing a body home from the origin

This kernel's body first grew in `Coherence-Network`, the origin repo. The origin keeps its own
ledger of what it proved (`form/fourth-arm-bands.txt` in that repo); this kernel keeps no copy of it.
Lineage lives there and in git; this page carries only the practice for work here.

## When a live organ needs a body the origin proved

- **Need first.** A body comes home when a live organ here calls for it, not to complete a list.
- **Byte-exact.** It arrives from the origin's `form/form-stdlib/<name>.fk` together with its
  `tests/<name>-band.fk`, unchanged, and lands in the faculty whose organ needs it.
- **Re-proven here.** It reads re-proven only when its witness on `fkwu` equals the origin's four-way
  witness; a body that floors is never claimed proven. `observe/preflight.fk` names the arm a band
  binds on.
- **Then lifted.** Once home, it rises toward the highest grammar it can honestly carry
  ([`docs/uplift-high-grammar-goal.form`](../uplift-high-grammar-goal.form)); new meaning is authored
  in BML or higher.

What stays in the origin — its app layer, web and API carriers, and their Python — is named in
[`MANIFEST.md`](../../MANIFEST.md) under *Scope*.
