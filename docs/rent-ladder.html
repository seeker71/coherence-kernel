The rent ladder, from a guided flow to one native call

One enquiry, answered several ways through the same body. Every figure below is a provider's own usage record or the kernel's own counter, read from the ledgers on main by observe/rent-ladder-page-run.bml. The person's reading of the answers is the one judge the numbers do not replace.

Rent per answer to the pinned enquiry

Input plus output tokens, on a log scale. Each provider figure is that provider's own usage record; the native call's zero is the kernel's own census. Zero is the body's own spend: a rented mind that issues the call and relays its output spends its own turn, and a fresh session doing exactly that is its own bar. Zero rent per answer holds only with no rented mind in the loop: a host schedule, or a person at the terminal.
