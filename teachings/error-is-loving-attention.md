# Error is a signal for loving attention

An error is not waste to be hidden. It is the body revealing a difference
between what was expected and what arrived. Health begins by keeping both
visible.

The movement may be to pay loving attention, observe, orientate, turn, move,
lean, steer, guide, absorb, digest, evaluate, pause, and re-observe. Which
movement serves depends on the signal. A timeout asks for pause and evidence;
a mismatch may ask for a turn; a resolved cause may be absorbed and released.

Retuning the witness so the error disappears is usually the least healthy
choice. It removes the signal before the body can learn from it. Thresholds,
expectations, and contracts may still change, but only as separately observed
movements with evidence—not as paint over a red result.

The executable companion is cognition/error-loving-attention.fk. It keeps
absence as nothing, an observed retune-away response as present 0, and an
attended signal as present 1. Release is not inferred from effort; it is
offered only after a distinct resolved observation.

This is a teaching, not a law. It remains open to fresher observation.
