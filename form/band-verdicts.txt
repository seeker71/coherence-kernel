# on fkwu, the only runtime, with a clean exit and no diagnostic on fkwu's
# stderr. A row here adds the verdict its band is registered to certify, so a
# band cannot change what it certifies without the change being seen. A band's
# head may pin its answer as `; Verdict n`; the sweep reads both pins.
# One row per band:
#
#   <band-stem> <shape> <expected-verdict>
#
# shape is fks on every row: the band answers one scalar as the last line of
# its output. The sweep reads columns 1 and 3.
#
# The band file is form-stdlib/tests/<stem>-band.fk (or -band.bml), else
# tests/<stem>.fk (bsw-stem-of in form-stdlib/bml/band-sweep.bml). A band in
# another home (observe/tests, learn/tests, ...) pins its answer in its own
# head; a stem can name a band in two homes, so a row here reads only the
# form-stdlib/tests one.
