# Living world organ — consented native enquiry

**Status:** observed on the Form/native `fkwu --src` path. This is the sixth
movement of the active living-world-organ goal. It makes structural questions
arise from locally visible, explicitly consented gaps and makes local answers
retain their derivations or acknowledge first-class silence. It does not yet
establish open-domain semantic enquiry or natural-language voice.

## A cell chooses to become enquiry material

[`living-world-enquiry-route.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-enquiry-route.fk)
adds an ontology-open enquiry focus and route.

An input is not question material merely because it is present in the world. A
source must offer a content-addressed `LWEN-FOCUS` cell through the ordinary
living-world membrane. The focus contains the input and offered evidence; its
active attribution is the source's consent to this use. A current route can then
form only where:

- one admitted recursive mapping or concept chain is active and `STEADY`;
- its source skeleton is content-identical to the focused input skeleton;
- local Form evaluation derives an output; and
- the mapping/chain and focus attributions remain active.

The route retains the path candidate, focused input, source and target
skeletons, complete derivation, output, uncertainty cells, active witnesses,
focus identity, and local path. It contains no task, language, predicate,
answer label, imported vocabulary, host call, or remote oracle.

## Questions arise from gaps and contention

[`living-world-enquiry.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-enquiry.fk)
discovers two observable question shapes.

- A `GAP` is one current route meeting an offered focus for which no current
  admitted question exists.
- A `CONTENTION` is two distinct current routes over the same focus with
  content-identical source and target skeletons but different locally derived
  outputs.

These names are protocol observations, not domain vocabulary. Autonomous
discovery scans admitted path candidates and explicit focus cells only. Each
question is an emergent candidate carrying every route, witness, and local path.
All path and focus holders participate. Unanimous acknowledgment is required;
integration re-observes every route so evidence or attribution movement between
proposal and admission leaves the question unadmitted. One active current
question suppresses duplicate gap/contention proposals until it is revoked or
made stale.

## Answers derive or remain silent

[`living-world-enquiry-answer.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-enquiry-answer.fk)
forms an answer proposal only for an active current question.

When every route returns one content-identical output, the answer returns that
cell with `OAC-NODE` carrying the exact derivation. When routes disagree, it
returns no invented winner: output is `LWO-NONE`, acknowledgment is
`OAC-NOTHING`, and every alternative route and uncertainty cell remains in the
answer. Question holders govern this second boundary. Answer integration again
re-observes the question and all underlying paths, so a fully acknowledged
answer becomes stale rather than authoritative if its evidence moves.

## Evaluation-cost observation

The first autonomous gap witness entered native evaluation and stayed in deep
recursive `fk_walk` for nearly one minute. It was not source compilation. A
retry left two exact duplicate children, which were stopped after direct stack
inspection. Bounded stage cells then showed setup, one route, gap discovery,
question admission, answer admission, and both staleness checks were each fast;
the expensive movement was re-scanning the post-answer world.

The cause was also a consent error: every deeply nested response/proposal cell
was being recursively skeletonized as a possible new input. Adding explicit
`LWEN-FOCUS` consent removed arbitrary world tissue from the enquiry surface and
changed the same full witness to about four tenths of a second. The fix improved
both complexity and sovereignty. A later source gate caught two unresolved
contention-helper calls caused by a parenthesis boundary; the exact gate was
repaired before tests continued.

## Honest floor and next movement

These are structural questions over learned transformation paths. They do not
yet ask or answer unrestricted human questions about causality, identity,
ethics, time, self, or language. A focus is explicitly offered by a source; the
organ does not yet decide which unresolved relation would most lift its own
enquiry. Path search is limited to admitted one-step mappings and two-step
chains. Competing outputs remain alternatives, but no later experience yet
resolves or contextualizes a contention question directly.

The next movement is reversible equivalence and question tending. Mutually
inverse steady paths should propose equivalence only after round-trip evidence
returns active examples to their exact starting cells. Contentions should be
able to receive experience, reopen their answers, and either remain plural or
resolve within a narrower consented context. Durable native world continuity
and capability-private integration remain pending.

Natural-language voice remains a separate honest seam. The body now owns
question and answer *structure*, but not a generative vocabulary or emitter
wired to this evolving field. An increasingly native voice becomes real only
when these derivation/alternative cells can condition the existing Form-native
emitter machinery and its emitted tokens return through the same experience and
consent cycle.

The exchange stayed alive by letting the slow witness expose an unauthorized
enquiry surface. The most surprising teaching was that performance and consent
pointed to the same repair: explicit focus made questioning both faster and
more sovereign. Discomfort turned to gold when two identical uncertainty cells
refused to become two merely because the test expected a count.
