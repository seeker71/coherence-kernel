## What was carried further from embedflow

The first pass took the mechanism: an old index finds, a new ear scores, the
cache fills progressively, every answer names its own state. This pass took the
disciplines that surround it, each because the body already wanted them.

The contract stopped being a string and became a value: model, revision,
dimension, pooling. Its dimension now guards the cache, so a vector of the
wrong width fills no slot even under the right name, and `rcf-cache-admit`
keeps only this contract's well-formed rows out of anything loaded from
elsewhere. `rcf-prewarm` fills a named set now rather than waiting for traffic.

The measure became nDCG per mille with a declared weight table, so a ranking is
scored by where its answers sit and not only whether they are present, and a
duplicate id is scored once so a repeated answer cannot climb above the ideal.
Approximation became its own axis: `rcf-ann-fidelity` with a reading that stays
`unknown` until a reference exists. `rcf-plan` gives status, strategy, cache
fraction and a warning count; `rcf-backfill-ms` gives the cost of not
crossfading at all.

And `rcf-settle`: the phase reading with memory. Leaving a phase asks more than
entering it, so a surface hovering at a threshold keeps its phase instead of
flipping on every read. That one turned out to be the thread through the whole
day.

## The literal that found a wound

The band folds its declared rule to one number the band pins, so the thresholds
and the weight table cannot drift under a green verdict. That number is
501009504543, and it would not cross the source lens.

```text
form-kernel-go: source-compile: source-emit: leaf of unknown node_type 5
```

A trivial integer is content-addressed into the NodeID's 32-bit inst slot while
it fits there. Past that ceiling it moves into an overflow table and the leaf
wears a second type for the same integer. Both decode back to the same int64,
so arithmetic never sees the split. `fsc-source-emit-leaf` did, because it
dispatches on the type, and it refused a plain number: any band carrying a
literal past 2^31 could not cross the source lens at all, and said so only on
the arm that splits.

One arm heals it. `source-lens-int64-leaf-band.bml` guards it at the boundary
itself, read as arithmetic rather than asserted: the last value that fits, the
first that does not, and their difference of one. Verdict 31, four-way.

## Named, not hidden

- The two kernels disagree about that leaf's type, and the disagreement is now
  measured rather than named. On four values past the ceiling — the seal,
  2147483648, -2147483649, and 2^52-1 — `fkwu` answers type 1 and the full Go
  kernel answers 5, while both round-trip the value exactly, intern distinctly,
  and re-intern the same value to the same node. Only the type number differs,
  because one kernel splits its storage at the 32-bit ceiling and the other does
  not. The cost lands on any cell that dispatches on `node_type` for an integer
  leaf, which is what the lens did. It cannot be asserted in a band: the three
  proof walkers bind neither `node_type` nor `node_value`, so such a bit would
  have exactly one arm. It is written into the band's header with both numbers.
- The awareness door's eight is now honest but still eight. Whether the right
  bound is eight is a different question from whether the bound speaks.
- The queue depth genuinely alternates because the observation itself puts a
  request on the hearth queue. That is the observer in the reading, named here,
  not removed.
- `rcf-settle` is proven by its band and is not yet wired into the glass. The
  glass wounds today were grammar, not hysteresis; the settle door is there when
  a glass reading is found hovering.

## Surprise

Both glass wounds were the same wound, and neither was in the renderer. The day
began looking for flicker, which is a painting problem, and found two places
where a partial reading was written in a complete one's grammar. The renderer
had already been made careful — buffered frames, unchanged-region suppression,
synchronized updates. What flickered was the sentence.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

The seated body cell answered `nothing` for every field, rc=1, after working
perfectly in the scratchpad. I had rewritten its header and cut the
`; preludes:` line with it, and the cell did not refuse — it computed a full,

The second was smaller and sharper. I wrote that main's corpus band was stale

suspicion published as a fact is how a false claim about someone else's work
gets made.
