## Surprise, and where the discomfort went

The surprise was that form-cli runs a different walker from every band I read. validate.sh's fourth
arm is the runtime fkwu, so value_str's mode 25 answered everywhere I measured, seven bands, the drift
run and the live witness among them, while the form-cli table's walker, emitted from Form, had never
heard of it and wrote "cell" where a number belonged. A present native answers its name, and on that
walker no native was present.

The discomfort was two findings in one hour, both mine: the open-item line I carried five times, and a
landed change that broke a proof on main through a door one walker lacks. The turn was to measure
before repeating and before landing: the committed bootstrap was built to show its failure rather than
assumed, the regenerated page was read to its last line, and the fix went back to the pattern that
stood before me, where the recipe stays as the statement and the kernel with a faster door answers the
name itself.

Frontier word, row 1482: **carryblind**, an open item carried forward without being measured again, so
it keeps naming a state that has moved underneath it.
