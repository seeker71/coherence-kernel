## Optional means optional

Minimal reflections omit view, alternatives, unknown, choice, and timeout.
Richer reflections may carry any of them without making them mandatory for
other senders.  An additional offered view produces a separate unchosen
hypothesis rather than becoming a default lens.

Time remains three distinct observations:

- no observed time produces no timeout conclusion;
- time before expiration remains pending and is not `NOTHING`;
- expiration condenses to the sender's optional declared default when one was
  offered.

Immediate `NOTHING`, waiting, and expiration are therefore different histories.
