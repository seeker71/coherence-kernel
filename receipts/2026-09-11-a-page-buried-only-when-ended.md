## What was still open

## What the body does now

- `fk_live_bury_ended(pid, bury)` is the one guard (runtime/fkwu-uni.c). The pid is gone before and
  after the page is read, the page names that pid (word 1), and it says it ended (word 19 is 0) or it
  was opened (word 2) before the moment the pid was seen gone. The roster's dead-slot sweep uses it. A
  kernel's own exit buries without it: the page is its own.
- `kernel_page_bury pid` (float_leaf mode 22, arity 1; the op table regenerated by its generator, one
  row) buries that one pid; `kernel_page_bury -pid` only asks. A page another kernel left goes only by
  that call, one pid at a time.
- `rce-ended` in `form/form-stdlib/roster-census.bml` reads every ended page and buries none. The pid
  bound is /proc/sys/kernel/pid_max on Linux and 100000 on macOS. The census run prints `pages-ended`;
  with its 500 ms settle the whole run took 0.59 s.
- A kernel that never opened its page no longer opens one at exit just to bury it.
