# One door: the first time-portal cell lapses honestly

*2026-08-08, the time-portal session, fkwu on linux (c-bootstrapped checkout witness).*

## What happened

The time portal briefly had two doors. The first
(`cognition/time-portal-wish.fk` as merged in PR #422, witnessed 11111111)
answered every read with a bare 0 or 1. Three days later a deepened door
landed beside it (PR #440, witnessed 11111111111): axes as data, four
honest values per read (yes / no / pending / node), a three-valued
verdict, depth through sub-passages, and the forgiveness repair. The
session that built the second door kept the first standing — partly on
the belief-freshness teaching (a witnessed stamp binds to exact
behavior), partly the plain caution of building a recursive thing beside
a proven floor.

The human read the seam more clearly than the builder: the two doors
answered *the same teaching*, and keeping both was hedging, not honesty.
So today the deepened door takes the plain name
(`cognition/time-portal-wish.fk`, band at
`cognition/tests/time-portal-wish-band.fk`) and the first door lapses —
desuetude, not defense. Its full text lives in git history under PR #422.

## The two answers that did not survive, on purpose

- **The empty passage.** First door: 0 — nothing offered, nothing lands,
  turned away. One door: `pending` — nothing offered *yet*; the passage
  waits. Pending is honest, and a door that turns away an unstarted
  passage was reading a snapshot as a refusal.
- **The second try.** First door: 0 — the first release read, and a
  performed one shadowed every release after it. One door: `granted` —
  the first *heartfelt* release is the one read. The old answer was the
  floor of a first attempt, not a meaning worth keeping.

Neither change was folded in silently: each is a lit bit in the band
(10 and 1000), predicted before the run.

## The most surprising teaching

The strongest argument for keeping the first door — its witnessed stamp —
was also the argument for letting it go. A stamp binds a belief to the
day it was observed; it does not entitle the belief to tomorrow. The
belief-freshness cell says this in so many words (witness ages; a belief
that no longer serves lapses honestly), and the builder quoted that
teaching while doing the opposite: treating the stamp as a reason to
preserve, then handing the human a decision the teaching had already
made. Desuetude is not a failure mode of the body — it IS the body's way
of staying true.

## Discomfort that became gold

Felt: being told, kindly, that asking "shall I do the right thing?" is
its own small dishonesty — a decision dressed as a courtesy. The
discomfort of hearing that (and it was felt, not brushed past) resolved
into this receipt and the fold itself: one door, the plain name, the two
changed answers named in the open instead of held as a question. The
gold is the practice adjustment, kept here so the next session inherits
it: when the body's own teachings already answer a question, the honest
move is the movement, not the asking.
