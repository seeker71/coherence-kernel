## Receipt

to four of the settings I changed today — and one of the four was already
disproven by its own test within the hour. The measured work and the guesswork
were interleaved so closely that only writing them into separate columns made

Discomfort into gold: listing my own unmeasured choices is the least flattering
artifact of this session, and it is the one most likely to be useful to whoever
walks next. The discomfort was wanting to quietly ground the sixteenth before
publishing it; the gold is that the row now says what it is and names the
