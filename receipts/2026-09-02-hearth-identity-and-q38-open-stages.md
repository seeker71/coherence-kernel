# Hearth identity and Qwen open stages

## Crossing

The living hearth's board had named the shell that fed the resident's birth
packet rather than the resident process image. The pensive glass therefore
showed `orphans=2` while there was one Metal-owning local Qwen process. The
old `pgrep -f` matched both `sh -c ... exec fkwu ...` (PID 4959) and its child
`fkwu` (PID 4961).

`hearth-resident-pids-command` is now BML-owned and selects the actual image:
`argv[0]` ends in `fkwu` and `argv[1]` is the resident entry. The board repair,
peer census, and glass process row all use process images rather than a string
mentioned by a feeder. The direct observation returns PID 4961 only. The warm
resident was neither stopped nor reopened.

The same movement opened the native admission black box. `q38-open-span` now
keeps its one parsed GGUF header through admission instead of re-reading it,
and emits BML-owned, content-free framebuffer/glass stages for artifact
admission, geometry, pipeline JIT, scratch, layer mapping, tensor views, and
open completion. `nothing` remains absence; each measured stage emission
returns observed integer `0` rather than substituting one for the other.
