# Green instruments cannot say no — the 64-board measurement

Asked: how do we lift whole-carry from ~1% to at least 50%? The plan's first
draft assumed most board verdicts follow from native-witnessable evidence. The
measurement over two stores (claude 30 finished boards, grok 34; the codex
measurer died mid-response on a server error — its slice honestly pending)
answered with a finding that corrects the plan and improves it.

## The finding

**Evidence-determined verdicts: 27 of 64 (42%). And a perfect complementarity:
every evidence-determined verdict was a PASS** (plus the rare REVISE where the
reviewer *authored a new probe in-session* that made the disagreement
measurable — two mappings packing to 346==346 while their faithful surfaces
differ; a corpus `Authority` vs stored `authority` byte read). The other
nineteen claude REVISEs and eighteen grok prose-verdicts all sat on fully
green measured ground: bands green, preflight clean — and the *no* was born
in prose. The board's entire value-add over the native witnesses is exactly
the part a measured-features training row cannot yet carry.

A second independence, from the grok store: running evidence and being
determined by evidence are different axes — one board ran seven native
commands and its REVISE still hung on prose; another ran nothing and its
verdict was purely mechanical. Rows must be labeled by what *forces* the
verdict, not by what the session executed.

## What this does to the 50% plan

The verdict head becomes **two-stage, honesty structural**:
1. **Stage one, native**: evidence sweep + PASS-with-negative-controls. It may
   speak PASS (history says evidence determines those) and may *escalate*; it
   may never speak a no it cannot measure.
2. **Stage two, the no**: a manufactured probe (the two witnessed REVISEs show
   the shape) or rented prose — and every recurring prose objection that gets
   **operationalized** into a probe cell with a negative control joins the
   native floor *permanently*. The REVISE mass converts objection-class by
   objection-class: a ratchet, not a wall.

Corrected arithmetic: witness loop ~28% + evidence-determined boards ~18% ≈
46%, with the `talk` verb's sessions and the operationalization ratchet
carrying it past 50 — each converted objection class compounds.

## The most surprising teaching

The body's proof culture is built so that bands can only say yes — a band is a
claim held green. Sixty-four boards show the mirror image: the fleet rents
minds mostly to say *no*, and the honest no almost always required either
prose or a brand-new instrument. The student's first taught skill is therefore
not judging — it is **probe-authoring**.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

The claude measurer wanted to count two claim-vs-verdict mismatches as
evidence-determined because they were mechanizable *afterwards*; sitting with
it drew the honest line — a mismatch that had to be noticed by reading is
prose judgment even when it could later become a band bit — and that
reclassification (13/17 → 11/19) is what made the complementarity visible at
all. The measurement's key finding exists *because* the measurer shrank its
own favorite bucket.

## The frontier question

**What names turning an objection into a measurement that can refuse?**

*Operationalization* — social science's word for making a concept measurable.
An objection joins the native floor exactly when it is operationalized into a
probe with a negative control. 0-hit fresh; corpus row 1022, landed under the
counterweight before the numbers were written down.
