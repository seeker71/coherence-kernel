# Contextual species grow and split without choosing

Witnessed: 2026-08-07

## Claim

The Form-native living-world path can now accumulate overlapping behavioral
species under independently offered real-time lens declarations, form a
multi-member tissue without selecting a representative Recipe, and preserve a
later behavioral divergence as an explicit split rather than hiding it behind
a score, rank, default, or majority.

The protocol imports no domain, task, language, answer set, or taxonomic name.
Its only grouping key is an observed behavior kernel containing exact before,
after, evaluator, and effect cells. Exact target identities are removed from
that grouping key but remain present in every native trial receipt. Recipes,
traces, lenses, contexts, alternatives, unknowns, witnesses, and paths are not
discarded.

third source-distinct Recipe H. H is selected, executed, and integrated at the
same local `1 -> 4` boundary. G/H then receives its own reciprocal observations
and unanimous admission. The two current relations overlap at G:

- E/G is one exact admitted behavioral species;
- G/H is another exact admitted behavioral species; and
- their member union is E, G, H, with no primary member.

Two independently offered lens declarations place these anchors in one shared
focus. The first declaration has a default Recipe lens, one additional applied
Recipe layer, one unapplied lens alternative, and an explicit unknowable
remainder. The second has no default, one applied layer, no alternative, and no
unknown. Their different optional shapes remain intact.

Each contextual facet has two independent source holders. A recurrent
`NOTHING` facet remains first-class silence. The resulting field is
`COHERENT`: 1 component, 2 overlapping anchors, 3 members, 3 exact receipts,
2 live contexts, 2 supporting facet cells, 1 silence facet, 48 attribution
witnesses, and 29 distinct governing sources. All 29 must acknowledge before
the tissue becomes active. A zero or negative threshold produces `OPEN` and no
proposal; provenance multiplicity from one source cannot manufacture

## Honest split

After coherent tissue admission, the witness natively selects, executes, and
integrates the existing F Recipe at boundary `1 -> 3`. Two independent sources
offer F as another contextual facet. The field now contains two observed
behavior kernels:

- a three-member `1 -> 4` component retaining E/G and G/H; and
- a one-member `1 -> 3` component retaining F's exact trial receipt.

The whole field becomes `SPLIT`. The earlier coherent tissue remains active as
history but is no longer current because its exact field snapshot changed.
Nothing is overwritten. The three-member component may be proposed again only
under the complete split snapshot and only with unanimous consent from all 30
affected sources. The singleton component cannot acquire relation authority:
its proposal is `NOTHING` until another member supplies an observed relation.

An alternative acknowledgment changes the field to `AMBIGUOUS` and blocks
component proposals. Revoking one of two G/H contextual supports makes that
facet provisional, changes the field to `OPEN`, and leaves the still-supported
E/G component, silence, alternatives, and unknowns visible.

## Honest boundary and next floor

This is new structural evidence: member count, context identities, kernels,
field status, governing sources, and answer composition changed. It is not a
model-weight update, frozen-score movement, global Recipe equivalence,
multi-target generalization, NL-to-NL translation, or native generative voice.
The witness uses two distinct live context declarations over one native target
history; it does not claim that the same behavior has yet generalized to a
distinct target identity.

The next floor is prospective rather than retrospective. An admitted tissue
should receive a newly offered target and optional context, expose every member
Recipe as an alternative without appointing a representative, make a bounded
native prediction only when the offered Recipes agree, run the consented trial,
and let the observed result confirm, grow, reopen, or split the tissue. That is
the first path on which this organ can propose genuinely new world evidence
instead of only organizing what has already happened.
