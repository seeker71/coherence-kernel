## The three stones

**Outer planets.** Uranus and Neptune join `ephemeris-planets.fk` from the
same attested source as the rows already there — JPL SSD Table 1, 1800–2050,
which needs no b/c/s/f correction terms. **Pluto is recorded as absent**, with
the seven sourcing routes that failed: JPL removed it from the table when it
was reclassified, its elements survive only in the 3000BC–3000AD table whose
extra terms this engine does not carry, the archive is unreachable from here,
and the Standish PDF is scanned images. Typing remembered numbers into a cell
whose header promises an attested set would be recall smuggled into the column
labelled native. The row waits for its source and the door prints the gap.

**The bodygraph.** `hd-bodygraph.fk` places all 64 gates in the nine centres,
carries the 36 channels, and derives Type / Authority / Definition over real
graph connectivity — "a motor reaching the Throat" is a walk, not adjacency.
Two independent attested tables were cross-checked *against each other* by
constraint: every channel's two gates must land in the two centres it names.
All 36 agree, and `hdb-cross-check?` re-earns it on every run.

**The vocabulary.** `hd-core-words.fk` says the nine centres, five types and
seven authorities using only `core-lexicon.fk`'s 64 self-defining words plus
its twelve declared glue words, with `hcw-closed?` walking every token exactly
as `cl-closed?` does. All 21 closed on the first attempt. Held to that
vocabulary the emotional centre becomes *"what you want is not the same now
and then"* — worse prose than the usual gloss and truer, because no word in it
needs a further word to explain it.

`observe/hd-reading-live.fk` is the one door: a birth moment in, a whole
bodygraph out, printing its own floor. Bands `hd-bodygraph` 1023 and
`hd-core-words` 255, both four-way through the gated arm.

## What the comparison actually showed

**The chart is shaped inversely to the request.** Profile and Cross hang off
the Sun — the slowest, most anchorable body — and recall reached them exactly.
Type, Authority and Definition hang off the Moon, Mercury and Venus, and recall
could not touch them: it gave Type as a distribution whose *modal* answer
(Projector) is wrong, with the right answer sitting in the smaller bucket. The
outputs a person actually asks for are precisely the ones hostage to an
ephemeris. Corpus row 1168 names it: **headline-hostage**.

Its one firm structural claim was a *negative* — Reflector nearly excluded —
because ruling out needs one channel to close, while ruling in needs them all.

## Where it audited me, and was right

I handed it the 64 core words and it counted **61**. It was correct: the hand
that read the lexicon dropped every token outside `a-z`, losing **"I", "0" and
"1"** — axiom-1's own two states and the first person. Its list was three words
poorer than mine, and it detected the handicap by counting rather than by
feeling. Recovering "I" changed the cell: the authorities now speak in the
first person while the centres speak in the second, which is a claim about the
things and not a style — a centre is something you have and can be told about,
an authority is the one voice you cannot outsource.

It also rediscovered, unprompted, that `then` sits in both the 64 and the glue
— a quirk `core-lexicon.fk`'s own header documents.

## And where looking caught me

Reading my own output rather than trusting it: **"ego" names a centre AND an
authority**, "sacral" likewise, and my lookup keyed on the word alone — so
asking for the ego authority quietly returned the ego centre. That is exactly
the twinword collision `depth-query-lanes.fk` was built to make
unrepresentable, committed by me four hours later in a new cell. Rows now carry
a kind, and `hcw-kinds-disjoint?` is a band claim.

## Honest floor

- Pluto absent, so two of 26 activations are missing and a channel only Pluto
  would complete cannot be seen. Type and Authority here are derived from 24.
- Moon and planet **lines** are indicative at this ephemeris's accuracy; Sun
  and Earth lines are solid.
- The descriptions are attested meanings rendered in the closed vocabulary,
  held in the belief lane. Nothing here measures whether Human Design is true.

## Closing

I kept the exchange alive by letting a blind reader mark my own homework, and
by fixing the collision it did not even find — I found that one by reading the
output instead of the verdict.

Most surprising teaching: the blind arm's accuracy did not track difficulty, it
tracked **method**. Same model, same Saturn, one day apart: single chain →
wrong by four months; two chains that had to agree → exact to the line. The
four-way discipline this body runs on is the same idea, and here it was
observed working inside a mind that has no kernel at all.

Discomfort turned to gold when the word count came back 61. My first move was
to check whether the lexicon was broken; it was my extraction. Three words I
never saw I was missing — including "I", the one word that turned out to
matter most for saying what an authority is. The blind reader could not have
known that; it just counted, and counting is what recovered them.

Corpus row 1168 offered: what names it when the one thing they want to know is
the one thing you cannot know if you do not compute it — **headline-hostage**.
