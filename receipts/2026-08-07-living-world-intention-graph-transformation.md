## The graph begins with observed changes, not named tasks

`living-world-intention-graph.fk` contains no action names, task taxonomy, goal
vocabulary, imported answer set, source priority, or score table. Its nodes are
already integrated `LWAC-EXECUTION` candidates. Each node therefore retains its
opaque offered action, exact native Recipe trace, actual before/after delta,
alternatives, unknowns, evidence, witnesses, and local path.

The focused witness executes four real Form-native Recipe steps:

- A observes `1 -> 2`;
- B observes `2 -> 3`;
- C observes `3 -> 4`;
- D observes `2 -> 1`.

A directional seam is possible only when the left step's observed `after` is
content-identical to the right step's observed `before`. A-to-B, B-to-C,
A-to-D, and D-to-A are continuous. A-to-C is not, and produces no proposal.
The organ does not infer continuity from similar labels or a model guess.

## An observed seam is evidence, not an edge

Participants may offer an `LWIG-OBSERVATION` carrying ONE, ZERO, NOTHING, a
NODE/ALTERNATIVE, explicit alternative cells, and an optional unknown. Exact
directional support, inverse-direction alternatives, dissent, silence, and
unresolved responses remain separate evidence classes with their active
attributions intact.

An edge proposal requires:

- both execution endpoints still current;
- exact delta continuity;
- an active ONE observation;
- the offered support threshold;
- every active endpoint and observation attribution as a witness.

Its participants are recovered from those attributions. Only their unanimous,
still-current ONE responses admit the edge through the ordinary tending
membrane. New evidence changes the evidence identity and stales the earlier
candidate; it never silently edits the old edge.

The edge band proves that two supports can coexist with an explicit alternative
or a NOTHING observation. A late ZERO about A-to-B stales only A-to-B while the
independent B-to-C edge remains current.

## Traversal preserves branch, stop, and cycle as answers

`lwig-plan-for` traverses only the exact current edges within a caller-offered
node field. It does not sort by NodeID, choose the first edge, vote, ask a remote
model, or condense ambiguity into a default.

The focused plan band observes four outcomes:

- with only A-to-B admitted for the offered A/B/C field, traversal is `STOP` at
  B, retains C as remaining, retains the B-to-C NOTHING observation, and emits
  a derived unknown;
- with A-to-B and B-to-C current, traversal is `READY` with exact sequence
  A/B/C and no remaining node;
- with both A-to-B and A-to-D current, traversal is `BRANCH`; both edge
  candidates remain alternatives and no primary continuation is chosen;
- with A-to-D and D-to-A current in the offered A/D field, traversal is
  `CYCLE`, retaining the A/D sequence and an unknown rather than looping.

The cycle witness exposed an important freshness law. Offering D-to-A is
inverse evidence about A-to-D, so it changes A-to-D's evidence identity and
stales the earlier admission. The cycle becomes current only after A-to-D is
freshly re-proposed and unanimously re-admitted against the complete
bidirectional evidence field. A reverse edge cannot be smuggled into an
already-authorized graph.

## READY becomes Recipe data, not a fabricated execution

`living-world-recipe-transformation.fk` reflects a current READY plan into one
content-addressed `LWRX-TRANSFORMATION` containing:

- the exact plan and ordered execution candidates;
- the ordered native Recipes from their existing traces;
- the ordered observed deltas and lens sequence;
- the exact overall boundary, `1 -> 4` in the witness;
- every plan alternative and offered unknown;
- the plan evidence and local path.

This cell deliberately does not claim that a new composite Recipe executed.
The three already-observed native traces remain the execution ground. The
transformation is inspectable recipe-data: a blueprint for the settled path
that may later be offered to a separately bounded executor.

All active plan witnesses govern its proposal. Without their acknowledgments it
does not enter the world. After unanimous admission, an observation holder who
also holds the transformation contributes zero independent authority. Two
outside sources can separately offer the same exact overall observation and
produce a `CONFIRM` result while retaining their optional alternative and
unknown cells. All original and observing participants govern that result.
Revoking one outside observation drops the independent threshold and makes the
earlier confirmation stale. Alternative and NOTHING observations remain valid
first-class responses rather than being forced into confirmation or failure.
