# Living-world identity-aligned prospective suite

Date: 2026-08-09

## Claim boundary

This receipt observes one bounded Form-native movement: generated Recipe
hypotheses can be retained as plural candidates, differentiated by independent
placements, and compared prospectively on one predeclared held-out suite whose
dataset, split, and evaluation-contract identities are exact and separate from
candidate identity.

It does **not** establish general natural-language understanding, a broadly
improved world model, a native generative voice, learning from an open world,
or permission to promote a candidate.  The seven held-out cases are a tiny
constructed corpus.  The result is diagnostic non-promotion evidence.

## What changed

The prospective organ now carries these as separate content-addressed cells:

- the candidate's exact offered hypothesis field;
- dataset identity;
- a disjoint learning/differentiation/held-out split;
- an evaluation contract;
- each case's bindings, immediate acknowledgement, optional timeout, observed
  time, alternatives, optional unknowable declaration, optional lens, evidence,
  and local path;
- every derivation and every distinct output;
- a metric vector and an identity-gated comparison vector.

There is no winner, fixed class taxonomy, scalar quality oracle, accept/reject
bit, promotion field, or remote answer path.  A changed contract makes a
comparison inadmissible.  The same candidate is named as repeated and produces
a zero movement vector.  Revocation can stale a candidate locally or close the
dataset gate without rewriting history.

`NOTHING` now and a pending timeout remain different observations.  A sender's
optional timeout condensation is retained separately.  `ZERO`, an explicit
alternative `NODE`, and declared unknowable room also remain distinct.  Lenses
are optional and do not become authority.

## Recurrence repair

The first implementation exposed a deeper identity fault in
`living-world-recipe-hypothesis.fk`: a probe evaluation contained hypothesis,
output, comparison, and state, but not the exact probe identity.  Equal outputs
from different placements therefore interned to one cell, erasing recurrence.

`LWPH-PROBE-EVAL` now also carries the exact probe cell.  After the repair, two
independent differentiation placements remain four distinct receipts: two
compatible addition Recipes and two contradicted multiplication Recipes.  The
arriving tissue retains both compatible Recipes.  It does not collapse them to
an arbitrarily chosen representative merely because they share an output.

The incumbent retained twelve derivation traces and six distinct outputs.  The

The movement is comparable because dataset, split, and contract identities are

candidate produced a zero delta and is repetition, not new evidence.

This is a bounded placement improvement: plural output ambiguity decreased on
this exact suite.  It is not evidence that a general model or NL-to-NL voice
improved.

## Currentness and authority

Revoking one arriving-candidate support source made only that candidate's
evaluation stale; the incumbent remained current.  Revoking one case source
dropped the dataset below its support threshold and closed the suite for both.
Node and source history remained present.

No authority moved.  There was no promotion, owner authorization, external
training, remote oracle, or deployment decision.

## Honest failures that shaped the cell

The same direct band exposed each break rather than hiding it:

1. `1006645189`: one differentiation placement left compatible hypotheses
   below the two-source threshold, so no arriving evaluation existed.
2. Compiler failure: forward calls to `lwpsw-alternatives` and
   `lwpsw-unknown` were unresolved.  The definitions were moved before use.
3. Two placements still appeared as one recurrence because probe identity was
   missing from `LWPH-PROBE-EVAL`; the exact probe was added.
4. `1019117567`: exact placement incorrectly required exactly one matching
   derivation, which would erase equivalent Recipes.  Exactness now means one
   distinct output with at least one matching derivation.
5. A delta frame showed the sentinel `-4499999999999999999` because generic
   `map` was incorrectly used with `node_value`; explicit Form recursion now
   extracts delta values.
6. The declared-unknowable expectation was corrected from seven to six because
   the `ZERO` case intentionally leaves unknowable undeclared.
