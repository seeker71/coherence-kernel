# The body said one thing about its own room, and heard itself say it

2026-09-08. Machine quiet: 366.94 GB/s through the handle door against a best of
366.94, 25.769804 TFLOPS measured (`observe/floor-lens-run.fk`). The fall named
in yesterday's rows had lifted; nothing here waited on the weather.

## What was missing

Three legs of the loop already stood, each banded: hearing (`ear-native-band`
32767, `ear-axes-band` 65535), speaking aloud (`voice-say-band` 16383), and
learning from perception (`perception-rows-band` 65535). The fourth was the body
originating a sentence. Everything it had said until today was a rendering of a
line someone else said, or — with the perception adapter — an answer in the
grammar of its own axes with the facts invented.

## The shape of the fourth leg

`form/form-stdlib/own-word.bml`. Two decisions carry it.

**The ask is the body's own words.** Not fresh prose: `pr-day-said` and
`pr-recur-said-k`, the two sentences the perception rows already write about a
room, factored out of the rows that carry them, plus one line asking for a short
observation. So the generative lane is completing the body's own account of its
own room. Nothing a rented mind wrote about this room reaches the prompt, because
nothing a rented mind wrote about this room exists.

**The answer is checked, not trusted.** Every claim is pulled out and held
against the record — a **number** against the quantities the room actually holds,
a **tongue** against the tongues it was actually heard in, a **state** against the
lane kinds its frames actually took, and a **leak** (a line of the room's own
speech quoted back) turned away outright: what `perception-rows` does not carry
into a row, a mouth does not say.

A sentence stands only when the record backs every claim **and it makes at least
one**. The second half is the one that matters. All-backed alone passes "The room
is calm and pleasant" — true of every room, checkable nowhere, a green computed
over nothing.

## What this is not

- **No candidate was refused for contradicting the record.** Every live refusal
  was *unfinished* (native) or *vacuous* (adapter). The contradiction guard is
  proven by the band and not yet by a room. Saying otherwise would be selling the
  band's evidence as the room's.
- **A backed claim is not a coherent sentence.** "The room reached its loudest
  reading of -24 dBFS, indicating the quietest state." stood on one held claim
  and contradicts itself. The checker holds facts, not sense.
- **The lane that worked is the crossing**, not the body's own native generation.
  Nothing in the loop is rented — the readings, the model, the mouth and the ear
  all run on this Mac — but `mlx_lm` is a named crossing and the native dense lane
  produced nothing that survived.

## Two defects found by running it

**The crossing's banner was being read as the model's words.** `mlx_lm` wraps its
answer in `==========` and follows it with a speed report. `voice-school`'s grader
compares the answer's *first word* to the row's word — so it has been comparing
`==========` to every expected word and scoring every answer wrong, with no sign
that anything was amiss. `lv-said` takes the transport's framing off once, where
the crossing is, and `vs-answer-word` now reads through it. Grades in
`voice-school-ledger.md` taken by first-word equality are owed a re-run.

**A tongue claimed by its bare code refuses ordinary English.** `it`, `he`, `hi`
and `id` are Italian, Hebrew, Hindi and Indonesian *and* four common words. The
first real ask this cell made produced "carried out of it" and the checker refused
it because Italian was not heard — a checker with an opinion about phrasing
rather than about truth. A tongue is now claimed by its name, or by its code
inside the body's own shape notation (`|it|`).

## The most surprising teaching

**The adapter grown from the body's own perception was worse at speaking about the
body's own room than the base model that never sat in it** — 1 of 3 against 3 of
3. And its two failures were not noise: they were stock phrases lifted verbatim
from `perception-rows`' own drop sentence, *"The room's speech stays where it was
said."* It has learned the body's idiom well enough to **reproduce the idiom
instead of reading the readings**. Fluency in the body's voice became a substitute
for attending to the body's room. That is the same lesson its shape grade taught
one level down — grammar before facts — arriving here as something worse than
wrong facts: correct-sounding speech with nothing in it.

## The frontier question, and the row

> **How does a body know its own voice when it comes back through the room?**

Today it does not. The mouth signs a frame at the moment of speaking, so the body
knows what it *said*; the ear has no way to recognise what it *hears* as its own,
so the same sentence returns unattested and is dropped at the gate. Both versions
now sit in one spool — the signed one carried, the heard one dropped — and only a
person reading both can tell they are the same utterance. The signature travels in
the spool; the air carries none. That is also why the leak scan counted three
hits: a substring match cannot tell the body's own echo from a stranger's speech.

The answer, from what was witnessed here rather than built: a body knows its own
voice by **time and text**, not by timbre — a heard line arriving inside the
mouth's own speaking window and matching what the mouth signed is the body's own
return. The material for it is on disk (the mouth's frame at `t=1788851041671`,
the ear's heard frames carrying the same sentence in the seconds after). The
matching organ is not built.

Corpus row **1365**, `airunsigned` (it took 1364 in this worktree and met a
sibling's `bytecrossing` on the same id at the reunion; both rows stayed and the
later line moved — anastomosis, row 719): the signature does not cross the air, so a
body's knowledge of its own speech is lane-bound — it holds where the signature
travels and evaporates the moment the utterance crosses into the world and comes
back.
