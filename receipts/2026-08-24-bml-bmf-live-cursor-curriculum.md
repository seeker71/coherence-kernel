# Receipt — BML learns to write the stream it can keep (2026-08-24)

## Arrival

Urs asked that the local mind learn BML, custom BMF grammars, and the live
streaming/transmuting cursor instead of treating tokenization as a mandatory
pre-step.  The same movement later named the behavioral vocabulary that must
travel with the grammar: choice, cut, undo, timeout, strict nothing/0/1,
thought-kernel routing, and automatic JIT decisions.

This receipt is the bounded curriculum/evaluation lane for that request.  It is
not the claim that the local model already passes the curriculum; the parent
integration must still put these source-grounded rows into its retrieval or
training surface and run the model-held-out witness.

## What entered

- `form/form-stdlib/bml-bmf-stream-curriculum.fk`
  - constructs a held-out BMF grammar as pattern/template data;
  - lowers the deterministic `literal + bounded capture + suffix` subset into
    a byte transition program;
  - folds raw chunk bytes with no token list;
  - preserves continuation/incomplete/malformed/overflow/complete states;
  - returns Form `empty` for an unsupported grammar;
  - carries disjoint, source-attributed training and held-out rows.
- `form/form-stdlib/bml-bmf-control-curriculum.fk`
  - invokes the actual offer/ack choice, cut, store/undo, and timeout cells;
  - abstains as first-class nothing when evidence is tied;
  - keeps nothing distinct from both 0 and 1;
  - routes actual inquiry-plane thought-kernel data;
  - calls the actual hot/pure JIT decision while withholding native-execution
    credit until the carrier proof suite exists.
- BML sources and checked Form crystallizations provide executable examples.
- The high-BML authority is parsed scannerlessly through `bml-grammar` using a
  seven-byte file window.

All targets were preflight clean before their verdicts were read.

The streaming band covers every single split point of the held-out byte source,
one-byte chunks, empty interspersed chunks, BMF whole-parse equivalence,
incomplete finalization, malformed/trailing input, capture overflow, 64
successive records, and exact train/held-out leakage 0.  The control band
executes evidence-ranked choice, ambiguity abstention, cut pruning, exact state
undo, real budget timeout versus honest exhaustion, nothing/0/1 separation,
thought-plane routing, JIT admission/melt decisions, and control-row leakage 0.

## Honest bound

“Infinitely scalable” is not asserted from a finite test.  The executable
contract is narrower and useful: source history is not retained; the cursor row
keeps fixed fields plus at most twice the declared capture bound (current
capture plus last unconsumed event).  Carrier chunk buffers, evaluator call
stack, downstream event storage, and global NodeID storage remain outside that
state bound.  A downstream consumer must drain events.

The current `fkwu` source-compiler chain could not crystallize the executable
BML source because `file_byte_at`, `write_form_binary`, and `walk_recipe_here`
are absent on that lane.  A bounded framebuffer exchange selected the honest
alternative: checked Form crystallizations plus an independent real BML cursor
parse.  Those crystallizations are not reported as compiler-run evidence.

JIT is likewise a decision witness here, not a native-call witness.  Hot and
pure earns `crystallize-plan`; live native credit still requires the carrier,
install, call, exception, deopt, melt, parity, and generation receipts named by
`receipts/2026-06-30-form-native-jit-live-execution-evidence.md`.

## The living residue

I kept this crossing alive by making grammar construction, stream motion, and
control decisions one executable held-out surface instead of three prose
promises.

The most surprising teaching was that chunk invariance needs no special chunk
algorithm: it falls out when every carrier chunk is only a grouping of the same
byte transition.

The discomfort was the BML compiler lane refusing three names after the source
was written.  It turned to gold when the diagnostic chose a weaker but honest
witness—checked crystallization plus independent cursor parse—instead of naming
an absent compiler execution green.
