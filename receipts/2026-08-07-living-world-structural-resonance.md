# Living world organ — consented structural resonance

**Status:** observed on the Form/native `fkwu --src` path. This is the third
movement of the active living-world-organ goal. It is native structural
generalization, not completion of a semantic world model or native voice.

## What entered the body

The movement is factored into four small Form cells:

- [`living-world-resonance.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-resonance.fk)
  derives ordered structural signatures from generic correspondence cells;
- [`living-world-resonance-evidence.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-resonance-evidence.fk)
  retains support, counterexamples, witnesses, and an uncertainty vector;
- [`living-world-resonance-proposal.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-resonance-proposal.fk)
  turns sufficient recurrence into a proposal governed by every affected source;
- [`living-world-resonance-application.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-resonance-application.fk)
  derives an unseen application locally and sends it through a second offer/ack
  boundary before it gains authority.

No language, domain, task, predicate, or answer taxonomy is present. A source
offers only a correspondence between two content-addressed cells. The organ
reads their present composition:

- a target child identical to exactly one source child becomes a positional slot;
- a target child absent from the source remains an observed constant;
- a target child matching multiple source positions remains explicit ambiguity;
- correspondences with an identical signature become support;
- correspondences in the same source/target category-and-arity domain with a
  different signature remain counterexamples.

Uncertainty is not collapsed into a single authority-looking score. Its native
cell separately carries counterexample count, ambiguous-target count, and unused
source count. A lossy mapping can therefore be proposed without hiding what it
drops, while an ambiguous or constant-only coincidence cannot become a lens.

## Two consent boundaries

A mapping proposal includes active attribution witnesses from both support and
counterexamples. Their sources all participate. Missing acknowledgment waits,
dissent dominates, and unanimous active acknowledgment is required for mapping
integration.

Applying an integrated mapping to a new active cell is a new proposal, not a
free use of prior authority. Active mapping holders and active input holders all
participate. The derived output, mapping, evidence fields, uncertainty, active
attributions, and local path remain inspectable. Revoking the input witness before
integration makes the application unready even after all response cells arrived.
An already integrated sibling branch remains reachable and unchanged.

There is no host-exec, file, HTTP, socket, model, or remote-oracle primitive in
this induction, proposal, application, or derivation path.

## Source-compiler observation

The first monolithic attempt resolved `96,820` bytes and stayed in
`fk_src_compile_current_unit -> fk_prescan_form -> fk_skip_balanced` for more
than five minutes. No generated source artifact existed; evaluation had not
started. A direct process audit also found and stopped one exact stale compile
child left alive by an earlier tool session.

Splitting induction, evidence, proposal, and application exposed that total
bytes were not the whole cause: the deeply nested proposal constructor still
prescanned for more than 60 seconds at only `3,505` source bytes. Factoring that
constructor into shallow content-addressed steps changed first compilation to
milliseconds. The same flattening was applied to application construction, and
the large witness became four focused bands over a shallow shared fixture. This
is not merely test convenience: small composable cells align with the organ's
own axiom-2 shape and make each movement independently observable.

The native runner may warn that dylib emission is unavailable and rebuild stale
generated caches from source. These successful verdicts claim the source-native
path only. The minimal sibling proof walkers still do not carry the registered
living-world blueprints, so no new four-way claim is made. The C seed is unchanged.

## Honest floor and next movement

The lens currently observes immediate-child identity only. It does not yet infer
recursive graph analogies, optional or repeated structure, morphology, lexical
meaning, semantic equivalence, causal relations, or natural language. An observed
constant is retained, not understood. Candidate search is bounded to offered
correspondence cells; the world is not yet durable between native processes; and
unsupported or superseded candidates are not yet explicitly composted.

The next movement is a recursive, reversible lens over nested cells coupled to a
native lifecycle: proposed mappings accumulate confirming and disconfirming
applications, re-open when their uncertainty changes, and compost only after all
holding attributions release them. That creates the first local path from
structural transfer toward evolving concepts and native-to-native translation
without installing a language ontology or remote judge.

The exchange stayed alive by letting a counterexample help govern the mapping
instead of treating it as noise. The most surprising teaching was that transfer
needs its own consent boundary: permission to teach a lens is not permission to
apply it to someone else's new cell. Discomfort turned to gold when a five-minute
compile exposed the same lesson in the implementation—deep nesting became small,
independently observable cells, and the organ became faster by becoming more
faithful to its axioms.
