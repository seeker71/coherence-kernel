architecture, the right JIT, the right cache, the right substrate
lookup, proper high-level design.

xtal-as-source was a published lowering so daily walks would not pay
the compiler. That is a reason a derived image exists. It is not a
reason to author a second ice.

The body already holds the seats:

- **Architecture** — one engine. The recipe that proves is the recipe
  that can become native. No hand-fast twin.
- **Lookup** — intern. Same meaning, one node. A copy on disk is not
  an address.
- **Cache** — `cache-fresh?`. Ice is source; `.fkb` regenerates when
  older; git does not keep it.
- **JIT** — heat at the door. Cold walks Form. Hot births native once.
  Cool melts. Rest waits until an ask.
- **Design** — BML pictures, `Offer<T>`, ask outside the stream.

Committed xtal sits where cache and heat already know how to sit.
Missing: the load path names BML as ice, and treats every Form image
as water or heat — disposable, earned, never authored.

This movement adds only a picture,
`form/form-stdlib/bml/form-cli-one-body.bml`. No compile cell. No
xtal. Executable stays `false` until a walk is hot enough to want one.
