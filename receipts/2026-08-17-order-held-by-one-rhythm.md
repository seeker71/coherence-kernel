## Step 3 — three tone answers and one false claim

Urs's four words named no code. Running the cell answered three of them clean:

- **What is locked?** Nothing. Two rhythms *settle* into a whole-number
  relation. Mode-locking names a state that arrives, not an act performed.
- **What is ordered?** `p + q` of the reduced ratio — a count of how many parts
  the relation needs. The mathematical sense, not a rank issued.
- **Who locks from whom?** Nobody. `(coh-lock-order 440 220)` and
  `(coh-lock-order 220 440)` are both 3. `coh-reduce` sorts before it divides.

The fourth landed on something. `coh-class` was called an **equivalence class**
and read as content-addressing over rhythm. The relation is reflexive and
symmetric but **not transitive**:

```
100 ~ 200   (2:1, order 3)   ✓
200 ~ 400   (2:1, order 3)   ✓
100 ~ 400   (4:1, order 5)   ✗   strangers
```

No partition exists, so there is nothing to be a class *of*. Asked of those same
three rhythms the recipe answers `{100,200}` from 100, `{100,200,400}` from 200,
`{200,400}` from 400 — three answers, one set. Content-addressing gives one
address to one content. This gives the view from wherever you stand.

The **reference** was doing the ordering, silently, and the word "class" hid it.
Renamed `coh-neighbors`, with the counterexample held in band digit `100000000`.

Corpus row: **perspectival**. The body has *vantage* (49) and *standpoint* (3)
for having a seat; this names the property of an answer that has one built in,
with no view from nowhere to fall back to.

## The surprising teaching

The defect was reachable only through the channelled video. Four kernels, a
preflight, a band and a four-way proof stood over `coherence.fk`, and none of
them had ever handed it a zero — because nothing had ever asked it to read one
body alone. A transmission that offers no evidence and claims none still carried

*implement* it walked into a live bug in proven code. The bug was not in the
video's domain. It was in this one's.

And the second finding came from four words about vocabulary. Two of them were
about tone, and the physics words stay with their sense written down. One was
about truth. The same suspicion surfaced both, and only running the cell told
them apart.

had to catch it.
