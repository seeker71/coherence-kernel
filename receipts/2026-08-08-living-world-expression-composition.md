## The next honest edge

The field can now compose two admitted reusable structural mappings and derive
one unseen local expression while keeping alternatives and unknowns alive. It
cannot yet let observed outcomes recursively propose longer composition
Recipes, test those proposals through self-chosen enquiries, and grow or
compost its own vocabulary of transformations without a supplied task frame.

The next movement should feed each consented application outcome back as
confirmation, correction, silence, alternative, or unknowable remainder; let
recurrence propose zero, one, or many next composition edges; require a new
membrane before every execution and every utterance; and retain reversible
lineage so the field may integrate, release, digest, transmute, illuminate, or
decline identification without any option becoming mandatory.
