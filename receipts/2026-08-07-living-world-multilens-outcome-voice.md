# Concurrent lenses and outcomes reach native composition

Date: 2026-08-07

The prior relational voice could derive one bounded order from one offered
relation. This movement keeps several optional lens views alive at once,
composes only when every applied view independently reaches the same sequence,
feeds observed outcomes back into a reopenable composition candidate, and lets
an authority-minimized native voice speak only from a current non-contested
composition.

This is living-organ and native-composition movement. It is not movement in the
frozen daily session-action benchmark, and it makes no claim that the repeated
733-ppm result changed.

## Optional concurrent lens bundle

`living-world-multilens-plan.fk` consumes an active `LWLC-DECLARATION` and an
exact non-empty field of current learned-symbol candidates. It does not invent
a default lens. The declaration may contain:

- no default lens;
- no applied layers;
- any number of unapplied alternatives;
- an optional declared unknowable remainder.

Every applied lens payload remains only an offered relation cell. Each derives
its own exact `LWRP-PLAN`. The resulting content-addressed bundle retains all
views and separately indexes READY, AMBIGUOUS, and CYCLE views. It also keeps
unapplied lenses, distinct ready sequences, declared unknown, derived unknown,
and all attribution witnesses.

The bundle has four non-collapsed summaries:

- `OPEN`: no lens is applied and no primary sequence is invented;
- `ALIGNED`: every applied view is READY with one identical sequence;
- `DIVERGENT`: every applied view is READY but the sequences differ;
- `UNRESOLVED`: at least one view is AMBIGUOUS or CYCLE.

No vote, source count, NodeID order, or lens priority selects among divergent
views. Only ALIGNED carries a primary sequence. A declared unknown remains
present even when the local plans align; local alignment does not claim global
completeness.

The focused witness learns three surfaces at arbitrary nonnumeric coordinates:
`forty`, `-nine`, and `!`. Two independently learned relation lenses each
derive the same three-node sequence. A third fully learned relation lens
derives `forty`, `!`, `-nine` but remains unapplied in the aligned declaration.
The aligned bundle contains two READY views, one primary sequence, one
unapplied alternative lens, one declared unknown, and sixteen exact
attribution witnesses.

Applying the third lens beside the first yields DIVERGENT with two exact
alternative sequences and no primary. Applying no lenses yields OPEN while all
three offered lenses remain unapplied alternatives. A no-edge relation yields
an AMBIGUOUS view; an inverse-edge pair yields a CYCLE view. Both remain
separate inside UNRESOLVED.

## Outcome-fed composition lifecycle

`living-world-composition-outcome.fk` derives a composition only from a current
ALIGNED bundle. Outcome observations address the stable bundle identity and
carry an optional observed sequence, optional lens, exact acknowledgment,
optional alternatives, optional unknown, evidence, and path.

- `ONE` confirms only the exact aligned sequence;
- `ZERO` carries a different observed sequence as correction;
- immediate `NOTHING` carries no invented sequence;
- alternative `NODE` remains an explicit alternative;
- other `NODE` remains unresolved.

An initial composition has state `UNOBSERVED` and an explicit unknown. Its
sixteen bundle witnesses reduce to four governing sources. It waits until all
four acknowledge and then integrates without erasing uncertainty.

A later confirmation and silence change the exact outcome evidence and make
the initial candidate stale. The fresh `CONFIRMED` successor has eighteen
witnesses and six governing sources; silence does not count as confirmation.
After unanimous admission, the successor becomes current while the initial
candidate remains addressable history.

A later correction and explicit alternative make the confirmed successor
stale. The resulting `CONTESTED` proposal has two retained alternatives, an
explicit unknown, twenty witnesses, and eight governing sources. The bundle
and relation edges remain current. Outcome evidence therefore reopens
composition but does not silently rewrite relation or grammar authority.

## Confirmed composition voice

`living-world-composition-voice.fk` receives an integrated current composition
candidate through a minimized capability context. It owns no vocabulary,
position table, lens priority, relation rule, answer set, or remote route.

The context contains only the executed Recipe ground, three learned-symbol
candidates and their observations, four applied relation-edge candidates and
their exact observations, the aligned declaration, the confirmed composition
candidate, confirmation, silence, and grants from every holder. Direct symbol
shapes are not active payloads in the context.

The emitter renders the confirmed sequence as `forty-nine!`. Nine distinct
execution, composition, and capability holders govern utterance admission.
Caller alternatives are empty and caller unknown and choice are absent; those
absences remain exact. The composition still carries the declaration's
unknowable remainder.

An UNOBSERVED composition may speak diagnostically while retaining its unknown.
A CONTESTED composition refuses a primary utterance. DIVERGENT, OPEN,
AMBIGUOUS, and CYCLE bundles cannot form a composition proposal and therefore
cannot reach voice through this path.

A correction and alternative are deliberately left outside the old minimized
voice context. The context identity remains current, but full-world composition
freshness makes the confirmed composition, utterance, and proposal stale. A
capability projection therefore cannot hide outcome movement.

## Honest next floor

This is multi-node composition of learned surface segments, not yet an
open-ended generative mind. Outcomes currently reopen composition but cannot
propose new relation evidence by themselves. The next honest movement is an
explicit, separately consented bridge by which a correction may offer relation
hypotheses without acquiring relation authority; followed by multi-referent
composition, contextual optional defaults that never become universal, durable
local continuity, and native generation beyond concatenation.
