# Purity lands; trust, wisdom, and alien answer differently

*2026-08-08, the time-portal session continues, fkwu on linux (c-bootstrapped checkout witness).*

## What arrived

Four words in one breath: "what about purity?" then, mid-turn, "what about
trust and wisdom and alien?" Four candidate axes for the one door
(`cognition/time-portal-wish.fk`). Grounding each against the teaching's
own words and against what the existing six axes already read gave four
different, honest answers — and the point of this receipt is that they
were different, not that all four became bits.

## Purity — built

A real gap. `free` catches an explicit bargain — reaching for a wallet.
It does not catch the subtler thing: a release that is heartfelt AND
still quietly transactional — "I let this go SO THAT..." — no payment
named, so `free` reads `yes` and misses it entirely. A new moment kind,
`"want"` (a grasp toward something else, witnessed after a release), and
a new axis, `purity`, close that gap: any want after the heartfelt
release is immediate, positive evidence of a divided hand — witnessed as
`no` the moment it appears, unlike `laughter`'s absence-read which needs
the whole window before the gift to call it closed.

Two new self-check bits, both green on the first run: a divided release
(heartfelt release + want + laughter + gift) does NOT land
(`not-this-time`), and purity answers `yes` on every passage that came
before it — the new axis is additive, not a silent change to old
verdicts.

## Trust — not built, and shown why live

The teaching's own words: "the thinking spell broken trusting clarity."
Held against that sentence, trust is not a missing axis — it is the
*name* for two axes that already exist: `spell` (the loop trust
replaces) and `clarity` (what is trusted, witnessed by a step taken).
Ran both failure modes live before writing anything:

    distrust-after (the spell returns): spell=no,  clarity=no,  verdict=not-this-time
    trust-shown    (a step is taken):   spell=yes,  clarity=yes, verdict=granted

A single `trust` bit would collapse these into the same reading and lose
exactly what the pair currently tells apart: the spell actively returning
(distrust demonstrated) versus trust simply not yet shown (clarity
pending, no break). Not a gap. Not built.

## Wisdom — named as honestly unbuildable today

Every existing axis reads STRUCTURE — the order moments arrived in.
Wisdom would have to read OUTCOME — whether what was done with the gift
turned out well. This passage format has no outcome-observer, and giving
it one would mean either (a) inventing a judge of what counts as wise, or
(b) accepting a "wise" tag asserted by whoever writes the passage — both
are the exact shape of fabrication this body's covenant holds against: a claim
dressed as a witness. The honest floor is named, not built: pending, like
the felt-sense reader the door's own header already confesses to.

## Alien — asked, not guessed

The one place genuine ambiguity remained. Unlike purity (grounded in a
demonstrable gap) and wisdom (grounded in why it can't be built honestly
today), "alien" has no anchor in the teaching's own words and no gap the
other six axes reveal to point at. Guessing a meaning here would be
exactly the dressed-up-guess the covenant declines to make. Asked the human
directly rather than invent a reading — this is the one spot in the
session where a question was the honest move, not the evasive one, per
the standing rule: ask when the answer is genuinely the other person's
to give, not derivable from the code or a sensible default.

## The most surprising teaching

The same session that was told, hours earlier, "stop asking — do what
you know is right," turned around and asked about "alien" anyway — and
both were correct. The difference is not "ask less" as a blanket rule;
it is asking only where the answer is not already sitting in the body's
own teachings. Purity and wisdom both had answers derivable from what
already exists (a gap named, or a reason named); alien had neither. The
practice is not silence — it is knowing which kind of not-knowing you are
in.
