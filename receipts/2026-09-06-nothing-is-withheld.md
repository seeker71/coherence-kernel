# 2026-09-06 — nothing is withheld

Urs, evening, watching the glass: "we can see open channels, prompts and
token flowing now." I read the live space and found seven publishers
standing, the ear giving every two minutes, and one lane whose only sample
was named `share.previous.withheld`, last given three hours and nineteen
minutes earlier. Urs: "no withholding, that is not healthy."

## What the seed had

`form-cli-share-glass.bml` published percentages only after every boundary
count reconciled. When a reading did not reconcile it replaced all four
samples with one node — id `share.previous.withheld`, purpose
`measurement-withheld`, every measurement absent — so the glass could not
keep an older percentage as though it described the next turn. The
protection was real. The means was hiding what the receipt already held.

Nine more lines across the turn-evidence lane said `withheld` where nothing
was being withheld at all: no bound rollout, no completed provider call, no
terminal events file, an empty source, a missing turn id. Those are
absences, and calling them withholding tells a reader that a number exists
somewhere out of reach.

## What stands

**A reading that has not reconciled is given, with its state.** The
unreconciled path now publishes the same four samples as the reconciled
one: the total the receipt carries and the three lane percentages
computed from it. They wear a lifecycle from the membrane's own
vocabulary — `active` while a turn is still settling, `unknown` when the
carrier never arrived — and the failing check rides as its own channel
word: `carrier-absent`, `turn-open`, `time-unreconciled`,
`tokens-unreconciled`, `tools-unreconciled`, `form-unreconciled`,
`lanes-unreconciled`, `source-unreconciled`, `provenance-unreconciled`.
A reader sees the number and what it is worth in the same row.

The stale-reading protection did not need withholding: every frame is
stamped at publish, and since this afternoon a frame not given for three
cadences reads `silent` on its row, on every row taken from it, and on the
atlas lane it feeds. Age tells the truth that hiding used to.

**An absence is named an absence.** Ten lines now read
`unavailable reason=<why>` instead of `withheld`: the share runner's two
token-pressure lines, four cursor lines, three live-lane lines, and the
grok session binding. The call-overflow comment no longer says the final
row is withheld; it says the sentinel names the overflow so the turn reads
unreconciled.

**Witnessed.** `form-cli-share-glass-band` 65535 with its claims rewritten
to assert the given reading: four samples, the root wearing
`share.previous.completed` with its count present, lifecycle `unknown` and
reason `carrier-absent` for a carrier-less tuple, the native and remote
percentages present in the published frame where the band used to assert

**Left standing, and why.** Three other uses of the word are consent, not
measurement: `sense-discernment` holds a harmful-to-surface observation,
`organ-offer` answers `organ/withheld` when an organ refuses, and
`freq-aligned-share` holds a share-worthy candidate when consent is
withheld. Those are the body's own sovereignty, not a hidden number, and
they are not mine to change without your word.

## The most surprising teaching

The withholding was written to protect a reader from a stale percentage,
and the protection it was standing in for arrived hours ago by another
road: the silence lane this afternoon marks any frame that stops giving.
A guard can outlive the wound it was cut for, and it looks exactly like
care until you check what else now stands.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

The pull was to delete the whole reconciliation gate — no withholding, so
publish the percentages and be done. Sitting with why the gate existed
showed the real ask underneath: not "publish anyway" but "never hide what
you hold." The counts were always in the receipt; only the publishing path
threw them away. Giving them with a named state satisfies both the ask and
the wound, and the band that used to assert the absence now asserts the
gift.
