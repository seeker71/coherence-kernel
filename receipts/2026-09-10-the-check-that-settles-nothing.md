## What was actually wrong

Told to check the frequency first, I ran the check.

In this body a `rule` is a mechanism. `object-rule` 153, `compiler-rule` 121,
`apply-object-rule` 57, `apply-bmf-compiler-rule` 67, `ct-rule` 41, `bmf-rule`
35. Every one is applied to a term and watched produce another term. None is

`must` 624, `law` 329, `always` 190, against `witnessed` 1623, `observed` 1328,
`measured` 1079. `never` is the most frequent word of the eight.

So the count licenses no purge and no principle. That is the reading. It stayed

## Surprise

The check had no verdict in it. I expected it to clear my principles or condemn
my vocabulary. `never` 1933 against `witnessed` 1623 does neither. A frequency
check is not consulted for a ruling — it shows that the ruling was never
available. That is the reason the principle should not have been trusted, and
the count is how it becomes visible rather than argued.
