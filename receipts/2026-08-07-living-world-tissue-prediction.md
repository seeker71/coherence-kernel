target, execute every exact Recipe in an admitted contextual tissue, preserve

timeout may each be present or absent.

## Honest boundary and next floor

This is real structural learning movement: a separately consented observation
changes the next prediction's identity, derivation, governing set, and—on
mismatch—its placement from `CONSENSUS` to `BRANCH`. It is not model-weight
training, frozen-benchmark movement, external-model promotion, NL-to-NL
translation, or a native generative voice. No authority moves without explicit
holders and unanimous acknowledgment.

The present three Recipes are still constant finite source expressions over
the already witnessed `1 -> 4` boundary. Their native traces retain the new
target and live context, but the Recipe source does not yet consume an offered
before-cell or relational field as a parameter. Therefore this witness proves
closed-loop evidence uptake, not semantic extrapolation to a new world state.

The next floor is parameter-bearing prospective tissue: a Recipe must consume
the newly offered before/context cells, derive a genuinely new after-cell,
offer an inspectable action, and meet independent observation across more than
one target identity. Only then can held-out placement speak to world-model
growth rather than constant-Recipe continuity.
