# An offered action can learn its next prediction without self-promotion

Date: 2026-08-07

The living organ could already execute an explicitly selected Recipe and retain
its result, but `flow` and `changes` were caller-supplied trace fields. It could
not yet gather competing action offers, refuse arbitrary choice, derive an
observed change from native execution, compare that change with an offered
prediction, or carry a correction into a separately consented next cycle.

`living-world-action-cycle.fk` now crosses those boundaries in Form-native,
content-addressed tissue. This is structural cycle-to-cycle learning. It is not
a model-weight update, a change to the frozen 733-ppm session-action score, an
external training run, a remote oracle route, or promotion authority.

## Action vocabulary remains outside the organ

The module contains no names for `observe`, `be`, `do`, `integrate`, `release`,
`digest`, `transmute`, `illuminate`, `identify`, or any other task taxonomy.
An action is an opaque offered cell. Its exact intention retains:

- a focus and action cell;
- an optional real-time lens declaration, including any caller-offered default
  lens and ordered layers already represented by `LWLC-STACK`;
- the exact current Recipe lens and before-cell when local execution is sought;
- an optional predicted delta;
- any explicit alternatives and optional unknowable remainder;
- an optional open choice;
- an optional sender-authored timeout contract;
- evidence and a local derivation path.

An action may be absent in a valid offer; that offer remains inspectable but is
not executable and cannot become the primary. Non-Recipe lenses likewise stay
in the field without being forced through the current numeric Recipe codec.

## Unique recurrence may select; ambiguity may not

Every active action offer becomes exact offer evidence. Offers with identical
source-free intention identity share one recurrence group. The field retains
all intention evidence, explicit alternatives, offered unknown cells, dependency
attributions, and threshold.

A primary exists only when exactly one current Recipe intention reaches the
offered recurrence threshold. With zero eligible intentions, or with two or
more, the field is `OPEN`, primary is absent, every candidate remains an
alternative, and a derived unknown names the unresolved field. No majority,
NodeID ordering, source priority, first-item rule, hidden default, or remote
model breaks a tie.

The focused witness has two sources recurring on opaque action A and one source
offering opaque action B. Action A alone reaches threshold, while action B and
both callers' explicit alternative cells remain present. Five distinct holders
across action offers, focus/action dependencies, Recipe/declaration, and input
govern selection. Giving action B a second independent offer makes both
eligible; the field becomes OPEN and emits no proposal. Revoking action A's
source attributions also reopens the field and stales the old selection.

## Execution derives the change it claims

Only an integrated, still-current selection can execute. The cycle calls the
existing Form-native Recipe evaluator once to observe its result, constructs an
exact `LWAC-DELTA(before, result, lens)`, and re-runs the trace constructor with
that locally derived delta. The `changes` slot is therefore no longer trusted
from the action offer.

In the focused cycle the Recipe evaluates to `49`. The offered prediction says
`before -> 48`; the observed delta says `before -> 49`. The immediate comparison
is neither ZERO nor silence: it is an `OAC-NODE` alternative whose reference is
the offered prediction and whose offered value is the actual delta.

No prediction would return the actual delta through the generic NODE arm. An
exact prediction returns ONE carrying that exact delta. Inability to execute
returns NOTHING. Those arms retain their existing content-addressed identities.

The sender's timeout is observed beside, not instead of, the immediate result.
At observed time `5`, expiration `10` remains `OAC-TIME-PENDING`. At `10`, it
becomes `OAC-TIME-OUTCOME` carrying the sender-declared action-B alternative.
A timeout with no declared default condenses to an outcome carrying NOTHING.
Immediate NOTHING and expiration-to-NOTHING therefore remain distinct history.

Execution admission is separately unanimous across the active selection and
all Recipe/declaration/input witnesses. Revocation of the underlying action
offer stales both the selection and execution candidate.

## Independent observation can propose revision, not authority

Two sources that do not hold the execution candidate independently offer the
same observation confirming the actual `before -> 49` delta. Self-observation
by an execution holder is excluded from the independent-observer threshold.

Because the native comparison already shows that the old prediction differs,
the resulting learning cell has protocol state `REVISE`. It retains:

- the exact integrated execution and independent observation;
- old prediction `before -> 48`;
- proposed prediction `before -> 49`;
- all alternatives and the content-identical unknown cell;
- the still-pending timeout observation;
- seven governing sources across execution and independent observation.

The identical unknown offered by the action field and observer has one NodeID,
so its union contains one cell rather than fabricating two copies. Observer
revocation stales the learning proposal.

Even after unanimous learning admission, the revision does not activate an
action. It can only describe an exact successor offer. Participants must offer
that cell again through the ordinary membrane. While both old and revised
predictions have two active offers, the next field is OPEN and neither wins.
Only after the old sources explicitly revoke the old offer and re-offer the
revision does the next selection become READY. Its native execution compares
`before -> 49` with `before -> 49` and returns ONE.

That second-cycle change—from alternative mismatch to exact ONE under aligned
identities—is the observed learning movement. It is not silent replacement or
self-granted authority.

## Honest next floor

The executable branch is still the bounded numeric/control Recipe codec. The
organ does not yet discover Recipe source transformations from repeated deltas,
compose multi-step action intentions across several lenses, schedule its own
timeouts, generate new natural-language surfaces beyond learned segment
composition, or persist a self-directed enquiry loop across process restarts.

The next useful movement is a multi-step intention graph: each settled delta
may become consented evidence for a structural Recipe transformation; several
current transformations may form an inspectable plan; execution may stop,
branch, return an alternative, or leave an unknown remainder at every edge;
and no plan or rendered explanation may become authoritative without the
holders of every dependency crossing its separate membrane.
