# Living-world open transition and learned-surface witness

Date: 2026-08-08

## Claim

The Form-native living-world path can now observe arbitrary offered cells after
an arbitrary-length offered context, derive every recurrent next-cell branch
without a fixed action taxonomy, integrate a branch only through unanimous
participant tending, preserve unpromoted possibilities and the unknown, and
project current derivations through participant-learned surface cells without
an imported vocabulary, grammar, remote answer, or arbitrary winner.

This is a new organ capability.  It is not a score improvement in the frozen
session-action benchmark and does not change that benchmark's repeated `+733`
ppm control result.

## Direct observations

- `living-world-transition-field-band.fk` returned `262143`: all eighteen
  context, recurrence, plurality, unknown, growth, provenance, source-local
  revocation, and foreign-veto-refusal claims landed.
- Its same-child framebuffer exposed thirteen causal cells through
  `receive-observations`, `propose-recurrence`, `consent-integrate`,
  `predict-plural-unknown`, `grow-new-cell`, and `revoke-support`.  The first
  supervisor sample arrived within one second; the observed Form phases had
  completed within one millisecond.
- `living-world-transition-expression-band.fk` returned `262143`: all eighteen
  derivation-currentness, learned-surface, plurality, unknown, local staleness,
  and re-offer claims landed.
- Its same-child framebuffer exposed twenty-one causal cells through the
  transition phases and `teach-surfaces`, `consent-surfaces`, `project-voice`,
  and `revoke-revoice`.  The first supervisor sample arrived within one second;
  the final Form phase was observed at three milliseconds.
- Revoking one surface supporter made only that surface candidate stale.  The
  other derivation remained speakable and the affected derivation remained
  explicitly unvoiced.  A replacement surface became current only after its
  supporters and the surviving alternative-surface stakeholder all tended it.
- A next cell absent from the organ source became a third current prediction
  after two independent offers and unanimous responses.  No source edit or
  action-class extension was required.

## Honest boundary

The surface fixture proves a vocabulary-free route by which exact derivations
can acquire, lose, and reacquire consented surface cells.  It does not prove a
general natural-language generator, autonomous vocabulary discovery from real
sessions, quality gain, promotion readiness, or authority movement.  External
large-model training, model promotion, and remote oracle calls remain outside
this witness.
