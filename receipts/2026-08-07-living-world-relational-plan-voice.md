# Optional relation plans reach the native voice

Date: 2026-08-07

The prior grown voice still received an absolute integer position with each
surface observation. This movement removes that requirement from the growth
path. Symbols may now carry any offered coordinate cell; recurring situated
relation observations propose directional edges; a bounded group derives a
`READY`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or `CYCLE` plan without arbitrary tie-breaking; and only a
`READY` plan may order learned surfaces for native composition.

This is living-protocol movement. It is not movement in the frozen daily
session-action predictor, and it makes no claim that the repeated 733-ppm score
changed.

## Coordinates are optional lenses

`living-world-symbol.fk` now stores an opaque coordinate cell. It may be a
slot, relation anchor, lens-local address, blueprint, recipe, or another
consented cell; the symbol identity assigns it no universal meaning.
`lwnv-symbol-at` and `lwsl-observation-at` accept that exact coordinate.

The old numeric constructor remains as a compatibility lens for the already
witnessed position sorter. It is no longer the generic symbol validity rule,
and that sorter explicitly refuses nonnumeric coordinates. Existing numeric
voice bands remain unchanged.

## Recurrence grows relation edges

`living-world-relation-learning.fk` begins with no relation vocabulary.
Participants offer an exact relation cell, two current learned-symbol
candidates, an optional lens, an acknowledgement, optional alternatives, an
optional unknowable remainder, and evidence. Source identity remains outside
the observation and is recovered from active attribution.

For one offered relation and endpoint pair:

- exact-direction `ONE` observations support the edge;
- inverse-direction `ONE` observations remain alternatives;
- explicit alternative `NODE` observations remain alternatives;
- `ZERO` remains dissent;
- immediate `NOTHING` remains silence;
- other `NODE` observations remain unresolved.

No absent optional field is filled with a default. Every active attribution in
all five evidence classes governs admission. The focused witness contains two
supports, two alternatives, one dissent, one silence, and one unresolved
observation. Its seven attribution witnesses and seven sources unanimously
govern one forward candidate. One inverse support cannot independently cross
the offered threshold. Support revocation or a later inverse observation
changes exact evidence identity and makes the old edge stale without erasing
it.

## A group can be ready, ambiguous, or cyclic

`living-world-relation-plan.fk` receives an offered relation and a non-empty
field of current learned symbols. It builds one content-addressed group from
those exact nodes, every current admitted in-group edge of that relation, and
their active attribution witnesses.

The local topological observation has three non-collapsed results:

- `READY`: exactly one zero-incoming node exists at every step. The ordered
  sequence is inspectable; alternatives are empty and unknown is absent.
- `AMBIGUOUS`: multiple next nodes are locally possible. Those nodes remain an
  exact alternatives field and the unresolved remainder is explicit.
- `CYCLE`: nodes remain but no next node is available. The closed edge set and
  remaining nodes stay intact, with an explicit unknown remainder.

No NodeID ordering, numeric position, majority vote, or arbitrary tie-break
chooses among alternatives. A two-node/one-edge group derives `READY`; the same
nodes under a relation with no admitted edge derive `AMBIGUOUS`; two admitted
inverse edges derive `CYCLE`.

## Relational native surface

`living-world-relational-voice.fk` receives the minimized capability context,
execution basis, referent, offered relation, and exact node field. It emits
only when the context derives a current `READY` plan. An `AMBIGUOUS` or `CYCLE`
plan remains available to inspect but produces no primary utterance.

Alternatives, unknown remainder, and choice are independently optional. The
focused voice deliberately supplies an empty alternatives field and leaves
both unknown and choice absent. No hidden default is inserted.

The voice witness learns `forty` and `-nine` at two arbitrary blueprint-cell
coordinates. Neither coordinate is numeric, and the compatibility position
sorter returns no sequence. Two recurrent forward observations and one inverse
observation grow one admitted relation edge. That edge alone orders the current
symbol candidates and Form renders the executed referent `49` as `forty-nine`.
The utterance has ten exact attribution witnesses and six distinct governing
participants across execution, symbols, relation, and capability.

The minimized context contains no active direct symbol shapes. It re-derives
the learned symbols and edge from explicitly granted observations. A later
inverse observation is intentionally left outside the old context. The context
identity stays current, while full-world plan freshness invalidates the old
utterance and proposal. Capability minimization therefore cannot hide relation
movement.

## Honest next floor

This is relational ordering of learned surface segments, not yet an open-ended
generative voice. Surface segments, relation observations, and bounded node
groups still arrive through participants. The next movement is multi-node
grouping and composition across optional concurrent relation lenses, with
outcome-fed re-observation, context-local defaults that never become universal,
and explicit room for unchosen alternatives and what remains unknowable.
