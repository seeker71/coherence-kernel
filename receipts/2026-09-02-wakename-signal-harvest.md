## The most surprising teaching

The names were never missing. They rode in every `.fkb` image the body
ever wrote, section intact, walked past by one function whose name said
so plainly: skip_symbol_image. Three observation heals in one day —
misjoined names, rowless fires, sleeping names — and each time the body
had already written what it needed; a lane just stepped past it. The
observability debt of this body is not absent data: it is unread data.

## Where discomfort became gold

The identical counts across preflight and voice-frequency looked,
for one comfortable moment, like a deep shared-recipe truth — two organs,
one profile, a pleasing story. The discomfort of not believing a pleasing
story is what re-ran the sweep with honest cleanup and turned "two organs
share a profile" into "my cleanup never ran." A signal that flatters the
instrument gets re-witnessed first; today that reflex was the difference
between a receipt and a fable.
