# A transformation candidate can mature without becoming a winner

Date: 2026-08-07

The previous movement let the Form-native body reflect a settled intention path
as exact Recipe data. It still could not let a new candidate Recipe enter that
path's field, refuse arbitrary choice among candidates, execute one under its
own local contract, gather independent observations of the result, or return a
successful candidate to the action cycle without self-promotion.

`living-world-transformation-nursery.fk` now closes one full local loop:

```text
optional offered Recipe
  -> exact recurrence field
  -> unanimous nursery selection
  -> bounded native trial
  -> independent result recurrence
  -> unanimous growth admission
  -> inert successor description
  -> explicit re-offer
  -> ordinary action selection and execution
```

This is observable structural learning in the living organ. It is not a model
weight update, an improvement to the frozen 733-ppm session-action benchmark,
an NL-to-NL score movement, an external training call, or promotion authority.

## There is no mutation vocabulary inside the nursery

The module contains no named mutation operators, synthesis taxonomy, task
labels, answer table, source ordering, or remote oracle route. A nursery offer
may carry:

- an optional current Recipe-data transformation target;
- an optional local Recipe, declaration, lens, and before-cell;
- zero or more alternatives;
- an optional unknowable remainder, choice, and timeout;
- evidence and the local derivation path.

Incomplete offers remain valid cells. A syntactically valid Recipe whose source
exceeds its offered byte budget also remains an inspectable candidate, but it
is not executable and cannot become eligible. Absence is not silently filled.

Source-free candidate identity excludes only offered evidence. Every optional
choice remains part of exact identity. Identical offers from two sources form
one content-addressed offer-evidence cell with two active attributions. The
first field run returned `32759` because its witness incorrectly expected those
two sources to create duplicate evidence cells. Correcting that expectation,
rather than duplicating the cell, produced the exact `32767` verdict.

## Exact recurrence proposes; ambiguity stays OPEN

The focused nursery contains two local Recipe candidates for the already
admitted `1 -> 4` transformation:

- candidate E is independently offered twice and evaluates to `4`;
- candidate F is offered once and evaluates to `3`.

Only E reaches recurrence threshold two. F, both candidates' explicit
alternatives, and E's offered unknown remain in the field. All target holders,
candidate offerers, and dependency holders govern selection; nine distinct
sources participate. Missing acknowledgments leave the candidate inactive.

When a second source offers F, both candidates reach threshold. The field
becomes `OPEN`, primary is NOTHING, a derived unknown names the unresolved
field, every candidate remains an alternative, and no proposal exists. The
organ does not choose by NodeID, list order, majority, score, default, or model
opinion. Revoking one E offer likewise reopens the field and stales the earlier
selection.

## The trial derives the boundary it claims

Only an integrated, still-current nursery selection may execute. Candidate E's
Recipe carries the existing Form-native evaluator identity, pure numeric effect,
and source-byte budget `128`. The trial first evaluates the Recipe, constructs
its exact actual boundary from the native result, then reconstructs the trace
with that derived change. No caller-authored result is trusted.

The reflected target boundary has three lenses and the trial boundary has one.
They are therefore content-distinct cells even though both observe `1 -> 4`.
The comparison explicitly tests before/after agreement and returns ONE carrying
the actual one-lens boundary. It does not collapse behavioral agreement into
identity.

Candidate F's separate branch observes `1 -> 3`. Its comparison is a structured
NODE/ALTERNATIVE referencing the expected `1 -> 4` boundary and offering the
actual `1 -> 3` boundary. It is not reduced to false or discarded.

The sender-authored timeout remains beside the immediate comparison. At
observed time `5` and expiration `10`, the trial holds a pending-time cell while
the immediate comparison already holds ONE. Revoking E's Recipe attribution
stales both nursery selection and trial.

## Independent recurrence may mature; it may not self-confirm

An outcome observation may carry ONE, ZERO, NOTHING, NODE/ALTERNATIVE, explicit
alternatives, and an optional unknown. Observations by sources already holding
the trial candidate contribute no independent evidence.

In the focused witness:

- one trial holder offers the exact confirmation but is excluded;
- two outside sources independently confirm the actual boundary;
- a third outside source explicitly returns NOTHING.

The evidence set therefore contains two confirmations and one silence. Silence
does not disappear and its holder governs the proposal. The READY growth cell
retains all three trial alternatives, the offered unknown, complete evidence,
and twelve governing sources across trial and observation.

One outside confirmation remains below threshold and emits no growth proposal.
A ZERO correction or alternative observation creates contention and reopens
the growth field. Revoking one of the two confirmations also drops recurrence
below threshold. In both cases the previously integrated growth candidate stays
in history but becomes stale and cannot describe a successor.

## Maturity describes a later action; it does not activate one

An integrated, current growth cell can construct an exact `LWAC-OFFER` carrying
the transformation target, candidate Recipe/declaration/lens, predicted
`1 -> 4` delta, alternatives, nested unknown field, choice, timeout, and growth
evidence. That offer is not added to the world.

Before re-offer, the ordinary action field is OPEN and has no proposal. Two
holders then explicitly re-offer the successor through the existing living
membrane. Nine target, Recipe, offer, and dependency sources govern ordinary
action selection and native execution. The resulting actual delta is exactly
`1 -> 4` under candidate E's lens, equals the offered prediction, and returns
ONE. Revoking one successor offer stales both the selected action and its
execution.

The nursery therefore completes one real learning cycle without granting
itself a claim, action, or continuation.

## Honest next floor

Candidate Recipe source is still offered by participants; the body does not yet
synthesize or mutate source by itself. The local evaluator is source-byte
bounded but still not gas-bounded. Recurrence is exact Recipe identity, so two
different Recipes that repeatedly produce the same boundary do not yet become
one behavioral species. The completed loop is one bounded cycle, not durable
self-continuation across process restart. Native natural-language generation
also remains unbuilt.

The next movement is a behavioral transformation species organ: independently
execute several distinct, consented Recipe candidates against the same current
boundary; preserve mismatches, resource observations, alternatives, and unknown;
let repeated exact behavior propose an equivalence class without ranking a
winner; and allow that class—not a privileged source string—to participate in
later concept, enquiry, and native-voice composition.
