## What was already counted, and what was missing

`observe/answer-parity-check.fk` floors an answer: how many of the nine axes it
names, how many receipt paths it cites and how many of those exist, whether it
admits a limit, how many bytes. That is presence. It cannot tell a number the
body measured from a number the voice invented, and it cannot see an answer
saying the same thing three times.

Both are exactly countable. `form-stdlib/bml/form-cli-voice-quality.bml` counts
them: every maximal digit run is a number, thousands separators read through; a
number the answer states that its packet does not contain was not given to it.
Two shares read together — **grounded**, how much of what the answer says was
given to it, and **used**, how much of what it was given the answer reached for —
because faithful and narrow is a different shortfall than broad and invented.
Repetition is the distinct share of the non-empty lines.

## What it says

The two answers whose fidelity can be checked **invented nothing**: every number
they spoke was in the packet they were handed. Their shortfall is the other
share — they reached for 4% and 13% of what they were given, named 0 and 1 of
nine axes, and then repeated themselves. The failure of the small native voice
is not fabrication. It is abandonment.

The answers that look broad — nine axes, a limit named — come from the retired
loopback lane, and their packets were never retained, so nothing about their
faithfulness can be established now. That is the reading's own honest hole, and
it is a retention hole rather than a model one: the native lane keeps
`packet.txt` beside `answer.md`, so every walk from here can be checked.

Rows land in `receipts/voice-quality-ledger.jsonl`. None of this is a verdict; a
count is attention. Felt resonance is still the person's to read.

## Receipt

The surprise: the voice everyone worried was inventing things has never been
caught inventing anything — the two checkable answers are 100% grounded. What it
actually does is stop. The worry was aimed at the wrong failure, and only a
count could say so.

Discomfort into gold: I had written into the goal, from an earlier session's
note, that the unbound 3B "invented axis judgments". Measuring made that claim
unsupportable — the packets from that era are gone, so the sentence asserted
something the body cannot show. Removing my own inherited claim was the
measurement's first result.

Frontier question, answered by the rented mind and offered to the corpus:
**what is the shortfall of an answer that says only true things and almost
nothing?** **thinfaith** — faithful to every word it speaks and to almost none of
what it was given. Precision without reach reads as trustworthy and leaves the
question unanswered, which is why the two shares must be read together.
