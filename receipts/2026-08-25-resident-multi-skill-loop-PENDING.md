encodes the initial user turn once, generation retains the exact generated IDs

3. perform one strict lookup and inject one typed NodeID observation;
4. inject a typed BML/BMF/Form teaching challenge;
5. generate attempt one and execute-check its exact three artifacts;
6. inject a check bound to that candidate, verdict, signal, and their digests;
7. retain attempt two as either the exact success acknowledgment or one
   candidate/digest-bound undo/refinement;
8. on refinement, execute the second candidate, inject its distinct fourth
   check, and require a fresh exact success acknowledgment;
9. release the same q38 stream state and context.

source identity, concept/challenge teaching replay, candidate-content replay,
candidate-digest replay, a verdict borrowed across attempts, a refinement whose
parent is not the exact prior check, a replayed final verdict, false
acknowledgment, leakage in either retained attempt, reopened residence,
collapsed `nothing`/0/1, timeout/failure alias, or missing release.
