# A native answer can be contradicted and reopen its own world model

Witnessed: 2026-08-09

## Claim boundary

This movement closes one real learning loop between Form-native profile voice
and the Recipe-hypothesis tissue beneath it.  Participants can offer exact
outcomes for one current profile answer.  Confirmation, correction, silence,
alternative, timeout, lens declaration, unknown, and evidence remain distinct
content-addressed cells.  Recurrent outcomes form one plural proposal; only
unanimous acknowledgment admits that cycle, and admission alone cannot change
the model.  Exact derived probes must cross the ordinary offer and tending
membranes again before placements and a successor tissue can become current.

The organ contains no task name, domain label, imported answer set, surface
vocabulary, or remote route.  Its only presently executable observation codec
is an integer cell because the Recipe evaluator currently returns integers.
Non-integer outcome cells remain valid evidence but cannot produce an
executable probe until a separately offered codec can carry them.

## What moved

The witness starts from the current finite profile class containing two
surviving hypotheses and one shared native answer.  Two independent holders
recurred four exact outcomes:

- one confirmation carrying `ONE(observed)` for the shared output;
- one correction carrying `ZERO` relative to the answer and a different exact
  observed value;
- one explicit alternative carried through `NODE`;
- one immediate `NOTHING` whose expired timeout condenses separately to the
  sender-declared alternative.

The correction also retained a live lens declaration with one optional default
lens and one additional layer.  No lens was made mandatory and neither lens
altered the source answer by construction.

The admitted cycle retained four evidence cells, four derivations, and 206
exact attribution witnesses.  Before unanimous tending, the candidate was not
active and exposed no probe successors.  After tending, four probes became
available, but none entered the world automatically.

The correction probe then crossed a second independent two-holder offer,
proposal, acknowledgment, and integration membrane.  Its derivation preserves
both meanings of the acknowledgment boundary:

- the outcome's `ZERO` is dissent relative to the earlier answer;
- the lower probe's `ONE(observed)` records that an exact value was in fact
  observed.

The translation is inspectable and never renames dissent as consent.

## Holding zero-compatible contradiction honestly

The generic emergent readiness gate requires positive coherence.  A fully
contradicted tissue truthfully has zero compatible hypotheses and therefore
zero coherence.  The first successor proposal was current and unanimously
acknowledged, but it remained inactive because the generic gate could not hold
that state.

`living-world-hypothesis-tissue.fk` now has a type-specific contradiction
readiness boundary.  It does not change the coherence metric.  It admits a
zero-compatible contradicted tissue only when:

- the tissue state is exactly contradicted;
- at least one exact receipt is contradicted;
- the complete witness threshold is met;
- every witness remains active;
- every proposal participant acknowledges the same candidate.

The final witness therefore holds contradiction as the current model state
without dressing contradiction as coherence and without selecting a new
hypothesis.

## Nothing now, timeout, alternatives, and lenses

Immediate outcome acknowledgment owns learning semantics.  A timed default is
retained as a separate effective acknowledgment but never becomes probe
evidence.  In the witness, immediate `NOTHING` remained the probe observation
even though expiration produced the sender's alternative.  The alternative
outcome remained unresolved rather than becoming an observed value.

The correction's default lens and additional lens layer remained optional and
current only through their declaration offers.  Alternatives and unknowability
coexisted with the correction; none was selected by list order or hidden rank.
