# A learned world model can ask again and know the boundary of sameness

Witnessed: 2026-08-09

## Claim boundary

This movement lets an already learned successor tissue become the carrier of a
new Form-native enquiry.  Participant-supplied binding blueprints unfold exact
scoped possibilities without installing a domain, taxonomy, task, answer set,
preferred question, or remote route.  Every surviving Recipe executes on every
offered binding.  The resulting profiles can say that distinct hypotheses are
observationally indistinguishable in that exact finite space while preserving
all hypotheses, executable sources, provenance, uncertainty, alternatives,
and revocation.

This is world-model growth, but not a score improvement or universal
equivalence proof.  The organ learned a new epistemic relation:

```text
two different executable hypotheses
  + one exact consented possibility field
  + identical observations at every offered point
  -> one finite-space observed-profile class
  -> two hypotheses retained
  -> no representative, collapse, promotion, or default
```

## Why repetition had stopped moving

The active loop had already reduced an incumbent with four Recipes to a
successor tissue with two hypotheses.  Re-running the seven-case prospective
dataset produced no new separating question: all seven offered bindings were
aligned for the two survivors.  That was verification of the same field, not
further learning.

The new recursive path offered a wider, independently content-addressed
binding blueprint.  It contained two participant-named slots with ten finite
numeric views each.  Form unfolded their full cartesian product:

- slots: 2;
- values per slot: 10;
- derived binding cells: 100;
- separating trials: 0;
- aligned trials: 100;
- hypotheses profiled: 2;
- distinct executable source identities: 2;
- observed profile classes: 1;
- hypotheses retained in that class: 2;
- profile points retained: 100.

The broader enquiry therefore supplied new admissible evidence, but it still
did not distinguish the two executable sources.  The correct result is local
observational sameness with an explicit finite-space unknown, not another
forced probe and not a claim that the Recipes are universally equivalent.

## Organic binding blueprints

`living-world-binding-blueprint.fk` owns no slot name, arity, value, task,
answer, or preferred combination.  A blueprint contains an ordered sequence of
participant-named slots; each slot contains an arbitrary source cell and a
finite field of numeric views.  Expansion recursively follows the exact
offered fields and imposes no fixed slot count or value cutoff.

Every derived numeric view travels through a scoped projection whose scope is
the exact blueprint.  Expansion constructs cells only.  The blueprint,
sources, projections, bindings, and enquiry space still require ordinary
offers and recurrence before the enquiry is current.

The production files contain no numeric fixture values or learned surface
literals.  The sole constructed string in the blueprint organ is the exact
participant-supplied slot name.

## Recursive carrier and reversible profile classes

`living-world-hypothesis-enquiry.fk` now accepts either:

- the initial integrated hypothesis field; or
- an integrated learned successor tissue.

For a tissue carrier it executes only the tissue's surviving hypotheses, but a
selected trial still lowers through the tissue's exact underlying hypothesis
field.  Any independently observed answer can therefore enter the existing
probe/placement membrane and grow another tissue rather than opening a
parallel authority route.

`living-world-enquiry-profile.fk` creates one binding/output point field per
hypothesis and groups profiles only when those point fields are content-
identical.  A class retains the exact enquiry as its uncertainty boundary and
the enquiry's exact witnesses as provenance.  Withdrawing one of the two
required offers for the enquiry space changed recurrence from two sources to
one; the historical enquiry and class remained cells, but both ceased to be
current.
