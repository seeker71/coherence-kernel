# The walk that did not come home

The keeper fired at 03:30 as it was asked to. At 19:52 it was still speaking.

## What was there

`launchctl` said `runs = 2` and `last exit code = 0`, and the log held nothing
but a compiler rebuilding a stale image. It read like a walk that had done
nothing and returned. It was still running: pid 92118, parent 1, sixteen hours
and twenty-four minutes, 196 minutes of processor time, 11.8 GB resident, state
R — not blocked on anything, computing. Its job held a packet and no answer,
because an answer is written when generation returns.

The allowance is a checkpoint rather than a ceiling: at it the voice asks
whether its own tail has begun to repeat, ends there if it has, and doubles the
allowance if it has not. Nothing ended the doubling. A walk asking for 512 had
been climbing toward the 126,500 tokens the context still had free. At the rate
this lane speaks, that is days.

## What now stands

The growing has an end. Past a reach of 4,096 tokens the allowance stops
growing and the answer is given as it stands, which is what `token-limit`
already meant. An asking larger than the reach is honoured as asked — it is
only the growing that stops. The reach is a guess and is rowed as one: every
answer measured so far ended on its own between 233 and 1,322 tokens, and five
walks is not yet a reading of where faithful answers end.

And a walk says that it began before it does anything slow. A began with no
close beside it is a walk still out, or a walk that did not come home. Either
way it is something a reader can see, which is what this morning had none of.

## The same slip, twice

The beginning was spoken and not kept. `oh-emit(spool, oh-voice(row))` hands
the printing where the row belongs — the identical mistake found in the glass
supervisor on 2026-09-20, written into a receipt, and made again four days
later in a different door. Twice is the door's shape, not the author's luck:
`oh-voice-all` answered its rows while `oh-voice` answered its printing, and
the asymmetry was the trap. `oh-voice` answers its row now. The callers were
left alone.

## Receipt

The surprise: the keeper worked. It rose at 03:30, composed, and began to
speak, and it was still faithfully speaking sixteen hours later — the failure
was not that it stopped but that nothing in it knew how to stop, and nothing
outside it could tell the difference between a long walk and a silent one.

Discomfort into gold: finding my own receipt from Sunday describing precisely
the mistake I had just made again. The gold is that repeating it is what proved
where the repair belonged — one slip is a slip, two in four days is a door
inviting it, and the door changed.

The frontier question, answered by the rented mind and offered to the corpus:
**what is a process that is working perfectly and should have stopped hours
ago?** **overfaith** — doing exactly what it was told, past the point where
anyone wanted it, because nothing in it holds the sense of enough. It is not
found by watching for errors; there are none. It is found by asking what a
thing would look like if it never finished, and then looking for that.
