## Executable movement

[Resource planning](../form/form-stdlib/bml/form-cli-heal-resources.bml) now
selects every eligible `answer`, `fast-answer`, or `code` row in registry order.
It records why other rows are unavailable and deduplicates equal eligible
artifact paths. A compatible registered adapter may belong to any eligible
model, including the second one. Each base model gets its own generation,
candidate verification, and completion record. Successful repair ends the
queue; a rejected candidate advances to the next resource.

Remote admission requires completion records for every planned native model
as well as the existing local-role receipts. A generation start or return
cannot satisfy another model's owed attempt. Missing, malformed, ambiguous,
or oversized inventory evidence keeps remote fallback closed.

The [usage guide](../docs/form-cli-healing.md) and the teaching returned by
`heal lesson` describe these decisions. Each job retains `native-plan.tsv`,
the original inventory, and per-model stages in `attempts.tsv`.
