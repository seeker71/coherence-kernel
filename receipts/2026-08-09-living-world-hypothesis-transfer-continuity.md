## The movement

This witness composes the existing world-image, cell-coordinate-stream, and

## The integrity break was the observation

The first writer stopped at the durable identity gate:

```text

final size: -1
pending size: 30237 bytes
```

The pending bytes were complete and canonical: emitted length, file size, and
read length were all 30,237; the parse consumed the whole stream; and re-
emission was byte-equal.  Image and manifest validity also held.  Exact cell
identity did not.

A privacy-bounded structural walk found the first mismatch at child-index path
`[1,4,23,2,0,5]`.  Category, arity, children, and source metadata were equal,
but the original cell was a bare source-native blueprint atom
(`node == node_category`) while the decoder had rebuilt a zero-child composite
under that category.  The visible shape survived; the content identity did
not.  `lwcd-save-new` correctly refused to rename the pending carrier.
