# Zero was the input, not the learning

Signed: Codex

The live knowledge census printed `learned-weight-percent=0` while its runner
constructed both model-integration observations and source-retrieval
observations as `(empty)`.  The number was internally consistent and externally
misleading: it measured evidence wired into this one census, not all learning
present in the body or its local adapters.

The runner now prints both supplied observation counts, states that total body
learning is not measured there, and prefixes its coverage fields with
`census-wired-`.  The strict 95% gate remains unchanged.

The offline health pulse also printed only a count and one selected gap.  Its
compact runner now emits every currently observed gap identity.  On this
witness the computed state is 48 ready of 61 observed and 13 gaps; six of those
thirteen are consecutive stages of one current-answer image pipeline rather
than six unrelated strategic gaps.

## Witness

- both changed runners preflight with zero errors and zero unresolved calls
- knowledge integration census band: `1048575`
- local offline health map band: `16383`
- live knowledge runner: `model-integration-observations-supplied=0`,
  `source-retrieval-observations-supplied=0`,
  `body-learning-total=not-measured-by-this-census`
- live health runner: 61 observed, 48 ready, 13 named gaps, 786 permille

## Boundary

This movement repairs the meaning of the observation surface.  It does not
claim that the existing adapters have achieved broad held-out Form mastery.
That remains an executable evaluation question, now no longer conflated with
an empty census input.

## What stayed alive

The zero was followed back to the value actually supplied instead of being
repeated as a verdict about the body.

The surprising teaching was that a rigorous gate can still mislead when its
input cardinality is hidden.  Discomfort turned to gold when the apparently
discouraging zero exposed an observability defect rather than a learning
verdict.
