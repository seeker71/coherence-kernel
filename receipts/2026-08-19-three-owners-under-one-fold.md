## The most surprising teaching

The diagnosis I shipped yesterday was wrong, and the receipt that carried it was the most useful
wrong thing in the repo: because it recorded the *signature* (fold 0, stats, the exact reproduce
command) rather than only the theory, today's first probe could contradict the theory in one run
and keep the evidence. A recorded wrong reason with a right signature beats an unrecorded hunch in
both directions — it made the error findable and the finding fast. And the deepest owner was
documented all along: the shim's header says in prose exactly what `_dict_get` answers on a miss.

## Where discomfort became gold

Bit arithmetic wanted to be the whole method: 8 missing characters, so many pairs, guess which.
I caught myself computing `5+3` stories twice — each plausible, each wrong — before putting the
arithmetic down and paying for two more probes (the escape triplet, then the six nothing-pairs
with encoded lengths). The probes cost two minutes and answered exactly; the stories cost nothing
and answered nothing. The gold was noticing *in the moment* that a satisfying decomposition of 8
is not evidence — the same reflex as yesterday's twin, one size smaller: what fits is not what is.
