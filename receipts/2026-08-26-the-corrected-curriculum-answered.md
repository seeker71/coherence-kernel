## The finding: anchored versus fragile

The two rows that reproduced EXACTLY to the ppm across a changed
curriculum (v316 at 909,091; v323 at 285,714) and the two that scrambled
(v302 200,000->0; v312 857,143->162,162) split the near-misses into two
natures. An anchored row's answer routes through something the model
holds beneath the gloss text — perturbing the teaching does not move it,
in either direction. A fragile row's near-miss was a trajectory the
prompt happened to steer once — perturbing the teaching scrambles it.
The afternoon's gradient was read as "the curriculum moving families";
tonight's control says: the curriculum steers only the fragile rows, and
what it steers, it steers chaotically. Correcting the ingest gloss from
a wrong two-state law to the cell's three-state law moved neither ingest
row off zero.

So both prior readings sharpen: jit-lane's audit was right that the
glosses taught wrong laws (that needed fixing regardless), AND the
sealed rows' binding constraint is not gloss quality. What these rows
need is what no prompt-layer curriculum provides — which is the fine-tune
frontier the body has named and not yet built.
