## Surprise, and where the discomfort went

Most of the fourth arm's differences were not kernels disagreeing. Nine were rows nobody moved when
their bands grew, four were a directory read from the wrong root, and several were a name that
reached a file only through its callers. Where the kernels were asked the same question, they mostly
agreed; the manifest, the paths and the preludes had drifted. The one place a kernel truly parts is
small and exact: fkwu lets a global function's name win over a parameter the closure holds.

The discomfort was in my own first heals and in the tool I trusted to judge them. My channel-query
heal routed its names through the shared table and would have crashed Go in every consumer; a
before-and-after comparison caught it. My directory heal read right to me and failed in the witness,
because a cache directory nobody tracks made form-stdlib look inhabited from the root. And
validate.sh read a warm image compiled with errors as clean. The gold: ask for the file you need,
not for a directory that lists something; compare against HEAD with fresh caches before believing a
heal; and let the count hear every word fkwu says about a compile.
