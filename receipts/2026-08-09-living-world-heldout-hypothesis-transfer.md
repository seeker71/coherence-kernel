# A held-out Form hypothesis reached reusable native voice

Date: 2026-08-09

## The movement

The living organ already had three separate capacities:

1. offered operators and recurrent examples could grow plural executable
   Recipe hypotheses;
2. two independently admitted held-out placements could differentiate a
   compatible hypothesis from a contradicted alternative without deleting it;
3. an independently re-offered prospective application could execute the
   compatible Recipe on new bindings.

Those capacities ended at a numeric execution cell.  The result could be
inspected, but it could not become proposed next content for a new transition
context and could not reach the native continuation voice.

`living-world-hypothesis-transfer.fk` adds that missing membrane:

```text
current differentiated tissue
  -> independently re-offered application on identity-new bindings
  -> exact Form Recipe execution
  -> recurrent execution/context transfer offer
  -> unanimous transfer-plan acknowledgment
  -> inert transition observation
  -> independent transition re-offer and acknowledgment
  -> exact next-content prediction
  -> already learned content surface
  -> native continuation trace
```

No stage writes through the next stage.  The transfer candidate can only
describe an ordinary transition observation.  It cannot install that
observation or speak.  The transition and the content surface each retain
their own sources and their own currentness.

## What remained open

The production bridge embeds no operator, task, class, output, surface,
ranking, timeout, default, or remote route.  Its only production categories
are interface codecs for one offered relation and one admitted plan.

An offered relation retains its exact execution, target context, optional
lens, four-arm acknowledgment, alternatives, optional unknowable remainder,
evidence, and local path.  `ONE` must carry the exact execution output before a
transition may be described.  `ZERO`, immediate `NOTHING`, an explicit
alternative, and other `NODE` acknowledgments remain valid observations but
produce no transition.  Their existence is not coerced into failure or a
default answer.

Recurrence is counted by distinct active source identity, not by duplicate
attribution count.  Every recurrent holder governs the unanimous proposal.

## Identity-clean held-out placement

The witness reused the already admitted hypothesis tissue without changing its
evidence:

- training examples: 1;
- generated hypotheses consistent on training: 2;
- independently admitted discrimination placements: 2;
- compatible after both placements: 1;
- contradicted after both placements: 1;
- prospective application bindings: 1, distinct from training and both
  placement identities;
- transfer target contexts: 1, absent from training and placement evidence.

The compatible Recipe produced the predeclared held-out output.  Before the
transfer relation and transition membranes crossed, the target context had no
current transition.  After both crossed, its prediction contained one current
derivation whose exact next cell was the execution output.  A surface already
learned for that content then produced a one-turn native trace.  The target
context/output relation itself had never been a training example or placement.

This is structural transfer and evidence arrival.  It is not a broad natural-
language quality score: the corpus contains one training example, two
discrimination placements, and one prospective application.  The result says
the mechanism generalized on this bounded identity-new placement; it does not
claim population-level model improvement.

## Authority did not leak

After voice became current, one transfer holder revoked its offer.  The
transfer candidate and its successor became stale, but the independently
re-offered transition remained current and the trace reconstructed exactly.
The transfer plan did not own later evidence.

One transition holder then revoked its own support.  The transition candidate
became stale, a new prediction acknowledged immediate `NOTHING`, and a new
trace contained zero turns.  The learned content symbol remained current.  A
relation disappeared only at the evidence boundary that supported it.

## The compilation break was evidence

The first full-source attempt exposed a pre-existing mutual-recursion seam in
the continuation walker:

```text
fkwu:7770:14: error: [unresolved-call] 'lwsg-walk' matched no
op/rewrite/fn/binding -- typo or missing prelude?
```

The body still computed the expected numeric verdict, but the command exited
1.  That verdict was refused as inadmissible.

A first repair folded the recursive cycle into one deeply nested Form.  It
removed the unresolved call but made compilation pathologically slow.  At five
minutes:

- direct declared-source size: 398,437 bytes;
- generated/open source artifact: none; the combined unit was held in memory;
- process footprint: 35.8 MB;
- direct stack: `fk_run_src -> fk_src_compile_current_unit ->
  fk_prescan_form -> fk_skip_balanced`;
- runtime framebuffer: unavailable because execution had not begun.

That child was interrupted.  The settled repair is a bounded Form trampoline:
non-recursive stages return an explicit content-addressed `CONTINUE` cell and
only `lwsg-walk` calls itself.  This removed both the forward-reference seam
and the giant nested form.  The same full witness then compiled and executed in
2.04 seconds before the final source-coordinate correction, and 2.98 seconds
after it.  The existing three-turn continuation band remained bit-identical.

## Honest next edge

The organ can now transfer one learned structural relation into one held-out
transition and reuse a learned content surface.  The surface bytes still came
from participant offers, and the hypothesis grammar currently composes only
binary operator Recipes from offered tokens and observed role names.

The next quality-bearing edge is an identity-declared prospective suite with
several unseen applications and multiple offered operator species.  Candidate,
dataset, split, and evaluation-contract identities must remain fixed across
the comparison.  New admitted examples may grow or differentiate candidates;
an unchanged suite must be called verification-only.  Plural outputs,
contradictions, silence, alternatives, and unknowable placements must remain
first-class rather than becoming a forced accuracy denominator.
