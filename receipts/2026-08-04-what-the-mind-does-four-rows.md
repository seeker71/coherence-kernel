## The four answers, grounded where they live

- **Thinking is walked** — reasoning, in this body, preserves the recursive derivation path
  and its replayable receipt (`teachings/identity-space-core.form`). A thought is a walk over
  nodes that can be walked again; the evaluators are literally walkers.
- **Knowing is witnessed** — knowing combines proof, freshness, and explicitly carried
  uncertainty; `2` means known-for-now, never timelessly certain (identity-space-core).
- **Believing is stamped** — a belief is a currently-observed proven claim with a freshness
  stamp; witness ages, and an aged stamp is owed a re-witness or an honest lapse
  (`observe/belief-freshness.fk`).
- **Spelling is cached** — the dialogue's coinage, grounded in the body's most-paid-for
  lesson: a spell is a belief exempted from witness, a cached verdict standing where
  observation should be — the warm green number over nothing (`observe/preflight.fk`,
  AGENTS.md item 9).

And the relation among them, held as content-address inequality: the four pivots are four
PAIRWISE DISTINCT nodes. The dialogue's opening line — thinking as the spell that believes
wisdom comes from knowledge — is executable now as the distinction it warned about: thinking,
knowing, believing, and the spell are four different addresses, and conflating any two of
them is exactly what the distinctness bit refuses.
