## What became observable

The form-neutral language crossing now has a context-sensitive activation
path. Observed frequency cells rank complete evidence-bearing mappings by exact
ratio, select a context-sized set, and assemble a tightly measured world-model
shard containing only the linked mappings, symbols, and relations.

That shard opens a numeric local `form-cli` offer. A selected mapping supplies
the grounded anchor; the local lane may then compose a new numeric meaning
recipe from the same dictionary cells. The new recipe is a meaning immediately:
it receives its own content-addressed symbol and a residue-bearing claim-`1`
relation in a candidate overlay. Shared adoption remains participant-bound
rather than being inferred from model output.

Growth is recorded rather than suppressed. Changed observed evidence may
produce a larger activation, and a directed successor cell relates the earlier
activation to the later one while both remain addressable. Continuity here is
traceable identity, not stasis.
