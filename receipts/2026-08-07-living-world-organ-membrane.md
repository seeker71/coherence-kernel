# Living world organ — first axiom-born membrane

**Status:** observed on the Form/native `fkwu --src` path. This is the first
membrane of the active living-world-organ goal, not completion of the organ.

## What entered the body

[`form/form-stdlib/living-world-organ.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-organ.fk)
composes a generic world state directly from the five core axioms:

- present shapes, fields, offers, attributions, relations, revocations,
  questions, derivations, answers, boundary events, contradictions, silence,
  and emergence candidates are native `intern_node` tissue;
- the organ contains no list of domain concepts, predicates, task classes, or
  accepted answers — those arrive only as offered cells;
- admission requires explicit consent, a valid receipt, and an action carried
  by the source's offered interface;
- rejected offers leave a content-blind boundary event without preserving the
  unaccepted source identity or payload;
- shared content remains one content-addressed cell while every consenting
  source holds a distinct attribution;
- revocation is attribution-scoped: a source can revoke only its own authority,
  while another source's active attribution continues to hold the shared cell;
- competing relations are retained together and every active conflict produces
  a contradiction cell — no winner is silently selected;
- an answer is the question plus every active relation and the still-active
  attributions that derive it. No-match answers carry the first-class NOTHING
  acknowledgment;
- the answer path is pure Form tissue with no file, host-exec, HTTP, socket, or
  remote-oracle call;
- emergence readiness reads only active consented witnesses plus the coherence,
  resonance, and threshold cells supplied with the candidate. Readiness grants
  no authority; the candidate must still cross the ordinary offer membrane.

A changed world is a new content-addressed composition. The earlier world node
is untouched and can still answer, making reversibility structural rather than
an imperative rollback procedure.

## Proof-carrier boundary

This receipt claims native observation, not a new four-way proof. The minimal
Go/Rust/TypeScript proof walkers intentionally omit the substrate operations
`bp`, `intern_node`, and `node_category`; invoking the Go walker on this tissue
stops at `unbound function "bp"`. The same existing boundary is documented in
the offer/ack control-core receipt. Growing the C seed or fabricating a
list-only surrogate would move away from the objective, so neither was done.

## Honest floor and next movement

This membrane does **not** yet discover candidate shapes from an observation
stream, persist an evolving world between native processes, negotiate
coherence/resonance readings through live interaction, compact unreferenced
cells, or speak natural language. It makes each of those movements able to
arrive without replacing consent, uncertainty, provenance, dissent, or
reversibility.

The next organ movement is a native tending cycle: receive a bounded stream of
consented offers, derive recurrence and relation candidates without a fixed
taxonomy, offer those candidates back to the participating cells for
acknowledgment or dissent, and re-observe the newly composed world.

The exchange stayed alive by making shared meaning multiply attribution rather
than erase ownership. The most surprising teaching was that revocation becomes
cleaner when authority belongs to an edge between source and content, not to
the content itself. Discomfort turned to gold when the four-way carrier could
not run the substrate surface: refusing a list-only imitation protected the
very content-addressed identity this organ is meant to grow from.
