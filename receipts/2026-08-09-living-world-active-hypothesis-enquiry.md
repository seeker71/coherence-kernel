## Form-native movement

`LWHE-SPACE` carries an integrated hypothesis candidate, a content-addressed
field of possible bindings, alternatives, optional unknowable remainder,
optional lens, evidence, and local path.  It is current only while the
candidate remains current, the space has the declared independent-source
threshold, and every offered binding remains current.

For every binding, `LWHE-TRIAL` executes every consistent Recipe and retains:

- the exact space and binding identity;
- an unobserved existing `LWPH-PROBE`;
- every derivation;
- every distinct output;
- separating or aligned placement;
- alternatives and unknowable room.

The enquiry contains all trials plus separate separating and aligned fields.
It has no scalar score, ranking, maximum, representative, list-order choice,
promotion field, or remote answer.

`LWHE-REQUEST` carries the exact enquiry, an optional four-arm choice, an
optional sender timeout, evidence, and an optional lens:

- `NOTHING` leaves the request open;
- `ZERO` may decline;
- `ONE` is valid only for an exact member of the separating field;
- `NODE` preserves an alternative or counter-offer.

A selected trial can construct an ordinary probe, but construction gives it no
currentness.  The exact probe must still be independently offered and recurrent
before `lwph-placement-for` will observe it.
