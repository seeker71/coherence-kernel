# Hot integer power in BML; honest JIT panel

## Observation

The repaired pensive glass named its actual resident as PID `4961`, then
reported the hottest evaluator recipe as `fq-pow-int` with `171670580` calls
across `44` hot recipes. Its `crystallized=0` field was not an observation: it
was a literal in the panel source. A fixed zero cannot say whether native-page
birth happened, so it obscured the very signal the panel was meant to carry.

`fq-pow-int` feeds the Metal binding byte packer. Its old recurrence decremented
the exponent one at a time. The new `integer-power.bml` owns the generic exact
integer rule: zero is one; an even exponent halves and squares; an odd exponent
multiplies once then continues; a negative integer exponent is `nothing`, not
an invented integer or zero. `metal-door.fk` retains its established
`fq-pow-int` entry while delegating to that BML rule, so callers acquire the
better shape without a function table or a special runtime seat.

The panel now says `native-pages=nothing` until an actual native-page birth
receipt reaches it. Heat remains a real discovery signal, not a claim of JIT
completion.

## Next grounded gap

The evaluator's heat file is a discovery channel, not yet a generic
source-to-native dispatcher for arbitrary hot Form functions. The next direct
crossing is a Form-owned lowering/admission hook that accepts a discovered
source definition with its caller-born meaning context, proves it lowerable,
and emits the native-page birth receipt that the panel can count. It must not
turn a hot name alone into authority to compile or execute arbitrary source.
