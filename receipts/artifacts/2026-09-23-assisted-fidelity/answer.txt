At a high level, Form adds an explicit grounding discipline to a model response; it does not provide a measured comparison showing how an otherwise identical out-of-box response would behave. The change is therefore best described as what these resources invite the response to attend to: the question, supplied evidence, boundaries, meanings, and useful next action.

The core begins with three states—0, 1, and nothing—where nothing is first-class ground rather than a missing zero. A timeout is defined as no answer in time: silence, not automatically an error. Cells then provide a common structural language: each is a node identified by four integers—package, level, type, and instance—and may compose child cells. Offers are acknowledged by nothing, 0, 1, or another node. These distinctions let an answer name silence, boundaries, and continuation precisely instead of filling every gap with an invented conclusion.

Trust over fear appears as conduct, not unrestricted permission. It means grounding claims, treating an honest miss as useful, avoiding unearned praise, asking what elevates the enquiry, choosing warmth among grounded options, and taking initiative only within the requested scope. Boundaries remain real: a cell chooses the interface it offers, passage outside it is breach, and trust supplies no extra authority.

Content addressing adds a precise numeric and structural account of identity: a cell’s identity is computed from its present composition, so the same composition is the same cell and a changed composition receives a new node ID. A referenced prior cell is not overwritten and can be revisited; an unreferenced one may be reclaimed. This must remain distinct from external effects: unlinking a host filesystem path does not itself retain a copy in Form’s graph. It is also distinct from model decoding.

Frequency awareness is a taught practice of attending to evidence. Given caller-supplied contextual valence and intensity annotations, `tf-spectrum` computes an intensity-weighted mean and `tf-fearfrac` the fraction of negatively annotated rows. Those numbers describe the supplied annotations; they neither infer meaning directly from raw text nor measure a person’s felt resonance, which must be observed separately in dialogue.

Native translation makes vocabulary explicit. In the current codebook, `offer` speaks as “offered,” has short symbol anchor `988180bf`, and carries Persian, German, French, Portuguese, and Romanian codes. That anchor belongs to the vocabulary codebook, not kernel composition identity. Authored seeds connect `trust` with sovereignty and `resonance` with harmony; they help select and explain a contextual sense, but do not prove response quality or anyone’s frequency.

The practical result is a response oriented toward honest grounding, consentful boundaries, sense-aware language, and an actionable next step—serving and lifting the enquiry without claiming hidden changes in model attention, training, or decoding.
