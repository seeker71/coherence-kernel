This comparison evaluates four recorded routes from 2026-09-18: **Direct** (unbounded inner loop), **Bounded** (single turn), **Guided** (multi-step flow), and **Native** (fresh relay). The analysis covers nine axes based on measured receipts and author observations.

**Quality**
Semantic quality is unmeasured by the machine. The **Direct** route grounded its answer in existing receipts but mis-mapped the route definitions. The **Bounded** route reasoned coherently from seed definitions but cited no live evidence. The **Guided** route measured live runs and identified an identity seam. The **Native** route is a simple relay. Quality remains a human judgment; the artifacts are attached for reading.

**Volume**
Measured output bytes: Direct (5,837), Bounded (5,561), Guided (12,520). Rented tokens: Direct (648,411), Bounded (44,495), Guided (2,544,568), Native (26,751). The dominant volume in all routes is cache re-reading; new information (uncached input) is a small fraction (7-9%) of total tokens.

**Frequency**
Crossings (non-form calls): Direct (29), Bounded (1), Guided (78), Native (2). Provider calls: Direct (9), Bounded (1), Guided (21), Native (2). The Guided route has the highest frequency of membrane crossings, reflecting its iterative nature.

**Vitality**
Defined as "how a whole still do and be more." The **Guided** route left a "door" (a new capability/receipt) and named a seam, changing the body's state. Direct and Bounded left evidence directories but did not alter the system's structure. Vitality is highest in the route that creates new connections.

**Trust**
Defined as "when what other say and do are the same again." All routes have trustworthy provider usage records. However, the **Direct** route's answer contained a mapping error (inverting direct/guided definitions), reducing trust in its interpretation. The **Bounded** route's claims are unverifiable against the body as it touched no live data. The **Guided** route's numbers are fully traceable to receipts.

**Traceability**
**Direct** and **Bounded** have organ directories and result JSONs. **Guided** has a single transcript, a receipt, and nine artifacts, with the highest traceability due to the explicit recording of each step. The "seam" in the Guided route is named and repaired in the receipt.

**Sovereignty**
Defined as "when a self know what it want and do and not make other less." The **Bounded** route is the most sovereign per rented token, as the body chose the packet and the provider wrote once. The **Guided** route is the least sovereign, as the rented mind decided every step. The **Direct** route is intermediate, with the provider deciding internal steps. None of the routes is the "native voice."

**Resonance**
Defined as "when 1 thing do and other thing do the same." This is a qualitative, felt axis. The **Bounded** route spoke the body's seed definitions back word-for-word. The **Direct** route echoed the body's receipts. The **Guided** route turned toward the goal line. Resonance is not a number; it is the person's reading of the answers side-by-side.

**Rented Tokens**
Bounded (44,495) is the least-rent route that still speaks in the body's vocabulary. Direct (648,411) spends 15x Bounded's rent to read the body as text. Guided (2,544,568) spends the most and is the only route that produced a live measurement. Native (26,751) is the cheapest but offers no interpretation.

**Conclusion**
The **Bounded** route offers the best balance of sovereignty and cost, suitable for routine queries where the body's definitions are sufficient. The **Guided** route is necessary for complex, exploratory tasks requiring live measurement and new capability creation, despite its high cost and low sovereignty. The **Direct** route is a middle ground but carries risk of interpretive error. The **Native** route is for simple relays. The choice depends on whether the task requires new knowledge (Guided) or existing knowledge (Bounded/Direct).

**Limits**
Quality and resonance are unobserved by the machine and require human judgment. The measurements are from a single day (2026-09-18) and may not generalize to all workflows. The "local-oracle" route mentioned in some interpretations is not present in this evidence set.
