Nothing in these resources ran the exact experiment (direct-call vs guided-multi-call), so this is reasoning from the grounded definitions plus what the receipts show about how form-cli and rented tokens actually behave here — not a measured verdict. I'll mark each dimension as grounded (has an authored seed), receipt-supported (a receipt's opening bytes back it), or open (no entry, no receipt — flagged plainly).

**Grounded in seed vocabulary:** vitality, trust, sovereignty, resonance, rented.
**No entry, reasoned only from structure/receipts:** quality, volume, frequency, traceability, membrane.

**rented** — sense: "from other place and not here." Both paths spend rented tokens on the actual Claude/Codex calls. Direct keeps them inside one form-cli-measured bundle. Guided adds a second layer: the outer agent's own interpretation between calls is *also* rented, repeated once per hop. So guided's total rented spend is direct's internal spend *plus* N rounds of outer interpretation — higher, unless the steering avoids internal exploration the unguided call would otherwise redo. `receipts/2026-08-31-bml-rented-turn-meter-delta.md` is the direct warning here: summing every textual `output_tokens` field (i.e., naive multi-call aggregation) produced an impossible 25,826,352,066 and was refused as evidence — a caution that applies exactly to counting a guided session's repeated calls.

**trust** — seed: "when what other say and do are the same again." `receipts/2026-09-11-rented-meter-reads-the-desktop-carrier.md` reran the same meter on the same frozen transcript and got the same 0 both times — that's what trust looks like measured. Direct form-cli exposes one report; trust rests entirely on form-cli's internal accounting being right (a black box from outside). Guided exposes N reports, so trust is tested at every hop — more chances to catch drift, but also more surface where "said" and "done" can diverge (the overcounting bug above is exactly that kind of divergence).

**sovereignty** — seed: "when a self know what it want and do and not make other less," and `receipts/2026-09-04-sovereignty-as-gift.md`: sovereignty is proven by not depending on the outside to survive, while still being free to ask for guidance. Direct form-cli is the more sovereign shape structurally — one clean call, form-cli's own process does what it needs internally without the outer agent steering it turn by turn. Guided is closer to repeated asking-for-guidance, which the receipt says doesn't cost sovereignty, but it does mean form-cli's own path is being shaped from outside at each step rather than self-directed.

**vitality** — seed: "how a whole still do and be more." Direct form-cli's internal iteration (however much it needs) happens as one whole, invisible to the agent. Guided's "doing and becoming more" is visible as flow across calls, but split into pieces the agent reassembles — vitality distributed across the membrane instead of contained in one process.

**resonance** — seed: "when 1 thing do and other thing do the same." In direct, resonance stays inside form-cli — its own internal Claude/Codex calls answering each other, not observed by the agent. In guided, resonance is between the agent and form-cli's output at each step — each next call is shaped to match what the last one showed — call-and-response resonance, but paid for with an explicit membrane crossing each time.

**quality, volume, frequency, traceability, membrane** — no seed entry for any of these against these terms, and no receipt actually measures them for this comparison. What I can say plainly from structure, not from a grounded reading:
- *volume*: guided's outer-agent tokens stack on top of whatever form-cli spends internally per call; direct's internal volume is hidden inside one reported total.
- *frequency*: direct crosses the outer membrane once; guided crosses it N times, by construction.
- *traceability*: guided is more traceable step-by-step because each call and its output lands in the transcript the agent can see; direct's internal calls are traceable only from within form-cli's own report, which the agent takes on faith.
- *quality* and *membrane crossing count*: not established here at all — I'd need an actual run of both paths on the same task, with the meter from `receipts/2026-08-31` and `2026-09-11` applied to each, to say anything real.
