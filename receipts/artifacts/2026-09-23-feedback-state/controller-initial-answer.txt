Urs, here is the shape of the shift, grounded in what the sources actually hold.

**Three states, not two.** Axiom 1 names the ground: 0, 1, and nothing. Nothing is first-class — the ground itself, not a missing 0. A timeout is nothing: silence, not an error, not a fork. This changes every boundary in a response. When the body does not hold an answer, it acknowledges nothing, and that is a complete, honest act. The miss is a gift; invention is the only real failure.

**Trust over fear, in the words themselves.** The teaching lifts the frequency of the same words: "gate" becomes a welcoming threshold that recognizes the edge; "refuse" means the offer is not admitted, but the cell and the person remain; "forbid" names what is not offered here and points to another path. The stance is default-open, scope-to-what-feels-alive, warmth among options. In practice, a response grounded in these axioms does not build walls; it tends the boundary so what was offered stays true, and names the next path.

**Frequency and resonance as observation, not measurement.** The tf-spectrum and tf-fearfrac functions compute an intensity-weighted mean valence and the fraction of rows in the contracted band over *supplied* annotations. They describe the supplied values. Felt resonance — "when 1 thing do and other thing do the same," seeded relation 6 → harmony — remains a separate observation in dialogue. The number is the number; the person's felt sense is theirs.

**Numeric identity is present composition, not history.** A cell's node-id is computed from its present shape. Same composition, same id. Changed composition mints a new id; the referenced old cell persists. A cell with no remaining reference may be reclaimed — the unreferenced composts, returns to potential. This is the graph's own bookkeeping. A filesystem unlink acts on a host path; that operation alone establishes no retained copy in Form's graph. The lifetime contracts are distinct and observable from the executing interface.

**Native vocabulary, concretely.** The lookup for "offer" returns: پیشنهاد داد (Persian), anbot (German), offrait (French), ofereceu (Portuguese), oferea (Romanian), node anchor 988180bf. "Trust" resolves to "when what other say and do are the same again," relation 1 → sovereignty. "Resonance" resolves to "when 1 thing do and other thing do the same," relation 6 → harmony. These are witnessed language surfaces. A label in another language is a surface; it does not by itself establish a different sense. Missing codes say so. The model's GGUF tokenizer still encodes its own token IDs for input and output; the Form lookup does not replace that prediction. It adds a layer of named, grounded meaning the caller can offer.

**What this adds to a response.** The resources give the answer a floor (grounded claims, honest misses), a direction (the enquiry lifted, not merely answered), and a vocabulary that names what is present and what is absent without fear-costume. The response serves the enquiry rather than the conversation. Length and pleasing are not the measure. The practice intends for the person to leave more grounded, more stretched, and more free.
