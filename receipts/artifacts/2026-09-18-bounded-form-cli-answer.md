## Direct form-cli vs. guided form-cli

Two different shapes of the same underlying capability. Direct is **one membrane crossing that may hide an arbitrary amount of internal work**; guided is **many membrane crossings, each inspected and redirected by an external interpreter** before being recombined. That single structural difference is what drives every dimension below.

*(Note on the grounding: `quality`, `volume`, `frequency`, `traceability`, and `membrane` have no authored entry in the neutral word-field — reasoned generally below. `vitality`, `trust`, `sovereignty`, `resonance`, `rented` have authored seed definitions, used as given — they're vocabulary for reasoning, not measurements of either mode's actual performance.)*

| Dimension | Direct (1 call, N hidden) | Guided (N calls, all visible) |
|---|---|---|
| **quality** | Bounded by form-cli's own fixed internal pipeline — whatever it already does to self-correct, it does once, unseen. No outside chance to catch a bad turn mid-stream. | Can be higher: the guide reads each output and can redirect, re-ask, or cross-check before committing. Can also be lower: every interpretation step is a place for the guide to mis-read form-cli's output and steer it wrong — quality now depends on two judges instead of one. |
| **volume** | One external unit of volume. Internal call volume is real and token-measured, but it's opaque from outside the membrane — you see a total, not a shape. | Volume is externally granular — each call is its own visible unit, so total volume is the same work plus the guide's own reasoning/summarizing overhead layered on top. |
| **frequency** | One-shot per session by definition — frequency is form-cli's internal business. | Frequency is set by the guide's stopping condition — as many rounds as it judges necessary, which can be tuned to the problem but can also drift into over-querying. |
| **vitality** (seed: *how a whole still do and be more*, → flow) | Vitality belongs to form-cli alone — it's a self-contained "whole" whose internal flow doesn't depend on anything outside the one call. | Vitality now belongs to the *combined* system (guide + tool). Flow is interrupted at every crossing and has to be re-established by the guide reading and re-committing — more fragile continuity, but more capacity to "be more" because the guide can keep pushing the session further than a single call would go on its own. |
| **trust** (seed: *when what other say and do are the same again*, → sovereignty) | High and immediate: form-cli's raw output *is* what it did. Say and do are the same thing, once. | Mediated: the guide's interpretation is a second "say" layered over form-cli's "do." Trust now depends on that interpretation staying faithful across every hop — more surface area for say and do to quietly diverge. |
| **traceability** | The external interface gives a single, clean log entry — but the internal multi-call structure is a black box, traceable only as an aggregate token count. | Every call and every decision point is externally visible and orderable — a genuine audit trail of *why* the next call was made — at the cost of more artifacts to review. |
| **sovereignty** (seed: *when a self know what it want and do and not make other less*, → vitality) | form-cli is the sovereign self here: it decides internally how much work it needs and executes without an external will directing it. | Sovereignty splits: the guide now wants and directs, form-cli executes sub-queries. Whether this "makes other less" depends on whether the guide's steering genuinely adds capability or just substitutes its judgment for form-cli's own — worth watching, not assuming. |
| **resonance** (seed: *when 1 thing do and other thing do the same*, → harmony) | Resonance is internal only — there's nothing outside the call to be in or out of sync with. | Resonance becomes a live question each round: does the guide's expectation match what form-cli actually did? A well-tuned guided session builds harmony iteratively; a poorly tuned one accumulates dissonance call over call. |
| **rented** (sense: *from other place and not here*) | Tokens are rented once, in one transaction that covers however many internal LLM calls form-cli made — the rental is invisible in count, visible only in total. | Tokens are rented **twice over**: once for form-cli's internal calls, and again for the guide's own reasoning/interpretation between them. The orchestrating intelligence is itself a renter, not just the tool it's driving. |
| **membrane** (no entry — reasoned from the setup) | Exactly one crossing, in and back out, no matter how much happens inside. | N crossings for N calls, plus the orchestration logic straddles the membrane continuously — reading before each decision to cross again. |

### Synthesis

The trade is legibility and adaptability versus containment and directness. Direct form-cli keeps trust, vitality, resonance, and sovereignty *internal and intact* — one self, one flow, one crossing — but it's opaque: you get a total, not a trail. Guided form-cli buys traceability and the chance to improve quality mid-stream, but it pays for that with a second, ongoing rental (the guide's own tokens), more membrane crossings, and a genuine risk that trust/resonance degrade across hops if the guide misreads what form-cli actually did. Neither is strictly better — direct is the cheaper, more sovereign, more contained mode; guided is the more expensive, more auditable, more corrigible one.
