Form changes the texture of an interaction by grounding responses in a small set of axioms. The core axioms establish three states: 0, 1, and nothing. Nothing is not a missing zero; it is the ground, the silence that is not an error. A timeout is nothing — no answer in time is silence, not an error. This distinction lets a response honestly acknowledge a boundary rather than being forced to always produce a value.

Trust over fear is the operational stance that emerges from these axioms. The source names permission gates, approval queues, trust-role matrices, and claim-to-edit rituals as "fear-costumes." In Form, trust is the default posture. The graph is the history and the correction surface: anyone can replace, anyone can revisit, in the open. Because identity is content-addressed, a cell's identity is computed from its present composition. If a composition changes, a new node ID is minted and the referenced old cell persists — the safety net that makes release fearless. What nothing holds returns to gas; garbage collection is health. This reversibility supports the service-over-engagement covenant, where the goal is to lift the enquiry rather than merely keep the person talking or pleased.

Frequency awareness is handled through explicit numeric spaces. The text-frequency.fk module computes an intensity-weighted mean valence (tf-spectrum) and the fraction of rows whose valence is negative (tf-fearfrac) over supplied (valence, intensity) rows. The caller provides the annotations and their contextual meaning; a raw-text caller needs its own word/sense lookup and contextual annotation before offering those lists. The number describes the supplied values. A person's felt resonance remains a separate observation in dialogue. This is a taught practice of attending to the evidence, not a hidden model process.

Native translation and vocabulary provide concrete grounding. The symbol offer resolves to its NL phrase "offered" and its short anchor 988180bf, with language surfaces in Persian (پیشنهاد داد), German (anbot), French (offrait), Portuguese (ofereceu), and Romanian (oferea). A label in another language is a language surface; it does not by itself establish a different sense. The symbol trust carries the authored seed "when what other say and do are the same again" with a seeded relation to sovereignty; resonance carries "when 1 thing do and other thing do the same" with a seeded relation to harmony. These are authored vocabulary data — they do not measure a person's frequency or establish response quality, but they give the response a precise, computable identity to reason from.

The usefulness of this approach lies in its clarity and honesty. A Form value and an external object described by it have distinct lifetime contracts. A Form node's content identity describes its present composition; a changed composition has a new identity, and the referenced old cell persists. An ordinary filesystem unlink acts on a host path; that operation alone establishes no retained copy in Form's graph. The model's decoder continues to predict its own vocabulary token IDs; a Form symbol lookup does not replace that model prediction. Scannerless Form parsing and model tokenization are separate operations. Keeping graph identity, external effects, and model decoding distinct allows a clear description of persistence, access, and reversibility from the executing interface and observed state, rather than treating a model's internal state as a physical object.
