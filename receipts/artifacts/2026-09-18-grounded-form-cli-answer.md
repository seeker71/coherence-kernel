Direct comparison, term by term, using only what the enrichment and receipts hand me. Where the seed field returned `nothing`, I say so and reason from receipts alone.

**vitality** — seed: "how a whole still do and be more" → flow. Direct form-cli is one whole call; whatever happens inside (however many Claude/Codex calls) stays inside that one act. `receipts/2026-08-26-resident-cursor-and-membrane-homecoming.md` shows the same shape at a different layer: judgment kept resident instead of launching a child `./fkwu`, and "every used membrane spoke" — staying whole preserved vitality rather than fragmenting it. Guided sessions fragment the whole into N calls with an interpreter stitching them; vitality there depends on whether the stitching still "does and is more" than the parts, which nothing in these receipts measures directly.

**trust** — seed: "when what other say and do are the same again" → sovereignty. `receipts/2026-08-31-bml-rented-turn-meter-delta.md` is the load-bearing case: the old meter *said* 25,826,352,066 tokens and that was not what happened — refused, not retained, replaced by a caller-bound delta. That failure mode is a risk at every membrane crossing, so a guided session with N crossings has N places for said-vs-done to drift; a direct session has one.

**sovereignty** — seed: "when a self know what it want and do and not make other less" → vitality. `receipts/2026-09-04-sovereignty-as-gift.md`: sovereignty is proven by not relying on the external world to survive, but it explicitly allows asking for "reflections, guidance, mirroring, care, support, gifts." By that standard, a guided session's repeated interpret-then-call loop is not automatically a sovereignty violation — it's closer to what that receipt calls asking for guidance. But it is a dependency a direct single call doesn't carry: the direct call's result doesn't wait on an outside read of its own prior output.

**resonance** — seed: "when 1 thing do and other thing do the same" → harmony. This is structurally what a guided session is built to produce: each call's output is read and the next call is shaped to match it. A single direct call has no second thing to resonate with inside the session; resonance, if any, would be between the raw output and the enquiry, not between session steps.

**rented** — seed: "from other place and not here" (carrier-label). `receipts/2026-09-01-rented-session-local-form-ratio.md` establishes that a rented session can reach local form-cli "at zero remote tokens." In the direct case, any Claude/Codex calls form-cli makes internally are rented tokens, but they're bundled under one outer crossing. In the guided case, the same internal rented calls happen per form-cli invocation, *plus* the guiding agent spends its own rented tokens interpreting each intermediate output — that interpretation cost sits outside form-cli's own measured boundary entirely.

**membrane** — no seed entry (`nothing`), but this is the most heavily attested term in the receipts (`2026-09-04-cell-channel-membrane-on-glass.md`, `2026-09-03-zg-enters-form-cli-without-membrane.md`, `2026-08-26-resident-cursor-and-membrane-homecoming.md`, `2026-08-24-qwen-tokenizer-bounded-compatibility-membrane.md`). Direct: one outer membrane crossing; guided: N outer crossings, one per call, each a separate point where a host-exec/child-process style boundary receipt would apply if this pattern followed the resident-cursor precedent.
