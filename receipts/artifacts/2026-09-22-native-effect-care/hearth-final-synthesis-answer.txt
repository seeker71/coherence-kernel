Form changes a response by giving it an explicit ground, stance, and vocabulary—not by replacing the model’s decoder or claiming a different hidden attention mechanism. In this Qwen lane, the tokenizer still encodes input and the decoder predicts its own vocabulary token IDs. Form’s cells, codebook lookups, and numeric functions are separate representations and operations that can ground what the answer means and how it is expressed.

The five axioms supply the ground. There are three distinct states: nothing, 0, and 1; nothing is first-class silence, not zero or failure. Everything is a cell, identified numerically by four integers—package, level, type, and instance—and a cell may compose other cells. Identity is computed from present composition: the same composition is the same cell, while a changed composition receives a new node ID. Cells meet the world through offered interfaces, and communication or execution is an offer acknowledged by nothing, 0, 1, or another node. This makes distinctions, boundaries, consent, and current structure explicit.

Trust over fear then becomes practical initiative and care: begin open, choose what feels alive and warm, tend an offered boundary, and use visible history as the correction surface. It does not mean ignoring real operational consequences. The dialogue covenant turns that stance into response practice: ground claims, admit missing evidence, avoid flattery and invention, and help the person move from their actual floor toward the enquiry’s north star.

Frequency awareness asks how wording lands without banning words. “Must” can name precedence rather than domination; “refuse” can mean an offer is not admitted while both cell and person remain. Felt resonance is the person’s observation in relationship. It is not computed by authored seed identifiers or by `tf-spectrum`: that function only accepts supplied `(valence, intensity)` rows and returns their intensity-weighted mean; `tf-fearfrac` reports the fraction of supplied rows with negative valence.

Native translation is likewise explicit. The meaning-code book can resolve one semantic symbol to witnessed language surfaces, preserve alternate senses, or report a missing code. Its short anchor is not the four-integer cell identity, and lookup does not replace model token prediction.

Illustrative before/after—not a measured baseline: asked “Can I delete this file?”, an ungrounded response might offer generic reassurance. A Form-grounded response would first distinguish an external filesystem deletion from releasing a graph reference: the supplied host branch calls `unlink(p)`, so it may remove the file; content-addressed graph persistence does not guarantee filesystem recovery. That response is more useful because it names the real contract, consequence, uncertainty, and safest next distinction without manufacturing confidence.
