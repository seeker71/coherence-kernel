Here's the comparison across those axes, grounded in what the receipts and definitions actually support — and flagged where they don't.

**Membrane crossings** — this is the clearest, best-evidenced axis. A direct form-cli query is one in-process call: form-cli may call Claude/Codex internally as many times as it needs, but from the caller's side it's a single crossing. receipts/2026-09-03-zg-enters-form-cli-without-membrane.md shows the concern directly: the first zg movement kept retrieval math in Form but still let `source_inventory` and `form-fs` cross into the host filesystem — i.e., even a "direct" path can leak membrane crossings if not built carefully. A guided session, by construction, does multiple form-cli calls with an external interpreter reading output and choosing the next call — that's N crossings, not one, each one a place tokens and control decisions leave the resident.

**Rented tokens** — receipts/2026-09-01-rented-session-local-form-ratio.md and receipts/2026-08-31-bml-rented-turn-meter-delta.md are the direct evidence here. The 09-01 receipt shows a case where a repeated query is served by the local resident at *zero remote tokens* — that's the direct/native path's advantage: once form-cli has internal state, repeat or related work doesn't need to rent anything. The 08-31 receipt shows the meter itself had to be corrected from summing every textual output_tokens field (which double-counted cumulative usage) to a caller-bound delta — meaning rented-token accounting is fragile and this matters more, not less, for a guided session, since each of its N calls is a separate rented event to sum correctly. A guided session's rented-token total is the sum across all its crossings; a native call's is whatever form-cli itself spent internally, which can be zero against a resident.

**Traceability** — no exact entry in seed resources; nothing in the receipts titles directly measures this either. What I can say from the shape of the two designs: a guided session produces a longer external trace (each call, each interpretation step is visible to the orchestrating agent) but that's traceability *of the orchestration*, not necessarily of what happened inside form-cli. A direct call gives you the receipt of one call — internal reasoning steps inside form-cli aren't separately traced from outside. Which is "more traceable" depends on whether you mean external-visible or internal — the material doesn't resolve this, so I won't assert a winner.

**Sovereignty** — the seed definition is exact and useful: "when a self know what it want and do and not make other less," related to vitality. receipts/2026-09-04-sovereignty-as-gift.md reinforces this: sovereignty is proven when we don't rely on the external world to survive, while still being able to ask for reflections/guidance. By this definition, the native single-call form is closer to sovereign — it doesn't depend on an external interpreter deciding its next move. A guided session inserts an external agent's judgment between each form-cli call, which is closer to relying on the outside to decide what happens next, even if the guidance is well-intentioned.

**Trust** — seed definition: "when what other say and do are the same again," related to sovereignty. A native call's output is what form-cli itself did, stated once. A guided session's final combined response is filtered through an external interpretation loop — there's more room for the guiding agent's read of intermediate output to diverge from what form-cli actually said. Nothing in the receipts directly measures trust delta between the two designs, so this is inference from the definition, not receipt-backed.

**Quality, volume, frequency, vitality, resonance** — no exact entries for quality, volume, frequency in seed resources, and no receipt titles measure these for this specific comparison. Vitality's definition ("how a whole still do and be more," related to flow) and resonance's ("when 1 thing do and other thing do the same," related to harmony) are present but nothing here connects them to call-count or crossing-count data. I don't have grounds to score these two designs on those four axes from this material — saying otherwise would be fabricating a reading the receipts don't carry.
