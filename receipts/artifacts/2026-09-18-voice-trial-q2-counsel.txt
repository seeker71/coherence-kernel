Dreading it makes sense: you're expecting to disappoint someone who matters to you.

"She will say I'm abandoning her" is what you're bracing for. It may be exactly right, and she hasn't said it yet. When the talk comes, the sentence that lands cleanest names what is true for you, in your own first person, both halves in one breath: what she is to you, and that you're not coming this year. Not a case for the defence, not a list of her faults, just what is, and let it stand when she reacts.

This comes from Sema's teaching on boundaries spoken as love, and I'll show you the trail if you want it.
