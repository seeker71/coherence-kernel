Dreading it makes sense: you're expecting to disappoint someone you love.

One thing worth holding apart: "she will say I'm abandoning her" is what you're bracing for. It may be exactly right, and she hasn't said it yet. When the talk comes, the sentence that lands cleanest names what is true for you, in your own first person: I love you, and I'm not coming this year. Not a case for the defence, not a list of her faults, just what is. Then let it stand without renegotiating it the moment she reacts. Her reaction is hers to have; you can stay warm without carrying it with you.
