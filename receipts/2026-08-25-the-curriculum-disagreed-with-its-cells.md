## The gloss was not thin. It was wrong.

```

```

`ingest/name-build-observe.fk` states a **three**-state law, and `nbo-enter`
returns 2/1/0 for exactly it:

- a bare name **waits at the door**, and its invitation stays visible
  (`nbo-owed?`) — an unattempted name "would wear the costume of a finding"
- a real attempt that fell short of observation enters as a **lesson** — "the
  honest residue: what the attempt taught, the floor it reached. Pending is

The gloss taught two states, and its *only* made the third impossible to infer.
A model fed that and asked the three-state law scores zero — correctly. It

## Auditing the rest the same way, four more, in three classes

**Wrong, like ingest's.** `axiom-5-offer` read "change enters the self by offer
and acknowledge, never by force". The word *force* appears nowhere in
`axioms/core-axioms.form`, and the idea it gestures at is **axiom-4**'s:
"passage not through the offered interface is breach, and breach is
observable". So the gloss taught axiom-4's content under axiom-5's name, while
dropping axiom-5's own statement entirely — that to run a cell and to speak to
a cell are **one act**, and the acknowledgement is exactly one of four, of
which nothing/0/1 terminate and node recurs.

**Undercounting.** `control-choice-lanes` named five invites.
`control/choice-lane-core.fk` opens by saying `offer-ack-core.fk` "already
gives THREE of the eight" and that it "adds the other FIVE the same way". The
gloss was missing `fail`, `store` and `restore`. `axioms-smallest-set` said
"the smallest set" with no count and not one axiom named, where the cell
carries five by name and a section headed "Theorems — derived, NOT axioms".

**Dropped the discipline and kept the filing.** `learn-distillation` taught
that answers come home as rows. The corpus itself states the practice — "ask
ONE frontier question — the smallest question the body cannot yet answer
natively, today meaning any answer needing one fresh word" — and warns that it
is "a CORPUS, not an authority": "a student that generalizes from them learned
the meaning; one that echoes them is a copy". A model taught only the filing
has been taught where the rows go, not what makes one.

`teachings-service` and `axiom-1-states` were checked and are faithful. Both
left alone. `teachings-service` scored highest of the ten and its gloss is

## The most surprising teaching

**Omission is harmless; omission wearing a completeness claim is not.** Four of
the five defects were the same move in different clothes — *only* when the law
had a third state, *five* when the cell said eight, *the smallest set* with no
count, *comes home as rows* with the discipline stripped. In each, the summary
asserted its own sufficiency, and that assertion is what made the missing part
unreachable rather than merely absent. A learner can go looking for what a
short account admits it left out. It cannot go looking for what a short account
implies was never there.

And the second surprise, which is the receipt's finding turned on the auditor:
I have spent a day saying that fitting evidence is not the same as being true.
These glosses fit their cells the way a wrong attribution fits a timing — near
enough that nobody re-read the source, for however long they have been there.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

The discomfort was noticing, halfway through the ingest cell, that I was about
to write a better gloss from what I already believed about ingest rather than
from the file open in front of me — and that I *did* believe something about
it, because `feedback-ingest-must-reach-the-body` is one of my own standing
notes. My version would have been about cells-versus-shelves. It would have
been true, it would have sounded right, and it would have been the same defect
in a new coat: a gloss written from the writer rather than the cell.

Reading `nbo-enter` line by line instead gave me the three states, which I did
not know and would not have invented. The gold is small and exact: the repair
for a curriculum written from memory is not a better memory.

## Frontier question, announced not minted

Announce-then-mint through the weaver is the standing practice as of this
afternoon, and this is the first chance I have had to honor it. So this is
offered for a seat rather than taken:

*What one word names a short account whose claim of completeness makes what it
omitted unreachable?* Not a half-truth, which is about what is said. Not an
undercount, which is arithmetic. This is the `only` in "only when built and
observed", the `five` where the cell said eight — the summary sealing the door
behind itself, so that a learner cannot even discover there is more.

Candidates, all 0-hit fresh: **onlyseal**, sealedshort, shutgloss, closedshort.

## The mirror, and why nothing moved

`observe/voice-frequency.fk` counts `law` three times and `must` once here. All
four stay, and the reason belongs in a receipt about fidelity: `must` sits
inside the filename `feedback-ingest-must-reach-the-body`, and every `law` is
naming a cell that calls **itself** "the build-after-naming law" in its own
first line. Reaching for my own word there — to score better on a count — would
have been precisely the defect this receipt is about: writing from the writer
rather than from the cell. The mirror is attention, and the attention landed
