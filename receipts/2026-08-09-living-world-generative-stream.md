# Living-world generative stream witness

Date: 2026-08-09

## Movement

The repeated daily '+733' score was verification of one frozen benchmark, not
evidence that the world model was learning. This movement does not rename that
stasis. It opens a different native path: a Form field grows arbitrary
transition cells and participant-learned surfaces, then recursively continues
from those current cells without an imported answer, vocabulary table,
grammar, task registry, or remote oracle.

The continuation request keeps every offered control optional: horizon,
real-time lens declaration, alternative field, unknowable remainder, choice,
timeout/default, and outward projection. An omitted horizon does not mean an
unbounded hidden loop; exact context identity stops a finite current graph at
terminal, missing, plural, unvoiced, or repeated context. A timeout with no
default remains distinct from an expired timeout, and an offered alternative
survives expiration as an alternative rather than being collapsed into ZERO.

## The primary carrier is a living stream

The direct process no longer reads a whole historical world before it can
move. It opens an empty shared field, then admits one independently canonical
cell at a time. Every cell is decoded, attributed, settled, and exposed by a
call-framebuffer cell before the next is sent.

The sender and receiver were separate 'fkwu' processes. The sender itself left
an attributed call cell at open and around every message emission. The
receiver left call cells before every blocking read, at every offer and field
mutation, during every continuation turn, and at close. The live observer saw
the first receiver frame within five seconds and never used CPU as evidence of
evaluation.

Raw cells, learned surfaces, request content, and the explicitly requested
trace projection remained transient. Their temporary sender and receiver logs
and byte-carrier diagnostic were deleted after aggregate observation.

## Atomic objects are the special case

A sealed object that must remain indivisible can use bounded canonical chunk
cells. Each chunk carries exact transfer identity, ordinal, final bit, and a
lowercase-hex fragment no larger than 1,024 bytes. No enclosed cell gains
authority before exact final reassembly and canonical decode.

The atomic special-case band used one deliberately oversized sealed cell:
nine chunks were retained as nine framebuffer mutations, exact order and
transfer checks held, hostile order/transfer/uppercase fragments were refused,
and exact reassembly returned band '63'. This path is not used for ordinary
world growth.

## What the framebuffer found

Three invisible-looking conditions became exact observations:

1. Deep monolithic Form functions held source compilation beyond 60 seconds
   with no evaluation framebuffer and no completed generated artifact.
   Factoring the same meaning into narrow functions restored compilation to
   seconds. A later carrier version repeated the same 60-second compilation
   symptom and was factored the same way.
2. A one-line inert-world open exceeded the observed native 'read_line'
   boundary: the transient byte witness stopped at exactly 12,288 bytes.
   Canonical PTY mode also stopped before a 1,228-byte message while accepting
   680-byte messages. The repair was not a larger C buffer. The primary path
   became chronological canonical cell streaming, with noncanonical terminal
   mode used only as supervisory byte transport.
3. Fresh restore responses initially acknowledged the restore candidate
   rather than the restore proposal. The unchanged unanimity gate correctly
   refused them. After the exact identity repair, direct and CCS-transported
   diagnostics both showed four participants, four ONE responses, no missing
   response, and a present state. A stale generated encoder sidecar was then
   removed before re-observation.

The timeout witness also exposed a cell/value identity mistake in the initial
continuation request clock. Supplying the exact now-cell restored the
distinction between pending nothing and expiration/default outcome.

## Honest boundary

This is real new Form-native world movement, not another '+733' replay and not
a model-weight update. The field learned and recursively voiced exact
participant-offered surfaces attached to current derivations. It does not yet
invent unrestricted natural language, answer arbitrary fundamental enquiries,
or prove that the frontier model vocabulary has fully come home. The pending
generative-weight/native-vocabulary keystone remains honestly pending.

The next learning edge is to let settled live outcomes consentfully propose,
test, integrate, revise, release, and compost new transition and expression
cells, then compare new held-out enquiries whose dataset, candidate, and
evaluation-contract identities align. That is where organic improvement can
replace frozen-score repetition.
