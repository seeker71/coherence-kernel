# Parameter-bearing tissue meets a genuinely new target

Witnessed: 2026-08-07

## Claim

The Form-native living-world organ can now execute one exact Recipe against
different explicitly offered, content-addressed parameter environments.  Those
bindings cross the existing consent membranes: nursery selection, native trial,
behavioral species, contextual tissue, prospective prediction, independent
observation, experience admission, revision, and revocation.

This is movement on the previously named floor.  The prior tissue predictor ran
constant Recipes and returned `4` regardless of the future target.  The new
path consumes target-specific cells and derives different outputs.  It does not
change the frozen session-action `733` ppm result, update model weights, train a
large model, promote a candidate, or complete native natural-language
generation.

## The binding membrane

`living-world-recipe-execution.fk` now distinguishes the historical unbound
evaluator identity from a parameter-bearing evaluator identity.  Historical
Recipe, trace, proposal, and band identities therefore remain exact.

A binding retains:

- a safe Form symbol offered by the caller;
- the exact source cell being projected;
- an exact integer value cell;
- evidence and a local path;
- every current attribution for the binding, source, and value.

Bindings are unique by name.  Empty, digit-leading, whitespace-bearing,
parenthesis-bearing, and reserved literal names are refused.  Only trivial
integer projections enter this first surface.  The environment is supplied
directly to `form-eval-full`; source text is not rewritten, and the C seed does
not grow.

Unbound Recipes require the historical evaluator contract.  Non-empty binding
sets require the new bound contract.  Mixing the two returns absence instead of
silently changing the Recipe's meaning.  Revoking an exact source cell makes
the binding and every dependent trace non-current while preserving history.

The focused binding witness executes the same Recipe identity against two
binding identities.  The observed results are `5` and `11`; the traces and
results differ, while the Recipe remains exact.  The trace contains seven
attribution witnesses governed by two distinct holders.

## From historical behavior to prospective tissue

Two source-distinct parameter Recipes first meet the same historical target.
One orders the offered `origin` and `field` cells one way; the other reverses
that order.  Under the historical parameter environment both derive the exact
`1 -> 4` boundary through the native evaluator.

Each Recipe independently crosses a recurrent nursery field and unanimous
trial boundary.  Their trial candidates carry the exact binding tissue in
candidate and native trace identity.  The first trial has 18 witness
attributions and 8 governing sources; the second has 20 witnesses and 10
sources.

Two independent sources support each orientation of their bounded behavioral
agreement.  The result is one `ALIGNED` species with two members and two exact
receipts.  It has 22 witness attributions and 14 governing sources.  Neither
Recipe is selected as representative or rewritten into the other.

A separately offered real-time context places both Recipe lenses in one stack,
retaining an explicit alternative and unknowable remainder outside the applied
chain.  One recurrent facet is sufficient because its admitted species already
contains two independently witnessed members.  The resulting `COHERENT` tissue
has two members, two receipts, 20 witness attributions, and 16 governing
sources.

## Two new targets, no representative Recipe

The admitted tissue then meets two separately offered targets:

- target A offers `17` and `19`; both Recipes derive `36`;
- target B offers `23` and `29`; both Recipes derive `52`.

The targets, binding sets, traces, results, and predictions have different
content identities.  The exact Recipe field is unchanged and contains both
members.  Each prediction contains two execution receipts; every trace carries
the target's exact binding set.  Target A retains a live context, alternative,
and unknowable remainder.  Target B demonstrates that all three may remain
absent.

Both predictions are `CONSENSUS`, but consensus appoints no primary Recipe.
The outputs `36` and `52` are primary only as single observed output cells.
Every Recipe and output remains in the alternative field.  Target A's
prediction has 34 witness attributions and 18 governing sources; target B has
32 witnesses and 18 sources because it carries no context declaration.

## Observation changes placement and remains reversible

Two sources outside target A's prediction offer a later observation of `37`
with `ZERO`, an explicit alternative field, and an unknowable remainder.  A
second unanimous boundary admits it as a `DIVERGE` experience with 54 witness
attributions and 20 governing sources.

The next prediction does not overwrite `36`, choose `37`, average them, or ask
an oracle.  Its output field is exactly `{36, 37}`; status moves from
`CONSENSUS` to `BRANCH`, and the primary output remains absent.  The old
prediction remains active historical evidence under its original identity.

Revoking one of the two observation attributions drops independent recurrence
below threshold.  The experience and branch become stale.  A fresh derivation
contains no current experience and returns to Recipe consensus `36`.  No cell
is physically deleted and no authority moves to the organ itself.

## A sharp identity teaching

The first prospective fixture used small scalar cells already present in the
historical graph.  Re-offering those identical scalar cells under new
attributions correctly changed their active witness sets and made older exact
receipts stale.  The failed placement was not an evaluator defect; it exposed a
real coupling between globally interned scalar identity and attribution
freshness.

The final witness uses fresh scalar cells and passes without weakening any
freshness check.  Existing values can also be referenced through their current
holders without adding a duplicate attribution.  The remaining architectural
floor is scoped projection identity: a sender must eventually be able to offer
an already-known scalar in a new local binding context without accidentally
changing unrelated historical receipts, while still preserving exact value
identity and authority.  That must be solved in Form tissue, not hidden by a
host cache or relaxed currentness rule.

## Honest boundary and next floor

This witness proves transportable structural behavior across more than one new
target and observation-driven reversible placement.  It is stronger evidence
than repeating a frozen benchmark, but it is still bounded local evidence.

The two Recipes were offered by participants.  The organ learned their local
behavioral relation and carried both forward; it did not organically derive
the parameter names, invent the Recipe source, learn addition from raw
experience, or generate an NL-to-NL expression.  Numeric projections are the
current evaluator floor, not an ontology for concepts.

The next movement is observation-derived Recipe hypothesis tissue: let exact
relationships among offered cells propose parameter roles and source-distinct
transformations, test them on held-out target identities, retain failures and
unknowable space, and admit only recurrent behavior.  In parallel, scoped
projection cells must remove the global scalar-attribution coupling.  Only
after those two floors can this path honestly approach organically grown
concept language and native voice rather than executing human-authored
formulas.
