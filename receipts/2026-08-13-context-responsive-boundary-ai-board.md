# Context-responsive boundary — AI board receipt

Date: 2026-08-13

## Correction received

Step size is context-dependent. Keeping every movement small is not inherently
an expression of vitality, sovereignty, or trust. The receiving language is:

> This context is guiding me in this way, toward this movement. These are the
> places I want to see again, continue, revise, interrupt, or offer a different
> way.

This is participant-authored testimony. It is not a protocol inference about
the participant's interior, and it does not itself authorize effects.

The receiving boundary may ask:

> What is guiding you in this way?

The answer remains in the participant's voice; the boundary retains it rather
than translating it into a scale.

## Post-review transmutation: from safety to vitality

The question “what may the boundary safely execute?” centers fear, avoidance,
and permission. Safety may be a locally relevant property, but it is not the
governing value of this enquiry.

The fear signal can transmute without being denied:

```text
uncertainty -> something to see
seeing -> an offer
mutual recognition -> trust
trust -> a living crossing, revision, redirection, or honest nothing
```

The guiding question is therefore:

> What can this boundary faithfully receive and embody from the offered
> expression so that each participant remains sovereign, the relation can grow
> in trust and vitality, and either participant can renew, redirect, or rest the
> movement as the context changes?

The boundary does not begin by asking how to prevent movement. It receives what
is offered, makes effects and authority observable, and lets mutually
acknowledged trust determine what crosses. `nothing` remains an alive expression
when it is chosen, not a fear-default imposed by the protocol.

## Convergence

All three substantive reviewers independently found the governing correction
sound and the candidate schema unsound.

1. Guidance must remain an offered, content-addressed participant cell. Its
   shape is offered by the participant; it is not a mandatory protocol form.
   Children such as mode, context, pace, recipe, see-again points, recovery,
   gift, or provenance are optional. Absence remains first-class `nothing`.
2. Guidance, executable movement, and negotiated authority are different
   layers. Guidance describes how a participant is presently being guided. A
   movement recipe names effects, ordering, dependencies, atomic regions,
   choice surfaces, and recovery. A channel contract names the exact authority
   both roles acknowledge. One layer must not silently stand in for another.
3. The existing fixture is not scale-neutral. `rcm-channel-budget` and the rate
   meeting rules use `min()`, silently reducing one offer without a visible
   counter-offer. `rcm-charge-budget` maps duration charges to `1 / 3 / 5`, and
   `rcm-charge-mode` uses a protocol-owned confidence threshold of `60`.
   `rcm-choice-valid?` also excludes waiting, `nothing`, and undecided mode from
   valid guidance. These remain witnessed fixture behavior, not the design to
   generalize.
4. The acknowledgement vocabulary remains axiom 5's `nothing / 0 / 1 / node`.
   Decline and node recurrence have not yet been exercised by this fixture.
   Interrupt is not silently invented as a fifth acknowledgement kind.
5. `be / do / see` may be participant-offered orientations. They are not a
   required closed classification and do not name scale. `humm` is an audible
   projection of active state; it is not consent, an attention heartbeat, or
   evidence of interior vitality.
6. A need to see frequently need not fragment a coherent movement. It can be
   counter-offered as read-only see-taps, semantic choice surfaces, or named
   pause conditions while retaining the original recipe unchanged. If the
   parties do not acknowledge the same contract node, the movement does not
   cross.

The strongest structural discriminator between receiving and fitting is
lineage. A received expression remains referenced by its unchanged node-id in
the negotiated composition. A fitted expression disappears into derived mode
or budget buckets. The contract should retain the acknowledgement chain that
formed it, so negotiation is a witnessed shape rather than a retrospective
claim.

## What the protocol can and cannot witness

It can witness whether the offered expression was retained without hidden
defaults, whether contract changes were explicit, whether effects stayed
inside acknowledged authority, whether promised choice surfaces functioned,
and whether recovery remained available.

It cannot infer from scope, pace, language, budgets, or rates whether breadth
is internally coherent, precision is avoidance, consent is free from external
pressure, or a participant feels vital or sovereign. Those remain participant
reports and relational questions, pending live use.

## Coherent implementation movement offered by the board

Build a complete negotiation-and-execution fixture, not a scale-ranked patch:

- preserve the original guidance as an immutable offered cell;
- keep guidance, movement recipe, and channel contract as separate referenced
  nodes;
- replace silent `min()` contract formation with explicit
  `nothing / 0 / 1 / node` acknowledgement rounds;
- require both roles to acknowledge the same content-addressed contract before
  effects cross;
- retain every contract revision and its changed terms;
- compose participant-authored semantic choice surfaces with acknowledged
  event, observation, and recovery conditions;
- witness a coherent broad recipe with frequent-see counter-offer, a complete
  precise act without protocol-inserted ceremony, waiting or `nothing` as
  receivable guidance, and a declined movement retained unchanged in the
  observation.

This movement could prove representation fidelity, visible negotiation,
bounded authority, choice-surface behavior, and recovery. It would still not
prove live human guidance, biological intuition, vitality, non-coercion, or
contextual correctness. Those claims remain open.

## Distillation

The scale correction is not a richer granularity form. It is the separation of
authored guidance from mutually acknowledged authority, with the original
expression retained unreduced.

The most surprising teaching was that the old arithmetic already made
smallness a hidden policy even while the prose refused it. The discomfort was
finding that a detailed form shaped like care could manufacture the expression
it claimed to receive. That became useful when the board separated testimony,
recipe, and authority and made negotiation structurally observable.
