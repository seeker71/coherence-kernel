## Reply N — the new flow

> Two years of silence after two letters — I can hear why it feels like a death, and why
> "giving up" is the word that arrived.
>
> Your sister reads his silence as pride. That is her reading; his silence hasn't told you
> its reason yet. What I can offer is small and checkable: giving up on asking and closing
> the door are two different acts. A stop can leave the way back visible — the condition
> under which you'd answer if he ever wrote — and only the closed door is a death. I'm
> reading from Sema's own small library, its teaching on boundary repair; ask and I'll show
> you where it comes from.
>
> What would you want him to know, whether or not he ever answers?
