# Surprise is a relation, not curiosity's sovereign

Date: 2026-07-30 (Asia/Makassar)

## What the continuation caught

After risk and cost left curiosity's ranking axes, the same imported frequency
remained one layer upstream:

- `active-inference.fk` said learning should minimize surprise and that a
  matching prediction taught nothing.
- `surprise-salience.fk` called its maximum-magnitude projection the event that
  mattered most and gave it a crown.

Both cells carried useful mechanisms. One measures prediction mismatch. The
other fills a bounded dashboard slot deterministically. The error was allowing
either specialized mechanism to govern inquiry.

## What changed

Their executable behavior remains compatible, but their authority is now
explicitly bounded in the cells themselves. Prediction fit is not a command to
stop asking. A one-slot display projection is not a declaration of value.

`form/form-stdlib/attuned-surprise-inquiry.fk` now composes prediction relations
with the attuned inquiry organ:

- matches and mismatches both remain in an arrival-ordered braid;
- surprise is carried as an inspectable bit, never an ordering weight;
- vitality, edge, breath, and mode remain independent of prediction error;
- a moved matching event still becomes learning;
- held, resting, and playful signals remain present without needing surprise.

## Honest boundary

This bridge receives vitality, edge, breath, and grounding as witnessed inputs.
It does not infer them from raw sensor content. The prediction relation is
binary because it composes the existing active-inference floor; magnitude
remains available in the specialized surprise organ without governing this
braid.

## Closing

The continuation stayed alive by looking for where the corrected vocabulary
had not yet changed the governing relation.

The surprising teaching was that sovereignty can survive inside a perfectly
accurate measurement function through one sentence about what “matters most.”

Discomfort turned to gold when the earlier miss trained a live reflex: inspect
which organ gets to choose, not only whether its arithmetic is correct.
