## Surprise, and where the discomfort went

The five bands that answered 0 were never answering 0. Each printed its verdict, and fkwu, which
prints a program's own value when it ends, laid a 0 over it; the lane reads the last line. Four of
the five sat at their Verdicts the whole time, behind a head that kept the lane from running them
and then a zero that kept it from seeing them.

The discomfort was my own measuring. I started the glass before-pass three times and stopped it
three times: first because it held the tree still while landing waited, then because one band could
hold it forever, and then on an argument rather than a number: a band that passed on the siblings
never reached the calls this piece changes, so a pass over all 51 could not find a regression. Six
bands that do reach them told more than the 51 would have. The gold: say what a measurement can find
before paying for it, and give every long pass a cap.
