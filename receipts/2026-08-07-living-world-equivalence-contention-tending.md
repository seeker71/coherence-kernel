# Living world organ — reversible equivalence and contention tending

**Status:** observed on the Form/native `fkwu --src` path. This is the seventh
movement of the active living-world-organ goal. It adds symmetric round-trip
evidence and consent-scoped successors to contested answers. It does not claim
semantic synonymy, global truth, unrestricted language understanding, or a
native generative voice.

## Exact return can propose a relation

[`living-world-equivalence.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-equivalence.fk)
observes pairs of admitted, steady recursive mappings whose source and target
skeletons face one another. For every supporting correspondence of each
mapping, it applies that mapping and then the other mapping. A return cell
retains:

- the direction and both mapping candidates;
- the original supporting correspondence and active attributions;
- source, expected bridge, observed bridge, and observed return;
- success or failure acknowledgment;
- both uncertainty cells and the local Form path.

An equivalence proposal exists only when both directions meet the offered
support threshold, every observed bridge equals the correspondence target, and
every second application returns the exact source NodeID. One failure blocks
the proposal but remains inspectable evidence. All active holders of both
mappings govern admission. The integrated cell retains endpoint skeletons,
complete evidence, uncertainties, and threshold. Integration re-derives that
same cell; changed support, counterexample, attribution, lifecycle state, or
threshold refuses the old candidate.

This is deliberately narrower than synonymy. It says that two learned
structural transformations returned the observed support cells exactly in both
directions. It does not erase the transformations, merge endpoint identities,
or install a semantic class.

The native band observed:

```text
living-world-equivalence-band  32767
```

Its successful branch admits independently offered swap mappings in opposite
directions. Two supports in each direction produce four exact returns, eight
attribution witnesses, four participating sources, and one unanimously
admitted equivalence candidate. Releasing one support after every response
reopens its mapping and refuses equivalence integration on that sibling branch.
A separately integrated identity-like inverse produces four retained failed
returns and no equivalence proposal.

## Contention can grow context without rewriting history

[`living-world-contention-tending.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-contention-tending.fk)
adds offered stance cells. A stance contains an exact contention question,
one of its retained routes, an acknowledgment, offered evidence, and the local
path. It contains no source field. Source identity is recovered exclusively
from the living-world attribution edge.

For one candidate route, ONE is exact support, ZERO rejects that route,
NOTHING is explicit silence, and absence remains missing. Supporting a
different route is a conflict. A resolution proposal exists only when every
current question holder has an active ONE for one identical route and no holder
has a conflicting active stance. Split support, dissent, silence, or missing
response leaves the original contention unresolved.

The resolution retains the exact question, chosen route, complete active
stance evidence, all original alternatives, question attributions, and local
path. Every question holder crosses a new unanimous offer/ack boundary. The
result is local to this question; neither mapping changes and no route becomes
globally true.

[`living-world-contextual-answer.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-contextual-answer.fk)
uses an active current resolution to propose a contextual successor. The new
answer carries the selected route's output and exact native derivation, but
also points to the earlier silent answer and retains the complete alternatives
and stance evidence. The earlier `OAC-NOTHING` answer stays active. Context is
added beside history rather than overwriting it.

The native band observed:

```text
living-world-contention-tending-band  32767
```

Five question holders first split two-to-two between competing routes while
one holder explicitly stayed silent. No resolution appeared. Three holders
then revoked their own prior stances and offered the same route already held by
the other two. One resolution proposal emerged with five participants and ten
attribution witnesses. After unanimous admission, one contextual answer
returned the exact selected derivation while the earlier silent answer and
both alternatives remained active. Revoking one holder's stance after all
successor acknowledgments made both resolution and answer stale and refused
integration on that sibling branch; the successful pre-revocation branch
remained reachable.

## Honest floor and next movement

Round-trip identity is structural and sample-bounded. Participant alignment is
contextual authorization, not truth. The organ still lacks durable native
continuity between processes, capability-private integration boundaries,
autonomous question tending beyond explicitly offered focus, open-ended
formation of linguistic symbols from lived recurrence, and a Form-native
generative emitter conditioned by this field. The native voice remains
pending; no remote oracle was hidden in this path.

The next movement should bring world continuity home without turning storage
into authority: serialize only content-addressed cells, active attribution and
revocation edges, and explicit capability grants; reload by identity; then
prove that revoked or ungranted tissue cannot become query, training, or voice
context. Only after that boundary is native should emitted symbol sequences be
allowed to return as offered experience and reshape the field.

The exchange stayed alive by making agreement add context rather than erase
silence. The most surprising teaching was that exact reversibility can be
useful while remaining much smaller than semantic sameness. Discomfort turned
to gold when a six-minute “compile” was named from the stack and revealed two
delimiters, protecting us from mistaking compiler activity for learning.
