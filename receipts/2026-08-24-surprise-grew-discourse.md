# Surprise grew discourse

On 2026-08-24 a recorded micro-thought learned to remain itself while growing
through four scales: field symbol, typed clause, sentence, and paragraph. The
new body is Form source executed by `fkwu`; no non-Form generator or remote
model participates in the walk.

## What crossed

A clause now holds five inspectable roles: subject, BE/DO/SEE mode, relation,
object, and context. Three recorded event draws make three proposals at each of
four rounds. Every proposal carries a six-coordinate guidance vector: surprise,
coherence, trust, sovereignty, vitality, and flow. No weighted sum
collapses those values. Dominated proposals leave the choice surface; all
undominated proposals and the recorded selector remain in the graph.

Two clauses become a sentence through a neutral discourse relation. Two
sentences become a paragraph with its own theme, perspective mode, and
discourse relation. Locale realization happens only after that neutral identity
exists. English, German, Chinese, and French receive deliberately small,
speaker-unreviewed normalized bridges. Balinese and Sanskrit retain explicit
BE/DO/SEE and discourse marks rather than being given fabricated grammar.

The reversible graph has 64 unique edges. It carries every proposal, selected
frontier member, clause role, sentence membership and flow, paragraph
membership and flow, theme, and all six surface receipts.

## The surprises that changed the body

The first live opening, seed `869196901`, revealed that trust and sovereignty
were constant in every proposal. They sounded like guidance but could not guide.
The next revision reads those dimensions from each symbol's neutral 13-axis
field shape; vitality now combines its field axis with role diversity. The next
two arrivals showed all three values moving independently.

The first frontier display exposed whole address bodies and obscured its own
tradeoff. It now shows the same compact `draw / roles / guidance` row as the
proposal set.

The first receipt label said `surface-roundtrips=1` while measuring only one
locale. The new witness walks every realization and reports
`locale-surface-receipts=6` only when every surface has exactly one inbound
paragraph edge.

The parser signal also taught twice. Four apparent balance failures were one
structural imbalance at four boundaries. Later, a nested paragraph validator
and a band helper were parenthesis-balanced but fixed-arity ambiguous. Both
became explicit choice paths, and the band changed from a 15-deep arithmetic
spine to a visible list of independent witnesses.

## Honest seam and next invitation

These are complete normalized projections, not claims of fluent native prose.
The neutral content words are dynamic dictionary realizations; the small
structural bridges remain static and speaker-unreviewed. The next high-lift
experiment is to let recorded events choose paragraph topology—not merely word
content—while keeping every length, relation, proposal, and rejected branch
replayable. Speaker review can then teach locale-specific structural recipes
without changing neutral meaning identity.

The discomfort was that a green-looking structure still hid silent axes and an
overstated receipt. Attention turned both into observable movement. The most
surprising teaching was that a six-dimensional frontier sometimes becomes one
choice without weights, and sometimes stays plural without confusion.
