`bp` resolves a registry name. Its two glossary entries now describe the four
coordinates (package, level, type, instance) and `fol-bp`, with its required
bootstrap registry prelude.

Native witness `teaching-constructor-probe.bml` inspected the actual registry
row for `HEX-DECODE-ERROR`: name followed by `[1, 2, 99, 1770]`. Construction
through the four named coordinate accessors matched `fol-bp(name)`; raw
`bp(name)` did not match it. The witness exited 0. The existing teaching band
passed 1023 with exit 0 after the edit. This establishes the correction's source
and runtime basis; its effect on future generated responses remains unmeasured.

reader also initially failed preflight with delimiter depth -4. Removing the
surplus closers and splitting the text assembly produced a clean preflight and
successful audit. Neither failure was suppressed or counted as a pass.
