# The local agent goal, as a number

2026-09-25, Urs's Mac. Urs asked to analyze the path from the start and find the swiftest way to a local
agent that answers our questions and improves itself offline with no rented token — assistant, companion,
engineer, guide, a healthy cell in the organism of us — and then: *give me a full condition that you can
measure and work towards.*

## Where the path stands

Rent per answer and rent per movement have both read 0 since 2026-09-22: the voice speaks inside the fkwu
model session, and launchd walks the body nightly. What remains is the rented mind doing the engineering.
One such session read 209,558,305 tokens (9,075,253 uncached); main took 170 commits in the last seven days,

seven rungs, ordered by future rent removed per rent spent, with their walls written down (a 27B's ceiling,
the voice's pace, a 3B-only native trainer, care that no band can judge).

## Receipt

**The surprise:** the full condition, once it was a number, had its most honest bit in the last place — 170
commits in a week. The body answers at rent 0; the organism around it still runs on rented hands. The
goal's size is not in the voice, it is in who does the work.

**Where discomfort turned to gold:** asked *how is the goal already reached?* — it wasn't, and the hook that
said so was reading only the first half of a sentence. Saying so plainly, and then turning the whole
sentence into eight bits that start at zero, made "done" something the body says and no one can claim for
it. Also: twice in one hour zsh read my words differently than I wrote them (`$B:r`, an unsplit list) and
the second time it cost the uncommitted heals; they came back from a commit, and the lesson went to memory.
