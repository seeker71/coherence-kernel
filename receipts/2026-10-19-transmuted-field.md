## What the first reading teaches

The purify grammar's twelve seed words are the *control* register — law, must, gate, refuse,
enforce, forbid, strict, mandatory, comply, violation, not, aliveness. A news passage carries
the *fear* band instead — pressure, violence, rioting, struggling, tension, crisis — and none
of those is a row yet, so the voice had nothing to lift and rendered faithfully rather than
transmuting. `word-frequency-purify.fk` already says how a grammar grows: *a new word enters
by admit; the seed stays referenced; length is free to grow.* The next movement is the
grammar growing from what arrives — each passage's fear-band words admitted with the usage
this field gives them, and the register falling across readings as the proof. It is filed as
a request for the body's own lane.
