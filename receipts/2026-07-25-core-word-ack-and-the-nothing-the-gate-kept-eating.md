## The ladder: boolean → fuzzy → the escape

`form/form-stdlib/truth-arrival.fk` + band → **11111111111111**, fourteen
readings, **PASS-4WAY**, registered so the fourth leg fires.

Three rungs, each the one below plus one thing. Boolean {0,1}: total and
timeless. Fuzzy [0,1]: the interval is new, the totality is not. Arrival,
[0,1] ∪ nothing indexed by time: truth becomes **partial** and **temporal**.

The care that mattered was refusing the tempting shortcut — 0.5 is *not* "I
don't know". It is a claim, as committed as 0 or 1. Fuzzy logic is total; it has
no way to decline. `ta-not-a-degree?` sweeps the interval and finds every mu
leaves `and(true, mu)` known while `and(true, nothing)` is not: no point on the
interval behaves like the absence.

The Kleene connectives are **derived, not tabled**, from one rule — *a result is
known exactly when it is the same for every way the absent value could resolve*.
Since min/max/complement are monotone, two probes decide it, so `ta-ext1` /
`ta-ext2` are written once and every connective inherits Kleene-correctness for
free.

**The escape, in one sentence:** some answers arrive before their inputs.
`and(false, never)` is known false, now, on an input that never comes. Boolean
and fuzzy both refuse to speak without a total valuation, so neither can express
it. And it is safe, by theorem rather than hope: the derived answer is the one
true of *every* resolution, so any actual future confirms it — truth never
un-resolves, walked as a time sweep.

## The most surprising teaching

**One claim, three different deaths.** Excluded middle is where the rungs pull
apart, and each kills it its own way:

| rung | `p or not p` | how it fails |
|---|---|---|
| boolean | 1 | holds |
| fuzzy | 0.5 | **by degree** — a weaker truth |
| arrival | nothing | **by absence** — no truth at all |

Fuzzy weakens it. Nothing withdraws it. Not the same failure, and the band
computes all three rather than narrating them.

And from the merge: **axiom-5 has no fuzzy arm.** Its terminal acks are exactly
axiom-1's three states, and 0.3 is not one of them — so a degree cannot cross a
membrane as a terminal ack at all. It has to travel as a NODE. That reads as a
limitation and is the protocol being right: a degree is composed (a scale, a
source, a reading), axiom-2 says composed things are cells, and forcing partial
truth to travel as a cell keeps it content-addressed instead of an opaque number
that lost its provenance in transit.

# Third arc: 64 words that define themselves, eight lanes, twelve layers

`form/form-stdlib/core-lexicon.fk` + band → **262143** (eighteen readings),
**PASS-4WAY**, registered.

Eight families of eight. Every word's defining sentence is written from this
vocabulary plus twelve named glue words, and the check is real because the
definitions are stored as **token lists** — the readable sentence is the join,
the tokens are what `cl-closed?` walks. 494 tokens, 0 outside.

**Two closures, both measured.** Closed under *definition* — no 65th word is
borrowed to explain the 64. Closed under *questioning* — no lane answers with
anything outside the 64. You cannot ask your way out of the vocabulary.

**The eight lanes, and the one that differs.** Exactly one of the eight can
answer with a terminal ack, and it is `whether`. Not a design choice — terminal
acks are axiom-1's states, axiom-1's states are truth values, and `whether` is
the only lane that asks for a truth. The other seven ask for a *thing*, things
are cells (axiom-2), so they answer with a node and the conversation continues.
Read the other way: **a conversation ends only when someone asks whether.**

**Twelve layers, all four behaviours walked:**

    why     kind -> very -> between -> never -> whether -> make -> self -> if -> nothing …
    which   nothing -> 0 -> 1 -> is -> not -> and -> or -> if -> nothing …   (period 8)
    what    kind -> thing -> thing …                                          (fixed at 2)
    whether kind -> 0                                                         (rests at 1)

## The most surprising teaching

**The why-regress ends in `nothing`, not in `1`.** Every one of the 64 words
bottoms out within 8 layers — checked over all 64 seeds, deepest exactly 8 — and
every chain rests in the *nothing* arm. A chain ending in 1 would be a
self-grounding claim, a floor that asserts itself. This body has none. Ask why
of a ground word and the honest answer is silence.

## Urs's law, made operational

> each node as response from core + query is a new symbol or a sequence of
> symbols which then implies time unless the combination is reversible then it
> is a core concept w/o condition or separation

Made checkable: a lane is reversible when it loses nothing — distinct words to
distinct answers, no terminals. Then the step is a renaming, walkable backwards,
imposing no order; and with no order there is no before and after for it to live
in. A lane that collapses two words onto one cannot be undone — and the
discarded distinction is exactly the **condition**, while the one-way direction
is exactly the **separation**.

Exactly one lane of eight is reversible (`which`). The collapse is measured, not
asserted: `which` keeps all 64 answers, `whether` keeps 2, `what` keeps 1 — the
most expensive question in the set and the least informative answer, which is
the same sentence said twice.

The law holds across all eight lanes as an equivalence: **reversible exactly
when every chain returns to where it started.** And `what` is why a weaker test
fails — it collapses onto `thing`, a fixed point that is still a NODE, so the
chain runs forever and yet nothing can be recovered. *Not-resting is not
reversibility. Returning is.* That correction came from the band refusing the
first formulation, not from thinking harder.
