## The most surprising teaching

**The body had already built both halves and never introduced them.** A live
adapter slot that loads on every decode (empty), and a gradient engine that
trains real weights (discarded) — installed by different hands, waiting for
the introduction neither could perform alone. The rented mind's role at this
rung was not to build; it was to notice, register the judge, and row its own
work as the first training trace.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

Writing `wm-first-trial`, the pull was to row the stone as it *should* have
gone — six clean steps, prediction met. The honest row had to carry 191: the
prediction failed first, the door's refusal was read as zeros, and the fix is

falsifier — the trial's own stumble became the gate that keeps every future
trial honest. A training corpus that erases its teacher's misses would teach
a student to erase its own.
