# Learned content carries its voice without erasing situated speech

Date: 2026-08-09

## Why the repeated `+733` was not learning

The daily session-world score is a frozen verification replay, not a model
that has been accumulating experience across days.  Its source fixes:

- eight authored action classes, including a catch-all class;
- an order-two count table and eight majority counts;
- 31,827 training episodes and 1,364 held-out episodes;
- one historical train/held-out split identity;
- a 10,000 ppm materiality floor.

The candidate is reconstructed from the same frozen training rows on every
run and evaluated against the same 1,364 held-out rows.  One additional correct
held-out prediction is `floor(1,000,000 / 1,364) = 733` ppm.  Repeating that
gain therefore reports the same candidate on the same benchmark.  It is
verification-only/no-new-evidence, not daily world-model growth.  The fixed
classes also collapse novel action vocabulary into the catch-all class, so
this route cannot be the ontology-open living organ.

## The native voice seam that moved

The transition-expression organ previously accepted a learned symbol only
when its referent was one exact transition derivation.  That preserved situated
meaning, but it made every learned surface inseparable from one context.  If
the exact same next content recurred through a different context and different
evidence, Form could not reuse the surface it had already learned.

The organ now admits two exact, non-ranked reference modes:

```text
learned symbol -> exact current derivation       (situated surface)
learned symbol -> exact derivation next content  (reusable content surface)
```

Both are ordinary consent-learned symbol candidates.  No word, vocabulary,
context, class, or preference was added to production code.  Exact content
identity is the only bridge.  When both surfaces apply, both remain present;
the organ does not select a winner or manufacture a default.

## What the witness established

The Form-native witness began with one current context containing two plural
next derivations.  A second, distinct context then independently admitted one
of those exact next cells through two sources and unanimous proposal consent.
Two other sources taught one opaque surface whose referent was the recurring
next cell.

The same learned-symbol candidate then voiced two different derivations in the
two different contexts.  A separately taught derivation-bound surface applied
only in the first context.  The first context retained both applicable
surfaces and its unvoiced other derivation; the second context retained only
the shared surface.  Optional alternatives and the unknowable remainder stayed
identity-equal to each prediction.

When one source revoked support for the shared symbol observation, its exact
evidence snapshot changed and the shared candidate became stale.  The situated
candidate remained current.  The first context therefore retained its local
surface while the second returned to explicit unvoiced `NOTHING`.  Revocation
did not leak across candidates or claim authority over the underlying
transition.

## Honest boundary and next edge

This is genuine evidence arrival and vocabulary reuse across an identity-new
context.  It is not a natural-language generator, a benchmark score change,
or a promotion claim.  Participants still offer the opaque surface.  The
organ has learned when that surface applies, not how to invent its bytes.

The next edge is to connect already admitted structural or behavioral
hypothesis execution to transition observations through the same independently
re-offered membrane.  A held-out context can then test whether a learned
relation generates a correct next content and reaches the reusable native
surface without having received that exact outcome.  Dataset, candidate, and
evaluation-contract identities must be fixed before naming quality movement.
