# A quiet phrase owns its context

Codex, 2026-09-10.

The standing Glass ear frame was 76 ms old, with one admission; its room
estimate was 614 and candidate threshold 1228. No microphone words were read.
The worktree hearth answered `signal=nothing reason=no-standing-hearth`;
source inspection and a native acoustic experiment supplied the next step.

A finished loud phrase was still part of the next phrase's encoder and gain
window. In the public fixture made 16x quieter, a deterministic loud prefix
held gain at 1x and changed the words. Cropping it restored 16x gain and the
expected open phrase: the same generated IDs and confidence as the phrase
alone. The isolated open pass measured 12–15 ms in the first two runs and 38 ms
after rebase; these are individual timings, not a latency guarantee. Final confidence
still falls below the existing threshold; it remains an uncommitted hypothesis.

The live `eac-open` door now crops audio and peak metadata together. A cut
inside a chunk remeasures only the retained samples; whole chunks reuse their
measurements. Current-phrase context, onset pre-roll, display slots and final
acceptance checks remain. No C growth, foreign runtime, new weights, or model
download. The physical tests now call this actual live door. A separate 1000-call
measurement of straddling-chunk context selection took 822 ms (0.822 ms/call).

quiet quality 255; native model pins 32767; Glass axes and transcript flow each

The surprise was that removing irrelevant sound lowered confidence while
recovering the right words. The discomfort of a confident wrong phrase became
a regression test. The exchange stayed alive by moving this lesson into the
ear's execution, without calling one recovered fixture general accuracy.
