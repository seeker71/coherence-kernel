# 2026-08-30 — Equivalence is live learning authority

## Crossing

The live Form learning controller previously promoted an A/B challenger from
sample count and score alone.  That differed from the body's existing
inside-out learning cells: `recipe-learning.fk` adopts only an equivalent,
healthier recipe, and `self-improving-thought.fk` keeps its conclusion while
improving its margin.

`observed-auto-learning.fk` now carries that same ground into its live
decision:

- `oal-ab-authority` needs a present equivalence witness alongside the score
  and sample observation;
- `oal-promote?` checks that witness again at the execution door, so a stale
  `"challenger"` label cannot create a promotion by itself;
- the typed receipt retains `equivalent` beside the authority and next action;
- the speech model selector receives the same explicit witness rather than
  treating its metric as authority.

`nothing`, `0`, and every non-`1` value remain non-authorizing.  They do not
become an error or a fabricated negative result; they leave the current
champion in place and allow the normal local route to continue.  Choice, cut,
undo, fail, and timeout retain their existing distinct actions.

This was a kernel-path repair: no HTTP service, sidecar process, external
model server, or new ambient authority was introduced.
