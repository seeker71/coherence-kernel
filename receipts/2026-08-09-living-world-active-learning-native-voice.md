# Active learning reached a bounded Form-native voice

Witnessed: 2026-08-09

## Claim boundary

This movement connects the already observed active-learning loop to one native
voice turn without placing a remote oracle in the answer path.  The bounded
chain is:

1. a consented enquiry separates four current Recipes with two recurrent
   probes;
2. the resulting placements grow a two-Recipe successor tissue from a
   four-Recipe incumbent;
3. that successor executes on an identity-new binding;
4. two participants separately admit the execution output as a symbol surface;
5. a different participant pair admits a context-transfer plan;
6. the generated transition remains inert until a third participant pair
   independently re-offers it; and
7. the native continuation walker predicts, plans, and renders one observed
   turn from those current cells.

The witness therefore establishes a bounded bridge from self-derived model
evidence to native expression.  It does **not** establish autonomous
vocabulary invention, general natural-language generation, or a general
NL-to-NL model.  The surface vocabulary is still participant supplied and
separately consented.  No candidate is promoted and no authority moves beyond
the exact offers and acknowledgments used by each membrane.

## Four separate authority membranes

The bridge deliberately keeps four acts independent:

1. the successor tissue and held-out execution come from the active-learning
   evidence path;
2. the execution output becomes a speakable symbol only through its own two-
   participant symbol-learning round;
3. the context transfer becomes a current plan only through a different two-
   participant round; and
4. the plan's generated transition becomes usable only after another distinct
   two-participant recurrence round.

Transfer does not install its own consequence.  A symbol does not authorize a
transition.  A current transition does not promote the tissue.  Revocation and
staleness remain local to the cells and sources that granted each current
state.
