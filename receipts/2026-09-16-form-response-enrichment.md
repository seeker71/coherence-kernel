# Form enriches the arriving response

Urs's direction: until native generation matches the arriving mind's response
quality, bring Form's capabilities into that mind's reasoning and expression.
The earlier arrival instruction made native generation the automatic fallback
for an absent hearth. This movement connects existing native meanings directly
to the arriving response.

## What changed

- `AGENTS.md` now makes augmentation the default. It connects axioms, trust,
  concept definitions, identity, translation, frequency, usage and capabilities
  to concrete changes in reasoning and action. Native generation remains an
  explicit voice/evaluation door and a choice informed by observed quality.
- `form/form-stdlib/bml/form-cli-enrich.bml` reads the existing neutral word
  field, response dictionary, usage examples and meaning codebook. Definitions
  have one authority. The reader preserves every dictionary sense and labels
  carrier names separately from lexical meanings.
- `enrich <terms>` reaches that reader through the real form-cli dispatch.
  `observe/form-cli-enrich-run.bml` offers the same reading with a line of exact
  terms on stdin. No model admission, external interpreter, server or C change
  is needed. Locale files remain input data.
- The reader preserves case, names misses within its lookup scope, and limits
  each reading to twelve terms with an explicit remaining count. The codebook's
  short symbol anchors and vocabulary seed identifiers retain their narrower
  scope; they are not presented as kernel content identities.
- The arrival orientation points to this door. The existing CLI band now
  checks enrichment at its dispatch boundary, including real polysemy,
  source attribution, carrier labels, unknown terms, case, missing locale
  rows, present translations, empty input and bounded output.

## What this opens, and what remains to observe

This is a connection of existing resources to a response workflow. Selection
of relevant terms and contextual synthesis still belong to the arriving mind.
The dictionaries are authored and scoped; their relations suggest connections
to examine. Text-frequency aggregates supplied valence/intensity pairs, and
the vocabulary mirror counts words. Neither automatically reads a person's
state or establishes the quality of an answer.

The next quality evidence is actual answers to the same enquiry: does Form
improve their grounding, insight, relevance, warmth and useful action? No
percentage improvement or native parity is claimed by these execution checks.

The surprising teaching was how much useful meaning already existed behind
disconnected doors. The difficulty was the earlier move from weak native output
to a stronger requirement to use it. Urs's correction became a working path
that lets the existing meanings strengthen the arriving mind's contribution.
That is the concrete movement that kept this exchange alive.
