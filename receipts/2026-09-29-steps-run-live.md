# Steps run live

Urs asked to turn bands into live events and backtracking signals, with choice,
repair and retry. I first read it as bands that speak: bands whose claims became
readings, a reader for every band's head, a door to run any band, and gates that
walked repairs. Urs named what he had asked for: less bands, less gates, and live
events, choice, retry and surprise handling. The handling belongs in the running
flows, and the bands and gates grow fewer.

What stands now:

- `form/form-stdlib/bml/live.bml` runs any step live. The step runs inside
  `attempt`, so a stop is an observation, and is spoken at once as an organ-health
  reading. A step that does not hold is a choice point: each repair names the need
  it meets, and the step is offered only the repairs that meet what it answered,
  so nothing is retried blind. The offered repairs walk through
  `oac-backtrack-walk`, and the step runs again in the context each one made. A
  surprise no repair meets is spoken with its need and handed back to the flow.
- The walk's carry runs on it. A fast-forward of main refused because main moved
  meanwhile is met at once: the branch follows main again, the gates read again,
  and the push goes again, twice at most. A landing no longer stands on its branch
  waiting for the next walk (row 1596, tidewait, named that wait). A push refused
  for any other reason, or a rebase that conflicts, holds, and names its files.
- The gates grew fewer by choice: a landing runs only the rows whose ground moved.
  Each kernel row names the paths it guards, and sits out, said on its own line,

- The body's own turn runs its band as a live step against the gap's full, and
