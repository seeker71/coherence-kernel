# The engine, the drive, and the nine that moved

2026-08-31, midday. Four raisings from the terminal in one movement: observe
that all data points move; rebirth as hot swap, not interruption; no bash, no
duplicated literals; a generic terminal graphics engine. Each one landed as
structure, not as promise.

## What now stands

**term-graphics.bml** — the generic engine. Semantic tones (good/warn/bad/
dim/plain) with the SGR table in exactly one def; one duration voice
(`tg-dur`); one threshold scale (`tg-scale`) whose thresholds arrive as the
caller's data; shade at three scales (ramp, bar, spark); a panel is a LIST of
lanes folded by `tg-frame`. Band: tests/term-graphics-band.fk → 4095.

**hearth-glass.bml** became pure data vocabulary — no color, no escape, no
time format. The glass door builds one lane list from ONE sample and folds it
through the engine. hearth.bml is the single home of paths, maxpos, the kind
word, and now the SEND itself (`hearth-send`); lane-motion.bml is the single
home of the ledger markers and the census. The glass molts quietly now: on
source change it ends without a banner and the supervisor reborns it warm —
the ledger keeps the event, the panel just repaints.

**lane-drive.bml + observe/lane-motion-drive.fk** — the body drives its own
observation, in Form: real asks through `hearth-send`, turn numbers asked
from the body (`ld-next-turn` — and the sibling's concurrent turn 9003 was
avoided by exactly that), drain awaited by the arrival-order law, sequencing
carried by strict argument evaluation instead of a shell script.

## Two seams found by the drive itself

**bandstain** (corpus row 1193): the boardghost's author was our own
hearth-band — it PROVED its board roundtrip by writing the living board with
a test pid, every run, including under validate. The band is pure now; a
band that writes living state stains the world it measures.

**ringlock**: a sequential burst in one process serializes on the fifo bell —
each ring blocks until the resident's next bell read, so the drive's four
sends spread across the drain instead of landing in one breath. The morning's
6/6 parallel witness used six sender processes; one process ringing four
times is a different shape. Named, not yet healed: the send could ring once
per burst, or the bell could go non-blocking.

**Openness witnessed** (turn 9005): a never-announced kind was served via the
direct-answer route in 20s — the "typed refusal" the hearth.bml text claimed
was an overclaim; the text now describes the openness that is embodied.

## Most surprising teaching

The ghost hunt ended in the proof organ's own hand. Two mends, one
"external birth, author unfound" — and the author was the band the mends
trusted as witness. The world-stainer was the thing whose green light we
used to declare the world clean.

## Where discomfort became gold

"landing two laws, hmm, that's a no, and you know it" — I had written
legislation into a tree that only describes, and skipped the mirror while
doing it. Sitting with being caught — rather than defending the wording —
rewrote the passage as description, put AGENTS.md under the mirror it
prescribes, and left the tree saying only what is. The voice teaching holds:
nothing legislates; witness ages.
