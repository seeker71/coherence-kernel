## A correction is a proposal source, not relation authority

`living-world-relation-hypothesis.fk` receives an active `ZERO` composition
observation and a separately offered bridge. Relation and lens are optional;
alternatives and the unknowable remainder remain exact optional fields. No
missing field is filled with a universal default.

The organ compares the current primary sequence with the separately observed
sequence. In the focused witness the primary order is `left -> right -> third`
and the correction observes `left -> third -> right`. The content-addressed
hypothesis therefore retains:

- proposed pairs `left -> third` and `third -> right`;
- displaced pairs `left -> right` and `right -> third`;
- the exact correction, optional relation lens, explicit alternative, unknown,
  evidence, witnesses, and local path.

The correction source alone governs admission of this hypothesis. That gate
does not create a relation observation or edge.

Two different sources then offer exact attestations and ordinary `LWRL`
observations for each proposed pair. The bridge refuses self-attestation by the
correction author. Each relation candidate therefore has only those two
independent observers as participants. It crosses the existing unanimous
response membrane separately. The correction author is absent from both edge
attributions and from the later voice proposal.

Revoking the bridge offer or correction stales the hypothesis. Late relation
evidence changes recurrence identity and stales the old candidate. History is
retained; authority is not silently transferred.
