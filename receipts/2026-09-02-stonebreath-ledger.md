## Most surprising teaching

The field's own instrument needed the same heal as the kernel's import lane
the day before: both observed loudly and forgot to make the observation
answerable. Icetide's wall got a doorpulse; stonepatrol's ledger got a
release door — one wound family, three organs apart, and the doc had
already witnessed its shape ("a logged event without an applied response
is observation, not bidirectionality") before either heal existed.

## Where discomfort became gold

The ledger said the mirror cost 143 seconds, and yesterday's own runs of it
felt slow — every reason to take the stone as ground and start crystallizing
the mirror. Timing it first (0.13s) turned the whole walk: the work was not
to speed up a cell but to teach the worklist itself to lose entries
honestly. Believing the field's finding without re-witnessing it would have
spent the day optimizing a ghost.
