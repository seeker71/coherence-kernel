## Most surprising teaching

The branch's biggest commits by line count were worth the least: every
cap-removal arrived already answered, grown a second time by the line that
stayed home — and the smallest, quietest changes (an `if (nothing? ...)`
before a measure, a fall-through that prints one line) were the whole
cargo. Size in a stranded branch measures effort spent, not debt owed.
And the stamp gate's staleness was nobody's regression twice over: main
had been carrying a stale bootstrap stamp for nine days, and the reunion —
suspected of breaking it — is what made it true again.
