engines. Each engine, and what bringing it home in Form needs:

- **espeak tongues (`:128`).** espeak-ng is compiled into piper's CPython

  `voice-onnx-band.fk:43`, piper's phonemes for "the body is learning to speak."
- **zh (`zh_CN-xiao_ya-medium`, pinyin).** g2pW, a polyphone model in ONNX
  (`g2pw.onnx` under the voice store), a pinyin-to-phoneme table, and
  `unicode_rbnf` for numbers. Home: g2pW's graph on the body's ONNX pass

- **ja (`ja_JA-hi_fi_captain-medium`).** pyopenjtalk: OpenJTalk's
  morphological analysis over a MeCab dictionary, the marine accent model, and

- **th (`th_TH-tsync2-medium`).** TLTK's `th2ipa`: dictionary word
