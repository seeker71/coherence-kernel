# Experience-grown symbols reach the native voice

Date: 2026-08-07

The first native text surface rendered direct symbol offers. This movement
removes that shortcut from the growth path: recurring consented observations
now propose symbol cells, preserve competing evidence, cross unanimous
admission, and become the only surface material used by a second grown-voice
witness. No lexical row lives in the emitter and no remote model enters.

## One symbol identity

`living-world-symbol.fk` now owns the sole `LWNV-SYMBOL` shape used by both
learning and voice. A symbol carries an exact referent, non-negative offered
position, exact surface segment, evidence, and local path. The module contains
no vocabulary, language name, grammar rule, truth predicate, or authority.

## Observation-derived symbol proposals

`living-world-symbol-learning.fk` receives source-free `LWSL-OBSERVATION`
cells. Source identity is recovered only from active attribution. Each
observation contains an exact referent, position, surface, lens,
acknowledgement, evidence, and path.

For a candidate referent/position/surface:

- exact-surface `ONE` observations are support;
- different-surface `ONE` and explicit alternative `NODE` observations remain
  alternatives;
- `ZERO` remains dissent;
- `NOTHING` remains silence;
- other `NODE` observations remain unresolved.

Counts are distinct active attribution counts rather than distinct payload
cells. Two sources offering the content-identical observation therefore count
as two witnesses without duplicating the observation identity. Every support,
alternative, dissent, silence, and unresolved source governs admission; a
numerical majority cannot erase a minority observation.

The focused world contains two exact supporters for `forty`, one independently
offered competing surface, one dissent, and one silence. The candidate keeps
all five witnesses and participants. A separate two-source recurrence proposes
`-nine`. The competing surface has only one support and cannot independently
cross the offered threshold. Neither proposal integrates while responses are
missing; both integrate only after every participant acknowledges.

Candidate freshness re-derives the entire evidence set. Revoking one of the two
shared-support attributions drops support below threshold while the other
source's observation remains active. A late alternative also changes evidence
identity. Both movements make the prior candidate stale without deleting its
history.

## Grown voice

`living-world-native-voice.fk` now resolves either a current direct symbol or a
current integrated symbol-learning candidate. Learned-symbol witnesses are the
candidate's actual active attributions, not synthetic ownership of its nested
shape.

The grown-voice context includes the executed Recipe result and dependencies,
both learned symbol candidates, and the exact support/alternative/dissent/
silence observations needed to re-derive them. Every holder explicitly grants
only its tissue to the voice capability. No direct `LWNV-SYMBOL` payload is
active in that context; the surfaces come only from current learned candidates.

Form orders the learned surfaces and renders the executed referent `49` as
`forty-nine`. The utterance has ten exact attribution witnesses and eight
distinct governing participants: execution holders, learned-symbol holders,
and the capability holder. It waits without responses and integrates only after
all eight acknowledge.

A late observation is deliberately not granted into the old minimized context,
so that context's content identity remains unchanged. Full-world freshness
still detects that the learned symbol reopened and invalidates the old
utterance/proposal. Capability minimization therefore cannot hide lifecycle
movement outside the projection.

## Honest next floor

This is vocabulary growth from recurrence, not yet grammar growth. Surface and
absolute position still arrive together in each observation. The next movement
is to learn relative-before/after and grouping relations from interaction,
retain cycles and incompatible orders as first-class contention, and let a
voice plan emerge from an admitted acyclic relation field rather than supplied
numeric positions.
