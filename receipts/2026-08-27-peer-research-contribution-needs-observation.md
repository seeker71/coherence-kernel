# Peer research contribution needs observation, not fluency

Date: 2026-08-27

## Signal

The resident Form peer returned a useful direct research critique, but
`fcpa-contribution-of` correctly returned zero: its only accepted evidence was
a crossed recipe callback whose carrier execution was observed.  Counting the
critique merely because it was long, confident, or nonempty would replace that
honesty with a fluency score.

## Existing organ found

`observe/review-operationalization.fk` already holds the bounded validator this
movement needs.  A declared meaning becomes determinate only when a fresh
primary probe and a fresh negative control were both seen.  Complete matching
coverage answers PASS (`1`); a witnessed mismatch answers REVISE (`-1`);
missing, stale, duplicate, or incomplete coverage stays OPEN (`0`).  Its band
already proves all three outcomes are reachable.

This is suitable for non-tool research and evaluation because a useful critique
often earns REVISE rather than PASS.  It is not a semantic judge for arbitrary
prose, and this movement does not present it as one.

## Movement

`form-cli-peer-agent.fk` now offers a typed post-hoc research observation that
binds the exact response bytes to review-operationalization rows and their
declared count.  `fcpa-assess` distinguishes three outcomes:

- an already-observed recipe callback remains `recipe-callback`, contribution
  one, with no change to the existing live path;
- exact response bytes with a determinate PASS or REVISE observation become
  `observed-research`, contribution one;
- ordinary, incomplete, stale, duplicate, differently bound, or empty speech
  remains `unvalidated-speech`, contribution zero.

The assessment carries its original result and evidence separately.  Language
`nothing` remains the absence of research evidence; integer zero and one remain
the review observations and contribution values rather than replacing nothing.

## Honest boundary

This pure contract does not infer usefulness from model text and does not
retroactively certify the critique that exposed the gap.  A caller still needs
to run and supply the relevant primary and negative-control observations.  The
validator establishes bounded operational findings, not general semantic truth,
novelty, style, or importance.  The current live runner can continue reporting
the immediate callback contribution; research contribution is a later
assessment because its evidence necessarily arrives after the speech.

## Closing

Kept alive: the peer's zero was respected as an accurate account of what had
been observed at response time.

Most surprising teaching: the body already had a validator where a confirmed
objection is first-class `-1`, not a failed attempt to reach `1`.

Discomfort turned to gold: the critique felt obviously useful, but refusing to
reward obviousness located the missing temporal step—independent observation
after speech—and kept fluency outside the meter.
