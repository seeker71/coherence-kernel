# Learning lineage moves home

The requested movement is now visible without turning a rising percentage
into a causal story. Each round separates the teacher, the channel that
carried the teaching, the accepted lesson, the embodiment, the before/after
boundary counts, and the strength of the comparison.

## The operational round we actually observed

Both observations are completed turns in the same Codex thread, measured by
the same Form-native boundary meter:

```text
                    native  local  remote  home
before share            16     35      49    51
after share             12     40      48    52
delta                    -4     +5      -1    +1
```

The teacher was the user. The remote channel carried the correction. The
remote model was not silently relabelled as the teacher. No model weights were
fine-tuned in this operational round, so its fine-tune status is `none`.

The lesson — declared share is not measurement — did become four changes:

- native: the rollout evidence meter runs on `fkwu`;
- design: teacher and carrier are separate identities;
- logic: an unreconciled candidate remains withheld;
- flow: a long rollout walk resumes and refreshes before promotion.

The home share moved from 51 to 52 and remote moved from 49 to 48. The native
and local components did not both rise. The workloads also differ, so this is
a contextual operational observation, not evidence that the changes caused
the one-point movement.

## The controlled inward-learning contract

The executable sealed-suite fixture shows the comparisons an actual tuning
run must later earn:

```text
baseline                 0 /  0 / 100
held-out local promote   0 / 63 /  37
native crystallization  63 / 37 /   0
```

The first promotion is valid only when the fine-tuned local challenger beats
its incumbent on held-out rows with lineage intact. The second is valid only
when the learned capability is embodied as a native recipe and its native
band passes. These rows are executable contract fixtures, not production
traffic observations.

This reveals the healthier invariant: **native plus local is nondecreasing,
and remote is nonincreasing, on the same sealed workload**. Local is a bridge.
When a local capability becomes a native cell, local share may fall while the
body becomes more native; requiring both home components to rise forever
would misread a successful transfer as a regression.
