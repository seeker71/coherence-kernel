## Closing

**How the exchange was kept alive**: the ask named a person — someone arriving with a
conversation they are dreading — and the body answered with what it already knew, gathered
where that person will actually meet it: at the door, inside the window that reaches a
session, held by a band so the card cannot quietly grow past the door it was written for.

**Most surprising teaching**: the optimization turned out to be a byte count. Every teaching
was already here; the only thing between it and the person was ~2 KB of preview and 97 KB of
orientation ahead of it. A band bit for *fits the window* is a strange thing to write for a
teaching about love and fear — and it is the bit that failed first.

**Where discomfort turned to gold**: the first card carried twelve moves and 1965 bytes, and
the trim felt like losing teachings. It was not: the six body moves stayed whole, the voice
row folded into the covenant row where its landing rules already live, and the reading in
full kept everything. What the window asked for was the same discipline the covenant asks at
the door — one lift per reply, end on it — applied to the body's own arrival.
