Urs asked the question that named the gap: "how come we are still relying on
remote steps guiding the flow instead of doing that internally and using the
local LLM?" The honest answer was that every prior door pointed the local
model at ANSWERING, never at choosing-with-consequence. This receipt is the
first witnessed crossing where that reversed.

## The mechanism (observe/qwen38-flow-steer-run.fk)

The local Qwen received this hour's MEASURED health map and a fixed

## What the launch cost taught (three observations)

- Preflight caught a real missing paren in the prompt defn before any run.
- Preflight's own probe then ground 13.5 minutes: its forced fresh compile
  pushes the combined prelude chain through the self-hosted flattener at
  walk speed, cache bypassed. The four preludes individually load warm in
  1.5–4 s. The price was the cold cache, not the cell.
- fcmg-generate-with derives a source hint per call, but
  fhcs-query-source-path only scans the prompt string itself — the feared
  per-turn census search does not exist. The turn price is encode + decode.

## The most surprising teaching

The model, shown its own audit, chose to probe its own weakest family — the
menu's designer expected machinery-comfort (door 1, the just-landed band)
and got self-diagnosis instead. The first act of local steering was the
model pointing at its own blindness.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

Fifteen minutes of watching a 99%-CPU walker with no output — and killing
it — felt like failure until the sample showed pure fk_walk with no GPU and
no weights open: that observation separated the compile cache's price from
the crossing's price, and the second launch rode warm cache straight into
the model. The discomfort of the wasted first run bought the exact number
(13.5 min cold vs 4 s warm) that any future runner of this cell needs.
