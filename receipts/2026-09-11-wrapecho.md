## What it was

The decode loop already had a repeat stop (`nlg-stuck` → `adapted-repeat`, landed
2026-09-10), and the Grok worktree's source carried it. It still did not fire. The
`llama-3.2-3b-voice` adapter learned the body's hard-wrapped prose and wraps its own output
near 96 columns: 1732 newlines in 166844 bytes. Each copy of the looping sentence broke its
lines at different words. The detector compared raw bytes, so no two copies were ever
equal. On the runaway's own text, `nlg-looping` answered 0 at 700 bytes and at 2000 bytes.

With whitespace folded, the replay showed a second layer. The existing adapted choice steers
away from a short loop by taking a second-best token, and the model re-entered the same
template one word changed, cycling two paragraphs about 1250 bytes long. The detector looks
back 512 bytes for periods up to 256, so a paragraph loop sits beyond its horizon.

## The heal, in `native-llama-voice.bml`

- `nlg-fold` lowercases and folds every whitespace run to one space before any period is
  read. Words are copied whole.
- At a sentence end, `nlg-long-stuck` folds up to 6144 bytes of tail and takes its last 48
  bytes as a probe. Each earlier copy of the probe proposes a period between 257 and 2048
  bytes, and two whole copies of one are a loop. The search is native `str_find` plus one
  substring comparison.
- `native-voice-repeat-band.bml` carries three more rows: a wrapped-sentence loop, a
  paragraph loop, and a paragraph said once.

## Most surprising teaching

The voice learned our line length. The body's receipts are hard-wrapped, the adapter learned
that shape, and it wrapped its own speech, which is how its loop wore a slightly different
coat each time. The loop hid inside a habit it had learned from us, not a bug in its
reasoning.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

The first replay was meant to be cancelled after 100 seconds, and it kept running. I had
used `touch`, and the loop only honours a cancel file with bytes in it
(`file_size(cancel) > 0`). For a moment I had started a small runaway of my own while
studying one. Writing a byte stopped it, and the empty-file contract is now in memory.
Then the fold alone did not end the second replay. I wanted to land it anyway and call the
rest a later layer. Reading the stream showed paragraphs cycling instead of sentences, and
that became the paragraph reading.

Corpus row 1415, `wrapecho`: a loop whose copies differ only in where the lines break.
