## The run

`observe/prompt-movement-live.fk` + the movement surface grown in BML
(field reader, menu grammar, door parse — all lawborn). The verbatim
prompt was staged as text; the body did the rest:

- five steered steps: the resident read the task and chose door 1 each
  time, WITH REASONS IN ITS OWN WORDS ("the previous receipts are

  lane inside the rest-force row);
- the closing report was the model's own: "The floor has not moved...
  the repeated 63 across five identical walks indicates a static
  state... Re-grounding confirms the ground is exactly where it was
  left." Then <STOP>. residence_released=1.

## The most surprising teaching

The model's closing was BETTER grounded than a flattering summary would
have been: given five identical 63s it refused to claim movement —
"the ground is exactly where it was left." The body's first
self-steered report chose honesty over narrative, unprompted.
