## What moved

The Recipe that absorbed the prior prospective contradiction now crosses two
new Form-native membranes:

1. Fresh I/J sources reoffer one exact composition-scoped Recipe and cause it
   to re-execute all three retained placements and all five prospective cases.
2. The admitted scoped trial may form one exact successor hypothesis only when
   composition, Recipe, retained re-execution, prospective re-execution, and
   the disjoint construction/trial source fields are content-identical.

The scoped offer kind is deliberately invisible to the historical global
revision nursery.  One source yields zero recurrent scoped candidates.  Two
sources yield one viable candidate.  Alternatives and unknowns remain in the
field; Recipe execution remains inactive and optional.

## Integrity break and repair

The first implementation reused the global revision nursery.  Observation
showed one source with one historical viable attempt and two sources with two
viable attempts.  More importantly, adding the new global offer changed the
historical trial-field identity and made the already-admitted parent successor
and composition stale.  The child was interrupted at that exact break; its
unfinished result was not accepted as evidence.

The repair introduced `LWRTRT-OFFER`, scoped to one exact composition.  The old
nursery does not collect this kind.  After both I/J offers arrive, the parent
composition remains current.  Fresh trial evaluation is derived from the
composition's own Recipe and exact retained/prospective cells, without a
remote oracle or imported answer set.
