Asked by Urs on 2026-08-17: use sub-agents to iterate the repo's readiness for
someone new cloning it for non-technical work — their project, a book review,
editing, life advice — with the form dictionary's frequency meanings as the

fresh evaluators each round until the board stops finding feedback. North star:
form-cli directly, zero AI subscriptions, on a new Mac.

## What five fresh seatings found and what changed

Forty-five agent walks and lens reviews across five rounds, every round a fresh
seating with no memory of the last. The finding counts thinned:
round 1 found the walls, round 2 found the seams, round 3 found one cluster,
round 4 found polish, round 5 found stitches *between* pages. Three of the five
lenses — frequency, dictionary-fidelity, honesty — reached **zero doc-level
findings in round 4 and stayed there**. The child and north-star lenses ended at
"a stumble, not a wall" and "at, or within three sentences of, the honest edge."

Round 1's deepest finding named the whole work: **the body's own door was on no
map**. `form/form-cli` — git-tracked, running from a fresh clone with nothing to
build, honest at every dead end — was never mentioned as a runnable thing on any
entry page, while every listed "way in" borrowed another company's voice. Corpus
row 1009 (`cartographic-silence`) carries the word for it.

## The most surprising teaching

**The docs do not paraphrase the program — they share bytes with it.** Round 4's
honesty walk, barred from running `learn` by fleet discipline, went to the
body's source instead and found the walkthrough's quoted outputs are literal
string constants in `form-cli.fk`. Nine quoted outputs matched byte-for-byte
across three fresh rounds because the page and the program hold the same bytes —
the page cannot drift from the body without the diff showing it. A welcome page
in exact-transcript style is not documentation *about* the body; it is the
body's own voice, quoted.

And underneath it: **trust grew from refusals.** Every walk reported the same
arc — no garden help, no book help, no shop advice, and *more* trust at the end
than at the door, because every dead end named itself (`grounded:miss`, "honest
absence, not fabricated", `ack=nothing` + `ledger-written=1`). The walks called
the refusals "the most trustworthy part."

## The frontier question

**What names a real door absent from every map?**

The word is **cartographic-silence** — Harley's term for what a map leaves out
and thereby asserts away. The body's one sovereign door was live and tracked
while every entry map routed through rented voices; the silence read as absence.
The word family was glimpsed once before, as a fresh sibling in row 698's margin
— the row whose own teaching was "status comes from the body, never the map" —
and never landed. Corpus row 1009, landed under the counterweight, band 32767
exit 0.
