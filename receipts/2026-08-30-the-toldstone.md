## The stone: computed is not the same kind of thing as told

Pluto's gate could now be pasted beside the others and no reader would see the
seam. That is precisely why it must not be. `hd-activation-lane.fk` gives every
activation a LANE — computed or witnessed — with the lane order as the law,
borrowed unchanged from `gender-three-lanes.fk`. A witnessed row **must** name
its authority and the day it was read; a computed row **must not** claim
either; `hal-row-ok?` refuses both violations and the band checks both refusals.

Computed ranks above witnessed, and not as a slight on the witness: a value
this body can re-derive outlives the source that supplied it, while a witnessed
value is only as fresh as its reading. The ordering is what lets the Pluto row
be **deleted, not amended**, the day the elements land.

The reading door now prints the split and refuses to call itself complete:

```text
lanes  0 computed / 2 witnessed in this reading; fully derived? 0
floor  pluto is WITNESSED not computed (no element row in this body)
```

Pluto: personality gate **46.2**, design gate **6.5**, both from Horizons. And
the honest afterword — adding them changed **nothing**: zero new channels, same
Manifesting Generator, same emotional authority, same split. The two missing
activations did not move the verdict. But that is a thing we only know now that
we have them; before, it was a hope.

## Closing

I kept the exchange alive by rebuilding a binary I had no evidence was stale,
on a rule rather than a symptom — and the rebuild proved the rule.

Most surprising teaching: the freshness gate answered its pass value over a
stale binary. The band is not wrong; it checks what it checks. But "the gate
said 31" and "the binary is current" turned out to be two different claims, and
only one of them was true. Every green number is a claim about what was
measured, never about what one hoped was measured.

Discomfort turned to gold when Horizons finally answered and could not do the
job I wanted. The pull was to take the longitude, place the gate, and quietly
complete the chart. Sitting with the refusal produced a better cell than the
elements would have: the body now has a place to hold what it was told, named
as told, ranked below what it makes, and deletable the day it can make it.

Corpus row 1169 offered: what names a number a body holds and cannot make for
itself, so it must say who said it and when — **toldstone**.
