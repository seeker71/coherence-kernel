# Behavioral species without a representative

Witnessed: 2026-08-07

## Claim

The Form-native living-world path can now retain two source-distinct Recipe
trials as members of one deliberately local behavioral species when all of the
following remain current:

- each member has an exact native trial receipt;
- target, observed before/after boundary, evaluator, and effect align;
- independently offered observations support both pair directions at a
  positive recurrence threshold;
- no current dissent, alternative acknowledgment, or unresolved observation
  blocks the membrane; and
- every trial and observation holder unanimously admits the resulting species.

The species contains an unordered member field and an exact receipt field. It
has no primary Recipe and grants neither source authority over the other.

## Native observation

The witness begins with one integrated transformation target and the existing
native Recipe trial whose local boundary is `1 -> 4`. Its offer recurrence is
then revoked without reconstructing or mutating the historical trial receipt.
A second, source-distinct Recipe is separately offered, selected, executed, and
integrated against the same target. Its local boundary is also `1 -> 4`.

The historical receipt check does not pretend the first nursery selection is
still the current action choice. It checks the still-active trial candidate,
current target, exact trace dependencies, expected and actual boundaries,
comparison, time observation, witnesses, Recipe source, contract, evaluator,
effect, byte budget, lens, and local path. This lets immutable observations
remain evidence after the action nursery has legitimately moved on.

Two current attributions support the first orientation and two support the
reciprocal orientation. One separately attributed `NOTHING` observation stays
first-class silence. The resulting field is `ALIGNED`, with 2 members, 2 exact
receipts, 23 attribution witnesses, and 16 distinct governing sources. All 16
must acknowledge before the species becomes active.

A second native Recipe with boundary `1 -> 3` is retained as `DIVERGENT`, not
forced into the species. A current alternative acknowledgment or `ZERO`
dissent changes the aligned field to `AMBIGUOUS`. Revoking one of the two
direct-orientation supports changes it to `OPEN`; the old candidate becomes
stale. Silence, explicit alternative fields, and offered unknown cells remain
present in every state.

A separately offered question, held by two sources, can ask the admitted
species for its members. The answer is a native `NODE` acknowledgment carrying
the exact member field, exact receipts, shared local signature, evidence set,
alternatives, unknowns, and a derivation containing the admitted species field
and question attributions. Eighteen distinct sources govern answer admission.
Revoking one question attribution makes the answer stale. No prose generator
or remote oracle is in this path.

A zero or negative threshold never synthesizes consent: it cannot align a
species or answer a question. Lenses, alternative fields, unknowns, silence,
and abstention remain optional; authority does not.

## Honest boundary

This is new structural evidence, not model-weight learning, NL-to-NL
translation, vocabulary homecoming, score movement, promotion, or proof of
global program equivalence. The species signature is intentionally local to
one target and one observed boundary. Two members do not establish a general
concept, and a native structural `NODE` answer is not yet Sema's generative
voice.

The next floor is multi-context recurrence: let a species remain provisional
across several separately consented targets, admit additional members without
pairwise winner formation, split honestly when a later context diverges, and
offer its accumulated behavior as evidence to the existing concept/enquiry
organs. Only after that should this tissue attempt native compositional naming
or language rendering.
