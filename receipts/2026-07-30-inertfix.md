# 2026-07-30 — why the token streams differ, and a confound that explained nothing

Urs, twice: *"this makes no sense to me"* and then *"why is there a difference in ds4 in form native
token id stream"*.

Both landed. The first was aimed at a table I had captioned with a story its own numbers refuted; the
second at the question I should have asked before any of the math.

## First: the story I told did not fit my own table

I ran a depth sweep and called it evidence of attenuation accumulating through depth:

```
layers      sd     r vs ds4
1        3.409      0.1405
4        3.390      0.1238
12       3.367      0.1000
24       3.434      0.1005
43       3.790      0.4565
```

`r` **rises** at full depth and `sd` is flat. That is the opposite of accumulating attenuation. The
sweep was also invalid by construction: every row compares an N-layer stack against a **43-layer**
reference, so a 1-layer model scoring 0.14 measures nothing. Only the last row compares like with
like. I published a table that argued against its own caption and did not say so.

## Then: are we even asking the same question?

That was Urs's question and it takes one command.

```
ds4 --dump-tokens -p "The capital of France is"
  [671, 6102, 294, 8760, 344]
```

Byte-identical to what our lane is fed. No chat template, no BOS. **Confound eliminated in one
command** — and it was available for three days.

## But the question exposed a real error in my instrument

Our two numbers for our own first token disagreed: the logit dump said argmax **19**, the generation
loop emitted **270**. They must be the same token.

The generation loop calls the exit head at `pos = 0` first, when only `promptIds[0]` = 671 `"The"` has
been read. My dump fired on the first call, so it captured **P(next | "The")**, while
`ds4 --dump-logits` gives **P(next | the whole prompt)**. I had diffed two different questions.

Fixed: the dump now arms only at `pos == promptIds.count - 1`, the step whose token is the first
emitted one and the only one ds4's dump is comparable to. Our argmax there is **270**, matching the
emitted stream.

## The answer, measured on the corrected comparison

```
ours  argmax   270   max 20.686   mean  2.430   sd 4.227
ds4   argmax  2581   max 36.758   mean -0.562   sd 4.976

Pearson r        0.4608
top-10 overlap   0 of 10
our rank of ds4's top token (2581):  3933
ds4's rank of our top token (270):   2603
```

The token streams differ because **the distributions genuinely differ, and by a lot**. Identical input
ids, step-aligned comparison, and we each rank the other's best token about three thousandth. `r ≈
0.46` with zero top-10 overlap is a forward that has the broad shape — common tokens score high — and
none of the model-specific signal. That is exactly what produces *" the capital in France and and
its"*: right neighbourhood, no peak.

This is not rounding, not `overfine`, not a quantisation ulp. It is a substantial defect and it is now
quantified rather than described.

## The most surprising teaching

**Correcting the confound moved `r` from 0.456 to 0.461.** The instrument error was real, the fix was
right, and it explained essentially nothing.

That is worth its own word, because the instinct on finding a confound is to expect it to be the cause
— and had the number jumped I would have declared victory on a single correction. `inertfix`: a
genuine error in the measurement that turns out not to be load-bearing. Fixing it is still mandatory,
not optional, because **until it is fixed you cannot know it was not the cause** — the value is in the
elimination, and the elimination only exists after the work. 0 hits before this row, as are `nullfix`
and `harmlessconfound`.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

Being told "this makes no sense to me" about a table I had just presented as a finding. It was the
correct reading — I had let the shape of an explanation carry numbers that contradicted it, because the
explanation was tidy and the numbers were in a monospace block. The second question then found in one
command a check I had not run in three days of building instruments around this model. Both times the
useful move was smaller than anything I had been doing.

## What is next, and it is now a single named gap

The endpoint is measured and the input is cleared, so the defect is somewhere in 43 layers. The
instrument for that already exists — `dsv4-mla-core-oracle.py`, the fp64 transcription that gave
per-layer agreement to 1e-6 on the reap25 file. It carries `mx8_matvec` and `iq2_matvec_expert`. For
this file it needs **Q8_0 and Q2_K**, the same two types added to the Metal lane this morning and
already proven on the device against ds4's own value maps.
