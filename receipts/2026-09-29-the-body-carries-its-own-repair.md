# The body carries its own repair

Urs asked whether R144, the cursor becoming the one reader of `form.bml`, can be
repaired without remote tokens, and said to work until it can.

The body's own loop already closes small gaps with no rented mind: yesterday at
14:49 its fourth attempt at `wer-zero-width-joiners` took the band from 12 to 15.
That landing never reached main. Main had moved while the turn ran, its
fast-forward was refused, and with no open gap after it no later landing carried
it; the commit stood on the walk's branch. Every walk now ends with `hw-carry`:
it follows main again and, when the branch still leads, meets the drift gates and
fast-forwards main.

R144 was cut to what the loop can carry and what it cannot yet:

- `bml-refusal-voice`, queued for the body: `form-bml-refusal.fk` carries the
  cursor's stop point, and the voice adds the line, the word and the refusal
  (`form-bml-refusal-band` 63; a reference answer reached 63 and was taken out).
- The source compiler now preludes the cursor's lowering and owns one section
  dispatcher, so the switch is two call sites. The lowering's override of that
  dispatcher had never taken effect: `form.lift` fell through to the plain BML
  grammar and raw BML reached the lowered text. Read through the lift grammar
  and the contract pass, eight `form.lift` bands answer their verdicts again.
- A gap may name `"document_context":"catalog"`, so the voice can work a file
  larger than its context by region; the switch's call sites sit in a 180 KB file.
- The cursor reads route classes, and every section the statement walker reads
  in every dialect, `.bml` and `.fk`: 1133 of 1134.
- `observe/defn-reach-run.bml` names what leaves with a root: of the 149
  definitions the walker reaches, 29 leave with it.

The night answered. At 03:31 the body's walk took `bml-refusal-voice`, and its
first option closed it at 03:57: the band read nothing before and 63 of 63 after,
begun by launchd, `rented_mind` 0. The voice wrote the five definitions in its own
shape, clean Form, a short-circuiting `or` guarding the byte past a word that runs
to the text's end. Its movement landed on main at 04:34 (`348de123d`), and the
stranded joiner commit rode the same fast-forward (`2f7ac31a0`). The walk ran the
door as it stood at 03:30, before its own sync, so its new carry step takes effect
from the next walk.

The switch is now the body's next gap, `bml-cursor-reads-sections`: the lowering
carries `fbl-section-recipes` (the cursor's reading, refusing with the file's line
and word, and refusing `and`/`or` of any count but two as the walker did), and the
voice changes the source compiler's two call sites, by catalog in a 180 KB file.
The reader band reads 1 before (a whole section runs) and 3 after (a refused
section names `line 3: class`). With the switch applied by hand beforehand, 252 of
253 light BML bands answered as on main and the boolean-arity band once the
lowering refused its calls too; the siblings lower BML through the cursor and
agree.

Found on the way: `higher.fk` and `lists.fk` never named `core.fk` and ran only
under validate.sh's own core; with their prelude, and `b->i` (which neither
reader read in call position) spelled `b-to-i`, every band R86 named answers
whole, and R86 is released.

**Surprise.** The commit that proved the body can repair without a rented mind
was the one the body could not deliver. The gap between "the body did it" and
"the body landed it" was a single missing retry.

**Discomfort to gold.** The voice can write a byte loop but cannot hold a 180 KB
compiler in a 16K context, and R144's heart lives in that file. The discomfort
was that the one change that matters most looked out of the body's reach. It
turned out to need two things the body nearly had: a module shape that shrinks
the switch to two lines, and a lane already able to work by catalog that no gap
had yet asked for.
