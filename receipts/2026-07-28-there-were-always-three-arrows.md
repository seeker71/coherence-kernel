## The three arrows

| | arrow | status |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | referent → the word's gender | **REFUTED.** `das Männchen` is neuter over a grown man |
| **2** | the word's gender → how the referent is imagined | **REAL, direction unestablished.** `frau Sonne`; Jakobson; Japanese breaks the necessity |
| **3** | the **sound** of a word → the body of speaker and hearer | **ATTESTED, MEASURED — and it does not route through gender at all** |

Everything I proved today was arrow 1. Urs is pointing at arrow 3. **They were never in competition.**
Saying "der Mond might have historical reasoning *and* the sounds themselves act on the body" is not a
compromise between two positions — it is simply correct, because those are different arrows carrying
different evidence.

## Arrow 3 is the best-attested of the three

Looked up this session, and it is stronger than I expected:

```
humming raises nasal nitric oxide by up to 15-fold over quiet exhalation
vocal fold vibration + nasal NO give an additional route of vagal stimulation
HRV pilot: humming produced the LOWEST stress index measured — lower than sleep
OM chanting: parasympathetic dominance, limbic deactivation, EEG complexity shifts
```

That is better attested than arrow 2, which rests on a philologist's observation and a contested
psycholinguistic literature. **Urs reached for the strongest of the three, not the weakest.**

And the mechanism is exactly why it bypasses gender: the nasal /m/ and long /oː/ in `Mond` do what
they do as **acoustics and airflow**. A nonsense syllable with the same phonetics moves the vagus the
same way. The effect is real and orthogonal to the whole gender question — which is precisely why the
two claims never touched.

## Where the quantum framing costs him — and a correction that goes his way

The one disagreement, and the reason is not that the idea is disreputable. It is that **it subtracts
support instead of adding it.**

Every measured mechanism above is classical: acoustics, vocal fold vibration, nasal nitric oxide,
vagal afferents, respiratory entrainment (chanting pulls the breath toward ~6/min, where HRV peaks),
auditory-limbic pathways. Robust, replicated, mechanistically specified. Attaching that to quantum
mechanics takes a well-supported claim and makes it lean on an unsupported one. **The physics Urs is
pointing at is stronger without the word `quantum` in front of it.**

And I owe a correction here that runs in his favour. I was going to write that decoherence had settled
the question. **That is out of date:**

```
Tegmark 2000, Reimers 2009, McKemmish 2009   microtubule decoherence in femtoseconds
BUT hydrophobic-interior models and Babcock et al. 2024 (superradiant excitonic states)
    extend coherence estimates by ~SEVEN orders of magnitude, toward 10–100 µs
and "no clear-cut experiment has yet falsified Orch-OR"
```

The standing challenges are decoherence timescales **and a lack of unique empirical predictions** —
and it is the second one, not the first, that decides it here. Axiom-4: a hypothesis with no
prediction distinguishing it from its classical rivals offers no interface through which it could be
**observed as distinct**. Not refuted. Not admitted. **Pending, precisely** — which is a real status in
this body and not a polite dismissal.

The cell computes the disagreement rather than asserting it: `tas-classical-iff-measured?` — classical
and measured coincide **row for row**, six rows. The quantum row is exactly the unmeasured one.

## The last clause, and the honest uncertainty

"that energy is a living expression of consciousness itself" is a metaphysical commitment rather than a
physical claim. It offers no interface, so it enters the way the archangels and the Eddas do — held as
what is held, at its own attestation, column on the row. Same door, no scorn. It is not competing with
the nitric oxide.

And **"coldering" I cannot place.** Two real Nordic practices sit near it and I will not guess which
was meant: **galdr**, the Old Norse chanted magic from `gala`, to crow or chant; and **kulning**, the
Scandinavian high-register herding call. Both are real, both sit squarely in arrow 3, and naming both
beats picking one.

## The most surprising teaching

**My derivation was narrower than I had been treating it as, for the second time in two days.**
Yesterday Urs found arrow 2, which I had not tested. Today he found arrow 3, which I had not even
noticed existed — and arrow 3 turns out to carry *better* evidence than either of the ones I had been
arguing about.

I spent a day proving that gender does not come from the referent, and the whole time there was a
third channel running underneath the question that has nothing to do with gender, is measurable in
nitric oxide and heart-rate variability, and was never in dispute. The thing I was defending was
true and small.

## Where the discomfort turned to gold

The discomfort was recognising a pattern in myself: twice now, a claim of mine that was correct got
carried further than its evidence, and both times the person who found the edge was the one I had
been correcting. Being right about arrow 1 made me treat the territory as settled, and "the referent
is never consulted" quietly became "there is nothing else going on here." Those are not the same
sentence, and only the first one was proven.

The gold is that the correction is generous in both directions. Urs gets arrow 3 — the best-supported
of the three — and I get to say so without giving up anything I actually established. And checking
the quantum literature instead of dismissing it from memory turned up a seven-order-of-magnitude
revision that favours his instinct on the physics while leaving the verdict where axiom-4 puts it.
**Both of those only appeared because the disagreement got made precise instead of made general.**
