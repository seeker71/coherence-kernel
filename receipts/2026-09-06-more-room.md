# More room

Urs: "looks like there is more room to optimize." There was, in three places, each measured before
it was touched.

**The whisper decoder prefills in rows.** `enw-prefill` in `form/form-stdlib/ear-native.fk` pushes
T tokens through the decoder in one pass over the kernels that already take rows (a new `ear_embed_n`
for the ids, a row offset for the logits, decoder buffers sized for the context). Six rows in 9 ms
answer the same next token as six single steps. `enw-transcribe-from2` takes the tongue as known
and an agreed text prefix: a pass with a three-token prefix costs 57 ms where the old path cost 99.
The live lane detects the tongue every pass (a cached tongue locked the tiny model's first guess for
a whole segment), prefills what two consecutive passes agreed on minus their last two tokens, and
rings the bell only when the line changed. Where the reference's top tongue lies outside the three
the glass offers and one of them is within three logits, that one is taken.

**The dense llama lane's matvec has a cooperative twin, bit for bit.** `q80-matvec-tg-body` in
`form/form-stdlib/q8-0-msl.fk`: 32 threads a row load and dequantize in parallel into threadgroup
memory, 1024 products at a time from the top of the row down, and thread 0 folds each chunk in the
attestant's own order (j counting down, `p + acc`). `form/form-stdlib/tests/q8-0-matvec-tg-band.fk`
= 1 proves byte equality of the read-back on llama3.2:1b's 2048x2048 Q8_0 projection: twenty
attestant matvecs 48 ms, twenty twin matvecs 6 ms. `dense-token-handle.fk` dispatches the twin for
every Q8_0 row; its own driver answers the same ids as before ("Paris. The Eiffel Tower..."). A dense
token: 149 ms to 109 ms. The body's cooperative rmsnorm twin was tried the same way and parts from
its attestant by one bit in 737 of 2048 elements (the compiler's contraction), so it is compiled and
not dispatched; the one-thread attestant stays, ~18 ms of every token.

**The tongue lane's ends are cached.** `et-ends?` walked the gguf header for the end-of-sequence id
on every generated token (generation ran at 460 ms a token against prefill's 115); the id is read
once, and a line also ends at the line break the prompt asked for. A tongue: ~14 s to ~5.5 s a line.

Where a dense token's 109 ms now is: 97% GPU time across 276 dispatches — 112 matvecs at ~0.3 ms and
164 small ones (the KV-cache copies, the residual adds, rope, swiglu, the one-thread rmsnorm) at
~0.4 ms of launch latency each. The next room is fusion: k and v written straight into their caches
by a row offset, the add in the matvec's epilogue, as the whisper lane already does.

The surprise: the twin that parts from the attestant by one bit is arithmetically identical in its
text; the difference is the compiler's, not the fold's. Discomfort turned gold: the first band run
"proved" the twin on the layer's f32 norm vector, a type-0 row, and passed on equal garbage; the
band now finds the first Q8_0 view itself and prints the shape it tested.
