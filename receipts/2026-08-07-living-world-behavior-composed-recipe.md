## Honest boundary and next floor

This is native world-model growth, but it is not weight training, a change to
the daily `733` gain, general natural-language generation, or model promotion.
It learns one bounded composition geometry from offered evidence.  Its fixture
contains one recurrent composite example and one held-out use, so breadth and
generalization beyond that consented corpus remain open.

The current implementation is also not yet interaction-speed tissue.  Field
formation took roughly two to three minutes, consent plus held-out answering
roughly six to seven minutes, and selective freshness after revocation roughly
five to eight minutes in the native walker.  The monolithic observer's twenty
minutes showed that repeated whole-world re-derivation hides causal progress.

The next floor is therefore twofold: carry a generated composite Recipe through
a new, separately consented bounded action-nursery offer, and amortize exact
currentness through the active index so field/answer/revocation can be touched
interactively without weakening freshness.  From those execution traces the
body can then propose new relation and symbol cells for its native voice.  Any
such proposal must retain alternatives, unknowable remainder, absence,
revocation, and sender-declared timeout condensation, and must remain proposal
tissue until a later consent gate.
