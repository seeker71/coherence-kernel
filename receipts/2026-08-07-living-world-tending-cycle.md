## What moved

adds a bounded offer/ack tending cycle to the first axiom-born membrane:

- ordinary cells enter only through the existing consent and interface gate;

  attributions can derive an exact-recurrence proposal;
- the proposal's candidate, origin, witnesses, participants, coherence reading,
  resonance reading, and threshold are all inspectable native cells;
- recurrence proposes but grants no authority;
- each participant can acknowledge, dissent, explicitly abstain, remain absent,
  or later revoke and replace its own response;
- any active dissent dominates, while abstention or a missing response waits;
- integration requires an active affirmative response from every participant
  and a candidate whose original witnesses remain active;
- integration crosses the same offer membrane once per participant, leaving one
  independently revocable attribution for each source;
- earlier rounds and outcomes remain content-addressed and unchanged.

The response cell deliberately contains no trusted source field. Source identity
comes from its consented attribution edge. A shared response may therefore have
one active holder and one revoked holder without confusing their authority.

## What this evidence means

This is real movement in the organ's learning protocol: repeated consented
experience can now become a candidate, return to its participants, encounter
dissent, survive revision, and enter a later world only through unanimous active
authorization. It is not movement in the old fixed-split action predictor and it
does not turn the repeated 733 ppm benchmark gain into learning.

The learned unit is presently conservative: exact shared content plus its active
attribution field. Coherence and resonance are transparent recurrence counts,
not yet independently observed relational qualities. The cycle does not yet
discover resonance across differently shaped cells, generalize a relation,
persist a tended world across process boundaries, schedule its own enquiries,
compost unsupported candidates, or generate natural language in the native body.
