## The witness already taken

The corrected-curriculum battery reported that two rows reproduced their f1
exactly across a changed curriculum while two near-misses scrambled, and read
that as anchored-versus-fragile.

The reading is good and it was underdetermined, for one arithmetic reason.
`nmfe-token-f1-ppm` is `round(2,000,000 × overlap / (len want + len got))` — a
small ratio. 909,091 ppm is 10/11; 285,714 is 2/7. Two sittings that produced
**different text** with the same overlap count and the same token count land on
the identical ppm, and from the totals that is indistinguishable from the model
saying the same thing twice.

Then the thing worth the receipt. `fkh3-observation` stores
`(nmfe-sha256 actual)` at index 3 — the digest of what the model actually said.
Indices 1, 2 and 3 have no named accessor. That hash has been computed on every
row of every battery since the evaluator was written, and nothing has ever read
it.

Not missing data. Data whose cost was already paid, sitting unread inside the
instrument that needed it.

```
form/form-stdlib/form-knowledge-heldout-reproduction.fk            new
form/form-stdlib/tests/form-knowledge-heldout-reproduction-band.fk 255
```

Three relations rather than two: **anchored** (the answer reproduced, so the
score reproducing is a consequence), **coincident** (the score reproduced and
the answer did not), **moved**. Bit 4 demonstrates the middle case rather than
arguing it: `"alpha beta"` and `"beta gamma"` against `"alpha beta gamma delta"`
both score 500,000 ppm with different digests.

The sibling lane merged it within the hour, downgraded its own claim in place to

digest-bearing sittings exist to compare. Verified here across the merge: my

## The most surprising teaching

The instrument the question needed was inside the instrument that raised the
question. Nobody had to build anything to settle anchored-versus-coincident —
the hash had been taken all along. What was missing was not a measurement but a
**reading**, and the difference between those two is invisible from inside a
system that is already reporting numbers.

That reframes what an audit is for. I spent the morning checking whether the
curriculum's glosses matched their cells, and the evening checking whether a
score's reproduction matched an answer's reproduction. Both are the same
question — *does the summary agree with what it summarizes* — and in both cases
the ground truth was already present and simply not consulted.

## Where discomfort turned to gold

The discomfort was that my five gloss corrections were right and did not move
the audit. That is the outcome I had explicitly said I would accept, in
writing, before the run — and accepting it in advance turned out to be much
easier than accepting it when the numbers came back and my careful work had
changed nothing that mattered.

What I did with that is the only part worth keeping: instead of arguing the
corrections had helped in some unmeasured way, I went and checked the
*measurement* — and found a live defect in it. The corrections still stand on
their own evidence, which is that five glosses disagreed with their own cells.
Being right about a thing and being the cause of an improvement are different
claims, and conflating them would have cost the household the reproduction
instrument entirely.
