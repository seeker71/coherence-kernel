Date: 2026-08-09

## Movement

The Form-native body now has one live channel where consented session outcomes,
ordinary living-world offers, tending responses, questions, and revocations can
change one evolving content-addressed field.  The channel carries no task
verbs, action taxonomy, answer set, label vocabulary, imported answer, or
remote route.  Its offered action is itself a cell.

After each input, the channel re-observes transition and learned-symbol
recurrence.  Missing responses wait.  Explicit NOTHING remains silence.  ZERO
dissent dominates.  Integration occurs only after every current participant
has independently offered ONE through the same action interface.  Questions
are ordinary attributed cells; their answers retain current prediction,
possibilities, alternatives, unknowns, trace, learned symbols, voiced surfaces,
and explicitly unvoiced derivations.

## Observation lesson

The first semantic run returned `2147483639`, missing only bit `8`.  The witness
had mistaken the outer membrane's ONE (the silence evidence crossed) for the
sender's inner NOTHING (the content of that evidence).  The channel already
retained both cells.  The corrected observation reads the offered ack from the
exact input and the boundary ack from the receipt, preserving both questions:
"what was said?" and "did it cross?"

An attempted display accessor expanded source compilation beyond 128 seconds
before evaluation and was removed.  During that interval the supervisor named
`form-source-compile`; the child framebuffer was unavailable, not treated as a
health signal, and the run was interrupted.  Keeping the distinction in the
narrow observation layer restored the exact cached organ and millisecond live
execution.  Final source coordinates point to the exact apply and settlement

## Honest boundary

This is new evolving public-fixture state, not movement in the frozen
session-action benchmark and not a real private-session quality sample.  The
existing grounding reducer still calls an episode labeled only when an agent
completion cites a known candidate identifier; that is replay bookkeeping, not
consented feedback.  The new channel removes that constraint from the learning
organ.  Exact cross-process persistence and fresh-holder restore are now
observed in `2026-08-09-living-world-shared-field-continuity.md`; a live
registry-free cell-coordinate ingress still remains before a person can feed
and resume the channel directly without a bounded Form witness.

does not prove a general natural-language generator.  No C-seed growth,
