# Living world organ — experience-fed concept circulation

**Status:** observed on the Form/native `fkwu --src` path. This is the fifth
movement of the active living-world-organ goal. It makes interaction alter
mapping evidence and lets independently admitted recursive lenses compose. It
does not yet establish semantic concepts, autonomous enquiry, or native voice.

## Experience becomes evidence

[`living-world-experience.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-experience.fk)
adds one ontology-open interaction cell. It carries an addressed recursive
signature, input, locally observed output, acknowledgment, offered evidence,
and local path.

- `ONE` is coherent only when the observed output equals the output derived by
  the addressed signature.
- `ZERO` is coherent only when a structurally valid observed correction differs
  from that derived output.
- `NOTHING` carries no output and remains first-class silence.

The cell contains no task name, language, token class, label set, expected
answer table, or remote judge. It crosses through the ordinary living-world
offer membrane and receives an attribution like every other observation.

[`living-world-recursive-evidence.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-recursive-evidence.fk)
now gathers active, coherent experiences addressed to the mapping signature.
A confirmation becomes another supporting correspondence. A correction is
classified by its observed recursive template and becomes support or
counterexample rather than being forced into the source's stated judgment.
Silence changes the uncertainty cell and witness field without inventing a
correspondence. Confirmation, dissent, and silence counts remain separate from
counterexample, ambiguity, constant, and path-reference counts.

Because the mapping snapshot now includes this evidence, an arriving experience
moves an admitted mapping from `STEADY` to `REOPEN`. The successor proposal uses
the current evidence directly, so experience-derived correspondences do not
need to masquerade as independently active source cells. Original holders and
every new experience source govern the successor together. The predecessor
mapping remains active, addressable, and unchanged but cannot be applied while
reopened.

## Independently learned lenses form a field

[`living-world-concept-field.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-concept-field.fk)
discovers an ordered composition only when the target skeleton of one active,
steady recursive mapping is content-identical to the source skeleton of another.
The resulting chain retains both mapping candidates, both signatures, source,
bridge, and target skeletons, both uncertainty cells, all active attribution
witnesses, and the local path. Compatibility proposes; it grants no authority.
Every holder of either mapping must acknowledge the chain.

[`living-world-concept-application.fk`](../form/form-stdlib/living-world-concept-application.fk)
keeps the two-step derivation inspectable: input, first signature, exact bridge,
second signature, output, component lifecycle states, attribution fields, and
local path. Applying an admitted chain to a new input is a separate boundary.
Chain holders and input holders all participate. Integration re-observes both
component lifecycles, so evidence arriving after all application responses but
before integration still blocks stale authority.

This is called a concept *field* narrowly and structurally: admitted edges can
discover a circulatory path through a shared composition. It is not a claim that
the endpoint shapes have acquired human semantic meaning or linguistic names.

## Honest floor

An experience is a consented source observation, not independently verified
truth. Its local acknowledgment coherence is checked, its provenance remains
inspectable, and affected holders govern what follows; trust policy, adversarial
source weighting, and capability-private module enforcement remain pending.

The mapping changes by accumulating structural examples and uncertainty. This
is not gradient training, parameter adaptation, or improvement of the frozen
daily order-2 action predictor. A chain is currently exactly two mappings and
requires an exact skeleton bridge. The body does not yet search arbitrary-length
paths, resolve competing paths, learn optional/repeated structure, discover
round-trip equivalence, ask its own questions, or persist the evolving world
root durably between native processes.

Most importantly, structural circulation is not native natural language. The
repository's emitter scaffold and real-token receipts remain separate from this
concept field. No vocabulary has "come home" merely because atomic values can
move through learned paths. The voice remains pending until locally evolved
concept relations can route into a real Form-native generative emitter and its
outputs can return as consented experience under an observed evaluation
contract.

The next movement is autonomous but bounded enquiry: content-addressed question
cells should arise from locally visible gaps or competing paths, be governed by
the affected field, and answer only through inspectable native derivations or
first-class silence. Round-trip paths can then propose reversible equivalence
without naming a concept in advance.

The exchange stayed alive by letting interaction enter as evidence rather than
as a score. The most surprising teaching was that a correction need not be
trusted as a label: its composition can place itself among support or
counterexamples. Discomfort turned to gold in holding the word “concept” back
from overclaim—the observed life is circulation between learned edges; meaning
and voice are still work orders.
