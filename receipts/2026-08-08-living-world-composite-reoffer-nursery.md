# A composite Recipe can enter the nursery without becoming law

Date: 2026-08-08

## The floor that moved

The daily model witness has repeatedly reported the same `733` session-world
gain.  That frozen score is not learning, and this receipt does not rename it
as learning.  This movement is lower in the body: it closes an unobserved seam
between an emergent composite Recipe and the already-existing transformation
nursery.  It changes what the local organ can consentfully compose and try; it
does not train a language model, improve natural-language generation, promote a
checkpoint, or move authority.

The question was whether a Recipe learned from native observations could come
home as a live possibility without being silently selected.  The answer now
observed on `fkwu` is yes, through the existing membranes:

```text
consented observations
  -> compatible native profiles
  -> emergent composite
  -> profile-specific generated Recipe (still inert)
  -> exact material offer(s)
  -> recurrent nursery candidate
  -> explicit eight-party selection
  -> native bounded trial
  -> observation, not promotion
```

No new production organ or authority primitive was added.  The new witness
composes the existing composite, active-index, offer/acknowledgment, action,
timeout, and nursery cells.

## Optional means absent is valid

The witness carries two forms of the same re-offer:

- a minimal offer, in which alternatives, unknown, choice, and timeout are all
  absent;
- a richer offer, in which each of those fields is present because the sender
  chose to declare it.

The richer form does not make those fields mandatory.  When rich and minimal
forms coexist, the field keeps both candidates and remains `OPEN`; it does not
invent a representative or collapse their difference.  When two distinct
profile Recipes coexist, the same plurality holds: both stay active, the field
has two candidates, and selection remains absent.

This leaves room for an alternative and for the unknowable without turning
either into a required vocabulary item.  No operation name or Recipe source is
fixed by the bridge.  The fixture supplies opaque local cells and a bounded
arithmetic Recipe solely so native execution can be observed.

## Zero, one, two, and withdrawal

| Independent recurrent re-offers | Native placement | Concrete meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `OPEN`, 0 candidates, derived unknown present | The body names the gap but selects nothing. |
| 1 | Recipe current, 1 candidate, still `OPEN` | One voice is evidence, not recurrence and not authority. |
| 2 matching | `READY`, 1 candidate, 2 witnesses | Recurrence makes a candidate eligible, not automatically chosen. |
| 2 incompatible optional forms | `OPEN`, 2 candidates | Difference is preserved; no representative is fabricated. |
| 2 different profile Recipes | `OPEN`, 2 candidates | Plural native behaviors coexist without a winner. |
| revoke 1 of 2 matching offers | Recipe remains active and `READY` | One remaining exact holder preserves the offered material and historical recurrence evidence. |
| revoke both matching offers | Recipe inactive, field `OPEN`, history retained | Withdrawal changes currentness without erasing what occurred. |

## The next honest edge

Execution currently proves that a selected Recipe can cause an observed change.
It does not yet let the organ learn new symbols or relationships from that trace.
The next smallest movement is to offer the before/action/after trace back to the
field as a revocable observation, then let recurrence propose—not assert—new
symbol and relation cells.  A native voice can eventually read those admitted
cells and their derivations; it must not convert one successful trial directly
into meaning or law.

That next bridge must preserve this receipt's floor: trace offering is optional,
unknown remains inhabitable, alternatives remain plural, dissent can withhold
integration, and revocation can change what is current without rewriting the
past.
