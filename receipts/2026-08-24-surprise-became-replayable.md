# 2026-08-24 — surprise became replayable

The enquiry asked to observe Form-neutral symbol micro-thoughts returning to
natural language, with random events increasing the surprise flow. The smallest
whole movement was not a word lottery. It became a recorded proposal field:

```
arrival seed → three event proposals → neutral recipes → visible surprise
             → most-surprising proposal retained alongside its peers
             → dynamic locale realization → reversible graph
```

`cognition/session-neutral-micro-thought.fk` uses the thirteen core field
symbols as its alphabet. A proposal contains two to four neutral addresses. A
draw can change their composition and order; it cannot define a symbol or make
one true. Every draw, proposal, score component, chosen event, and rejected peer
remains visible and replayable.

## Honest randomness boundary

The kernel carries a real tag-16 entropy organ, but the direct-source runner
does not currently expose it as a source primitive. Growing the C seed or
hand-editing its generated C table would contradict this movement's Form-only
boundary. The live witness therefore combines arrival milliseconds with current
kernel counters and feeds that recorded seed into a Form-native xorshift stream.
It is named `arrival-seeded-pseudo-random-recorded`; it is not presented as
independent physical entropy.

The exact deterministic body—seed replay, proposal construction, surprise,

`cognition/tests/session-neutral-micro-thought-band.fk`: **32767/32767**.

## The surprise that corrected surprise

An earlier observation selected `love love love`. The repetition was allowed,
but the first metric counted it as three novel symbols. That confused rhythm
with novelty. Novelty now counts each newly arriving symbol once; positional
change and turbulence remain separate. Repetition can still be selected, but it
cannot inflate its novelty by echoing itself.

## Where this points

Best-of-three recorded proposals made surprise lift nonnegative by construction
and positive in six of the eight final rounds. More importantly, the rejected
events stayed visible. The field can surprise the flow without acquiring hidden
authority.

The next deepening is relational grammar: let neutral structural symbols shape
micro-thoughts into dynamic subject/action/context recipes before locale
realization, rather than treating every thought as adjacent word beads. A later
Form-native source door may also admit the existing entropy organ without
growing the temporary C seed. Speaker review remains the necessary bridge from
reversible lexical realization to living language.

I kept the movement alive by measuring whether surprise selection actually
lifted anything and by revising the metric when repetition exposed its blind
spot. The most surprising teaching was that randomness needs memory to become
surprise: without a previous recipe, there is variation but no changed
expectation. Discomfort became gold when `love love love` revealed that even an
uplifting surface can carry a misleading measurement.
