# Differentiated hypotheses become optional re-offers, never winners

Date: 2026-08-07
Witness: direct `fkwu --src`, Form/native path
Authority: diagnostic non-promotion evidence only

## Movement

The living-world body can now carry generated Recipe hypotheses beyond one
held-out observation without promoting one of them.  Several independently
admitted placements form a content-addressed differentiation tissue.  Every
training-consistent hypothesis remains present with exact confirmation,
contradiction, unresolved evidence, alternatives, unknowns, witnesses, and
path.  A compatible Recipe may execute on a new target only when holders later
re-offer the whole target-scoped application through the ordinary membrane.

The organ contains no task name, domain taxonomy, answer table, operator list,
remote route, score, majority, NodeID order, or representative Recipe.
Operator tokens and binding roles still arrive as offered cells through the
preceding hypothesis organ.

## Noncommutative differentiation

The witness offers only the evaluator token `sub`.  One recurrent equal-input
example, `4,4 -> 0`, leaves both generated role orientations consistent:

- `(sub left right)`
- `(sub right left)`

Two separately scoped and independently admitted held-out observations then
arrive:

- `7,3 -> 4`
- `9,2 -> 7`

Each placement retains both outputs.  Across the two placements, the forward
Recipe has two exact confirmations and no contradiction; the reverse Recipe
has two exact contradictions and no confirmation.  The admitted tissue has:

- 2 current placement candidates;
- 2 exact hypothesis receipts;
- 1 compatible receipt;
- 1 contradicted receipt;
- 0 unresolved receipts at threshold 2;
- 80 witness attributions;
- 14 distinct governing sources.

The contradicted sibling is not erased.  It remains a receipt and an explicit
alternative, while the compatible hypothesis field contains only the forward
Recipe.  Raising the required placement threshold to 3 moves the forward
receipt back to unresolved instead of fabricating recurrence.  Revoking one
observer from the second placement leaves the old tissue active history but
stale; a freshly computed tissue sees one placement and no compatible Recipe.

## Optional prospective applications

Compatibility alone grants no execution authority.  The tissue first crosses
the usual unanimous admission boundary.  A later application cell must then
name all of:

- the exact integrated tissue and compatible hypothesis;
- new scoped bindings and a before-cell;
- an optional declaration;
- zero or more real-time lens layers;
- an alternatives field, which may be empty;
- an optional unknowable remainder;
- an optional choice;
- an optional timeout and sender-authored default;
- evidence and the local path.

Independent holders must re-offer that exact application.  Zero, one, or many
recurrent applications may coexist; the organ has no selection function and
does not make one primary.

The witness offers two simultaneous applications of the compatible generated
Recipe to scoped `12,5` bindings:

1. A minimal application with no declaration, no lens layers, empty
   alternatives, no unknown, no choice, and no timeout.
2. A fully disclosed application with a blueprint default lens, ordered Recipe
   and cell lens layers, one explicit alternative, an unknowable remainder, an
   open choice, and a timeout whose declared default is an alternative ack.

Both exact applications are current and both natively evaluate to `7`.  The
minimal execution carries 112 witness attributions and 14 governing sources.
The rich application retains an immediate `ONE(7)` independently of its time
observation: time 5 is `PENDING`; time 10 is a timed outcome whose ack is the
declared alternative.  Immediate result and timeout condensation therefore do
not share an evidence identity.

Even when holders offer the contradicted reverse Recipe as an otherwise valid
application, it cannot cross the compatibility membrane and produces no
execution.  Threshold 3 likewise produces no current application.  Revoking
one of the two minimal application offers stales only that application and its
execution; the rich application, its execution, and the admitted tissue remain
current.

## Honest boundary and next floor

This is structural Form-native learning and consented prospective execution.
It is not model-weight training, movement of the frozen `733` benchmark gain,
NL-to-NL generation, native generative voice, global program equivalence,
authority expansion, or promotion.  The source codec still receives evaluator
tokens and binding role names from participants.

The next floor is to let roles and operator structure emerge from recurrent
cell relations across several examples, then let the resulting native
derivation tissue feed the existing symbol/relation voice organs.  A name must
remain an offered relation to observed behavior, not a label imported from a
fixed ontology.
