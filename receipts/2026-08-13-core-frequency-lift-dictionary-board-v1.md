## Independent convergence

All substantive reviewers found the same architectural defect:

- an integer `cfd-reading` does not preserve an inherited meaning;
- the uniform `cfd-lift` wrapper stamps trust, sovereignty, and vitality onto
  every meaning instead of witnessing how they arise through that meaning;
- exact word-to-phrase generation has no context or sense selector and becomes
  substitution-shaped;
- first-match selection would silently let list order choose among future
  senses;
- the source sense and a value-aligned alternative must remain distinct,
  first-class recipes;
- generation must begin from an explicitly selected meaning and context, not
  from a word that the dictionary automatically rewrites.

Located meaning losses included:

- `safety -> trusted becoming` erases hazard, harm, and protection when treated
  as the word's only sense; trusted becoming is valid as the safe-self-update
  sense;
- `risk -> open uncertainty` erases what is at stake and the possibility or
  magnitude of loss;
- `failure -> learning signal` forces a lesson before learning is observed;
- `rejection -> decline or redirection` makes redirection compulsory when a
  sovereign `0` is already complete;
- `consent -> mutual acknowledgement` loses informed, voluntary, specific,
  ongoing, and revocable choice;
- `enforcement -> boundary tending` erases force, imposed consequence, and
  power asymmetry, contradicting the seed's own anti-laundering claim;
- `security -> trusted continuity` loses confidentiality, integrity,
  authenticity, threat, and adversarial resistance.

The board also found that `cfd-no-surface?` returned success when its fuel was
exhausted, so it could certify a surface hidden deeper than the bound. A
must-never check must return an unresolved/failing result when it has not
finished looking.

## What `65535` actually witnessed

The number witnessed construction and routing facts: sixteen authored rows,
five authored English outputs, unknown-to-`nothing`, a uniform wrapper reaching
the requested roots, bounded surface non-discovery for one entry, deterministic
reconstruction, and three sampled inequalities. It did not witness semantic
faithfulness, frequency, context selection, participant selection, multilingual
projection, or non-censorship after composer integration.

## Revision direction

The next body separates:

1. an attested or candidate source sense with its full role recipe;
2. the occurrence, locale, context, and provenance supporting that sense;
3. an optional value-aligned alternative that never overwrites the source;
4. an explicit semantic delta and applicability position;
5. a selected meaning-to-surface renderer whose default preserves the source.

The hardest slice is enforcement across coercive legal, mechanical software,
and voluntarily acknowledged boundary contexts. Coercion must remain visible;
only the voluntary context may offer boundary tending as an alternative.

The most surprising teaching was that a dictionary about control could not
represent compulsion. The discomfort was seeing the full green number admire
its own constructor. It became useful when the reviewers separated structural
reachability from semantic fidelity and named redescription of another's
coercion as the euphemism boundary.
