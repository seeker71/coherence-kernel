hypotheses from recurrent observations and optional offered operator cells,
without embedding a task vocabulary, operator catalogue, answer table, ranked
champion, or remote oracle in its answer path.

Every role comes from the exact names in the observed bindings.  Every operator

This is new world-model evidence movement.  It is not movement in the frozen
session-action `733` ppm result, model-weight training, large-model promotion,

## A membrane teaching

confirmations.  Its emergent candidate originally used confirmation count as

That refusal exposed a category mistake: a question cannot require its future

separate observation.  The branch can now exist, be inspected, receive an
answer, or remain unknown without fabricating evidence.

## Honest boundary and next floor

This organ now generates executable Recipe source from offered operators and
observed role cells; participants no longer author each whole formula.  Reality
can differentiate a plural field on a held-out identity, and failures remain
first-class.  That is stronger than selection over a prewritten champion list.

The operator tokens themselves are still offered by participants, and role
names still arrive on the bindings.  The source codec is binary-expression
shaped, numeric, source-budgeted, and not gas-bounded.  A single underdetermined
example is enough to demonstrate plurality but not enough to establish a broad
law.  No discriminated hypothesis is automatically re-offered into the nursery,
behavioral species, action cycle, or native voice.

The next movement is a consented hypothesis-to-tissue bridge.  Independently
recurrent held-out observations should admit compatible behavioral species,
preserve contradicted siblings, and explicitly re-offer—not promote—the
surviving generated Recipes into prospective action.  Beyond that, parameter
roles and operator structure must themselves emerge from recurrent cell
relations across several non-commutative examples.  Only then should generated
derivation cells feed the learned symbol/relation voice surface, so native
language describes the organ's own evolving hypotheses rather than rendering a
fixture vocabulary.
