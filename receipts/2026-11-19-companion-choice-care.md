# Choice and privacy at the executing boundary

The Pluribus enquiry led to a runtime audit of disagreement, privacy, pausing
and leaving. Codex supplied diagnosis and edits; native Form supplied the
executions below. Synthetic fixtures carried no personal conversation and no
model training or generation was needed for these observations.

The offer/acknowledge and backtrack paths already distinguish refusal from
silence. `control/tests/backtrack-band.fk` returned 255 and the existing
offer/acknowledge band returned 32767. Refusal ends the choice; it does not
silently try another route. The dialogue covenant now also accepts a grounded
`disagree` as a complete encounter and refuses an invented disagreement.
Its existing band returns 1111111111111111. This checks declared move data;
it does not certify a model's interpretation of arbitrary conversation.

The original companion entry appended a synthetic turn even when generation
was skipped, and recorded `/leave` as conversation. The retained before probe
reported `default-retained=1, leave-treated-as-conversation=1`. The repaired
entry reported `default-retained=0, after-leave-retained=0`, an explicit left
state and observed release. Default context now consists of the process's own
last six exchanges. Per-turn options separately authorize saving, recalling
saved history, mouth export and the resident's disk transport. Unknown options
admit no work. Requested writes report their outcomes; conversation directories
are protected before content is written. Existing saved files are not erased.

Standalone `/pause` releases the owner and skips ordinary triples until
`/resume`; `/leave` consumes no later input; `/forget` clears session context
and releases its model context. These controls are read between turns. Active
generation cancellation remains the process organ's operation. Its independent
probe observed cancellation before spawn, and cancellation of an executing
parent and grandchild with cleanup `released`. CLI `quit` and `exit` each left
a following `ping` unevaluated. Pausing session learning preserves examples and
lets a current round finish; it is distinct from scope withdrawal.
