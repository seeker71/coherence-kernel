## Surprise, and where the discomfort went

A label can go stale without a line of its band changing. form-agent-world-model's head was true
when it was witnessed on 2026-09-03; the thirteen-name commit, in other cells, made it false. Only
asking the three siblings again showed it; the band itself had nothing to say.

The discomfort was a pass that kept answering a question it was not asked. It was looking at paths
and kept stopping at names, band after band. Meeting each stop where it stood would have found the
twenty-four one at a time; asking the whole stdlib once turned them into one list, and thirteen of
them landed tonight.
