## The teaching under the number

The near-total orphanhood is not neglect — it is MANUFACTURED by our own
rule. The frontier-word discipline demands every row's fresh word be 0-hit
before it lands, so at mint time every teaching is definitionally unwoven,
and later questions (asked by rented minds each seeking their own fresh
word) structurally avoid re-speaking old ones. The rule that makes each row
a frontier is the same rule that keeps the corpus a shelf. The organ makes
that cost visible, and the loop it opens is the repair: the body now asks
questions built FROM its own words, so answering the body weaves by
construction. Each accepted row changes the weave, which changes the next
question the body poses — a trajectory steered by accumulated state that
neither mind plans.

essay: the question was put to the body and the body's answer led.
